## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a "green initiatives" title card: a full-bleed night-time city photo darkened by a translucent black overlay, eight icon-in-circle badges joined by a web of thin connector lines, and a green title bar reading "OUR GREEN INITIATIVES". LibreOffice draws the full scene — photo, connector lines, spaced-out title text. BetterOffice draws only the icon circles and the title bar on a flat black background: the background photo and every connector line are missing, and the title text loses its letter-spacing.

## Findings

### 1. Background photograph not rendered

- Where: "Picture 4" (id 5), full slide, region [0.0, 0.0, 1.0, 1.0]
- Reference: the cropped night-time city/highway JPEG fills the entire slide behind everything else.
- Candidate: solid black where the photo should be.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:pic><p:nvPicPr><p:cNvPr id="5" name="Picture 4" .../></p:nvPicPr>
  <p:blipFill rotWithShape="1"><a:blip r:embed="rId3"/>
    <a:srcRect t="8639" b="7091"/><a:stretch/></p:blipFill>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="20" y="10"/><a:ext cx="12191980" cy="6857990"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="rect"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom></p:spPr></p:pic>
  ```
  `rId3` resolves to `ppt/media/image1.jpeg` in `slide.rels.json`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the picture element is present, sized to the full slide, and its target JPEG is a plain embedded image (not a chart/diagram/OLE object BetterOffice might legitimately skip); nothing in the XML explains omitting it. This single miss also depresses everything drawn on top of it: the darkening overlay ("Rectangle 109", `schemeClr tx1` at 33% alpha) has nothing to blend with and paints flat black, and the title bar's `alpha="90000"` green fill loses the faint photo showing through the missing 10%, so it reads as a flatter, more saturated green than the reference. Those two are downstream of this one root cause, not separate defects.

### 2. All 16 connector lines missing

- Where: "Group 107" (id 108), region [0.088, 0.062, 0.957, 0.842] — contains 16 `p:cxnSp` children (ids 58, 59, 62, 65, 68, 71, 74, 77, 80, 83, 88, 91, 94, 97, 100, 104)
- Reference: a web of diagonal white-ish lines connects every icon circle to its neighbors, evoking a network.
- Candidate: no lines anywhere; the circles float unconnected on black.
- XML (representative, Straight Connector 57 / id 58):
  ```xml
  <p:cxnSp><p:nvCxnSpPr><p:cNvPr id="58" name="Straight Connector 57"/>
    <p:cNvCxnSpPr><a:stCxn id="8" idx="5"/><a:endCxn id="21" idx="1"/></p:cNvCxnSpPr></p:nvCxnSpPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="4584303" y="1720689"/><a:ext cx="1987489" cy="3248709"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="line"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom>
    <a:ln w="28575"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"><a:lumMod val="75000"/><a:alpha val="70000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill>
      <a:prstDash val="solid"/></a:ln></p:spPr></p:cxnSp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each connector is a normal `prstGeom prst="line"` shape with an explicit `a:ln` (2.25pt, 70%-alpha `bg1` at 75% luminance-mod), not an unusual or unsupported connector type. All 16 in the group are affected identically, indicating the whole `cxnSp` shape kind is being skipped rather than one line having a bad property.

### 3. Title text missing its character spacing

- Where: "Rectangle 28" (id 29), region [0.0, 0.45, 1.0, 0.55]
- Reference: "OUR GREEN INITIATIVES" is set with wide letter tracking, spanning most of the green bar's width.
- Candidate: the same string renders with normal/tight spacing, noticeably narrower and positioned differently (still centered within the bar, but the string's own width and per-letter gaps differ).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-IN" sz="3200" spc="600" dirty="0">
    <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill>
    <a:latin typeface="Open Sans Extrabold" .../>
  </a:rPr><a:t>OUR GREEN INITIATIVES</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `spc="600"` requests +6pt of extra tracking between characters at this 32pt size; the candidate's render matches the untracked (`spc` absent) case, so the attribute is not being applied.

## Not reported

- The icon circles read as brighter/more opaque in the candidate than the reference — this is the visual consequence of finding 1 (no background photo to show through their semi-transparent gray fill), not an independent circle-fill bug.
- The green title bar is a flatter, more saturated green in the candidate than the reference's faintly textured one — same root cause as finding 1 (the bar's fill is only 90% opaque).
- The reference's title glyphs look visibly lighter than the "Open Sans Extrabold" weight the XML specifies, while the candidate's glyphs look closer to an extrabold weight — plausibly a font-substitution artifact on the LibreOffice side rather than a candidate defect; not confident enough either way to file.
- Ordinary anti-aliasing/edge softness differences on circle and icon boundaries.
