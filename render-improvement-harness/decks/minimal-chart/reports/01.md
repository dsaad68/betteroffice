## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a single-metric dashboard card: a line chart (visitors per weekday) drawn over a teal patterned background, with three summary text pairs (TOTAL VISITORS/8000, RETURNING VISITORS/5000, BOUNCE RATE/40%) below it. LibreOffice renders the full chart — teal `smGrid` pattern fill, the white 6-point line with square markers, and both axis label sets — with the bottom info card matching pixel-for-pixel. BetterOffice renders the bottom info card correctly but the chart itself is almost entirely blank: only a horizontal line at the category-axis position and a broken vertical stroke near the left edge are visible; the series, all axis text, and the background pattern fill are missing.
