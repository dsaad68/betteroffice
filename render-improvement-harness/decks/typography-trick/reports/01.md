## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a title card: the words "CREATIVE VENUS" are cut from a photo (`image1.jpeg`) via a single `custGeom` freeform shape whose outline traces the letterforms, giving each glyph a different slice of the picture. The reference (LibreOffice) draws the full colorful lettering with a drop shadow and reflection, over a soft radial background glow (white center, blue-grey edge). The candidate (BetterOffice) shows only the flat blue-grey background — the entire text shape is absent, and the background gradient is rendered as a single flat color instead of a radial gradient.

## Findings

### 1. Freeform picture-filled text shape not drawn

- Where: "Freeform: Shape 5" (id 6), region `[0.068, 0.410, 0.925, 0.491]` — the horizontal band across the middle of the slide (all of row 2 in the hot-cell grid, which is exactly where this shape's bounding box sits).
- Reference: draws the full "CREATIVE VENUS" wordmark, each letter filled with a differently-positioned crop of `image1.jpeg`, plus an outer shadow and a reflection beneath.
- Candidate: nothing is drawn in this region; only the plain background shows through.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp>
    <p:spPr>
      <a:xfrm><a:off x="829322" y="2812610"/><a:ext cx="10450353" cy="552355"/></a:xfrm>
      <a:custGeom>
        <a:pathLst><a:path w="10450353" h="552355">
          <a:moveTo><a:pt x="1012222" y="136303"/></a:moveTo>
          <a:lnTo>...</a:lnTo>
          <a:cubicBezTo>...</a:cubicBezTo>
          <!-- long multi-letter outline continues -->
        </a:path></a:pathLst>
      </a:custGeom>
      <a:blipFill dpi="0" rotWithShape="1">
        <a:blip r:embed="rId2"/>
        <a:srcRect/>
        <a:stretch><a:fillRect l="-53000"/></a:stretch>
      </a:blipFill>
      <a:effectLst>
        <a:outerShdw blurRad="127000" dist="38100" dir="2700000" algn="tl" rotWithShape="0">...</a:outerShdw>
        <a:reflection blurRad="6350" stA="52000" endA="300" endPos="35000" dir="5400000" sy="-100000" algn="bl" rotWithShape="0"/>
      </a:effectLst>
    </p:spPr>
  </p:sp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape is present in the XML with a valid, non-degenerate `custGeom` path and a `blipFill` referencing `ppt/media/image1.jpeg` (present in the package). `bo-log.json` records no error and a normal render time for this slide, so the shape is being silently dropped rather than failing loudly — most likely the combination of a multi-contour `custGeom` (one contour per letter, several using `cubicBezTo`) with a picture fill is not being rasterized at all.

### 2. Radial background gradient rendered as a flat fill

- Where: slide background, full-bleed `[0, 0, 1, 1]`.
- Reference: a circular gradient from `bg1` (white) at the center out to `tx2` with `lumMod 20% / lumOff 80%` (a light blue-grey, since `tx2` maps to `dk2` = `44546A`) at the edges, per `p:bg/p:bgPr/a:gradFill` with `a:path path="circle"` centered via `fillToRect l/t/r/b="50000"`.
- Candidate: a single flat blue-grey fill across the whole slide, with no visible radial falloff — the color matches roughly the gradient's outer stop, not its center.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:bg><p:bgPr>
    <a:gradFill>
      <a:gsLst>
        <a:gs pos="100000"><a:schemeClr val="tx2"><a:lumMod val="20000"/><a:lumOff val="80000"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
        <a:gs pos="0"><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:gs>
      </a:gsLst>
      <a:path path="circle"><a:fillToRect l="50000" t="50000" r="50000" b="50000"/></a:path>
    </a:gradFill>
  </p:bgPr></p:bg>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the slide background explicitly specifies a path-based (circular) gradient with two scheme-color stops; the candidate appears to fall back to a solid fill using approximately the last gradient stop's color, dropping the radial shape entirely. Marked low severity since the slide reads fine either way, but it is a clear, XML-supported mismatch, not an anti-aliasing quirk.

## Not reported
