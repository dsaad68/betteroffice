## Reference vs candidate

This slide is a single-shape title card: the words "CREATIVE VENUS" are outlined as one freeform (`custGeom`) shape, filled with a colorful JPEG texture (`rId2` -> `ppt/media/image1.jpeg`) that gives each letter its own colored/noisy band, plus a soft drop shadow and a bottom reflection. It sits on a dark radial-gradient background. In the LibreOffice reference both are visible: the rainbow-textured wordmark centered in the frame, and a background that is lighter in the middle and darker at the corners. The BetterOffice candidate shows neither: the frame is a single flat dark gray with no text and no gradient.

## Findings

### 1. "CREATIVE VENUS" freeform wordmark is not rendered

- Where: "Freeform: Shape 5" (id 6), region `[0.068, 0.41, 0.925, 0.491]` (the full-width text band in the vertical middle of the slide).
- Reference: the custom-geometry outline of the wordmark is filled with the picture at `rId2`, producing pink/blue/purple/teal/green/olive bands across the letters, with a soft outer shadow and a faint bottom reflection.
- Candidate: nothing is drawn in this region; sampling shows a uniform `(38, 38, 38)` — the plain background color — across the entire bounding box. The shape has no visible pixels at all.
- XML:
```xml
<p:spPr>
  <a:xfrm><a:off x="829322" y="2812610"/><a:ext cx="10450353" cy="552355"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:custGeom>...</a:custGeom>
  <a:blipFill dpi="0" rotWithShape="1">
    <a:blip r:embed="rId2"/>
    <a:srcRect/>
    <a:stretch><a:fillRect l="-53000"/></a:stretch>
  </a:blipFill>
  <a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln>
  <a:effectLst>
    <a:outerShdw blurRad="127000" dist="38100" dir="2700000" algn="tl" rotWithShape="0">...</a:outerShdw>
    <a:reflection blurRad="6350" stA="52000" endA="300" endPos="35000" dir="5400000" sy="-100000" algn="bl" rotWithShape="0"/>
  </a:effectLst>
</p:spPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape has no `hidden` attribute and carries a fully specified `custGeom` path plus a `blipFill`; there is nothing in the XML that would make PowerPoint or LibreOffice skip drawing it. The most likely root cause is that a picture fill (`blipFill`) applied to an arbitrary custom-geometry path (rather than a rectangle or preset shape) is not being rasterized/clipped by BetterOffice, so the shape is skipped entirely instead of falling back to a solid or missing-image placeholder.

## Not reported

- Anti-aliasing / sub-pixel edge softness on the reference wordmark's letterforms — not evaluable since the candidate draws nothing there.
- Minor JPEG compression noise inside the reference texture fill.

### 2. Slide background gradient is flattened

- Where: `p:bg` (whole slide), region `[0, 0, 1, 1]`.
- Reference: a radial ("circle" path) gradient centered at 50%/50%, lighter near the center (`tx1` at `lumMod 75000`/`lumOff 25000`) and darker at the edges (`tx1` at `lumMod 85000`/`lumOff 15000`). Sampled pixels: center `(64, 64, 64)`, corners `(38, 42, 42)` range.
- Candidate: flat `(38, 38, 38)` everywhere — center, corners, and edges all sampled identical.
- XML:
```xml
<p:bg><p:bgPr>
  <a:gradFill>
    <a:gsLst>
      <a:gs pos="100000"><a:schemeClr val="tx1"><a:lumMod val="85000"/><a:lumOff val="15000"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
      <a:gs pos="0"><a:schemeClr val="tx1"><a:lumMod val="75000"/><a:lumOff val="25000"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
    </a:gsLst>
    <a:path path="circle"><a:fillToRect l="50000" t="50000" r="50000" b="50000"/></a:path>
  </a:gradFill>
  <a:effectLst/>
</p:bgPr></p:bg>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the XML specifies a two-stop radial gradient; BetterOffice's flat output matches neither gradient stop exactly (`38` is darker than the `100000`-pos stop would resolve to), suggesting the radial/circle gradient path is not implemented and some other fallback color is used instead.
