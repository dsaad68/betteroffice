## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a title card: the words "CREATIVE VENUS" are cut from a texture image (a photograph, `image1.jpeg`) via a `custGeom` freeform shape whose outline traces the letterforms, filled with `a:blipFill`, plus an outer shadow and a reflection, sitting over a radial gradient background that glows blue-ish in the center and darkens toward the edges. The reference (LibreOffice) draws both the textured lettering with its shadow/reflection and the radial background glow. The candidate (BetterOffice) draws only a flat, uniform dark background — the lettering shape and its effects are entirely absent, and the background gradient collapses to a single flat color.

## Findings

### 1. "CREATIVE VENUS" freeform text-shape not rendered at all

- Where: "Freeform: Shape 5" (id 6), region `[0.068, 0.41, 0.925, 0.491]` (roughly the horizontal band across the middle of the slide)
- Reference: draws the custom-geometry letterforms for "CREATIVE VENUS" filled with the picture `image1.jpeg` (a stretched/offset texture, `fillRect l="-53000"`), plus a soft outer shadow (`blurRad="127000"`) and a reflection beneath the text.
- Candidate: draws nothing in this region — the shape, its picture fill, its shadow, and its reflection are all completely missing; the pixels there are pure background.
- XML:

- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape is present in the XML with a valid, well-formed `custGeom` path and a `blipFill` referencing `image1.jpeg` (`bo-log.json` reports `"skipped_images": 0` for this slide, so the image loaded fine). Nothing in the XML marks the shape hidden, zero-sized, or off-slide. There is no visible counterpart in the candidate at all, which is a complete miss rather than a partial/incorrect rendering.

### 2. Slide background gradient rendered as a flat solid color

- Where: slide background, full slide `[0.0, 0.0, 1.0, 1.0]`
- Reference: a radial (`path="circle"`) gradient centered at `fillToRect l="50000" t="50000" r="50000" b="50000"` (slide center), going from `accent1` (`#4472C4`) at `lumMod 50%` at the center (`pos="0"`) to `tx2`→`dk2` (`#44546A`) at `lumMod 50%` at the edge (`pos="100000"`); sampled pixel at slide center is `rgb(32,56,100)`, versus `rgb(34,42,54)` at the corner.
- Candidate: a uniform flat fill across the whole slide; sampled pixels at both center and corner are `rgb(34,42,53)` — matching only the outer (`pos="100000"`, `tx2`/`dk2` at 50% luminance) gradient stop.
- XML:
```xml
<p:bg><p:bgPr>
  <a:gradFill>
    <a:gsLst>
      <a:gs pos="100000"><a:schemeClr val="tx2"><a:lumMod val="50000"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
      <a:gs pos="0"><a:schemeClr val="accent1"><a:lumMod val="50000"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
    </a:gsLst>
    <a:path path="circle"><a:fillToRect l="50000" t="50000" r="50000" b="50000"/></a:path>
  </a:gradFill>
  <a:effectLst/>
</p:bgPr></p:bg>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the `a:gradFill` explicitly defines two color stops and a `circle` gradient path; resolving `tx2`→`dk2` (`#44546A`) confirms the candidate's flat color is exactly the edge stop, so the radial interpolation and/or the `path="circle"` gradient type is not implemented and the fill falls back to a single (last) stop instead of blending.
