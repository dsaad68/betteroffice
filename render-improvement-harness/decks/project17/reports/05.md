## Reference vs candidate

A five-row comparison slide: a dark-purple title band, five purple label boxes down the left column, and five matching bulleted-text boxes to their right, closing with a gold "TOP CONFIDENTIAL" footer. LibreOffice renders a clean two-line title, five colored labels, and five bullet lists with gold-highlighted phrases scattered through otherwise-black text. BetterOffice renders the same layout but with a stray "Unit of measure" label bleeding through the title, a wider title font that overflows the header, no bullet glyphs anywhere, gold text-highlighting lost or misaligned in the bullet lists, and the bottom-left label box's text mirrored into unreadable characters.

## Findings

### 1. Hidden shape "Unit of measure" is drawn over the title

- Where: shape id 8, region ≈ [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125] (a full-width strip just under the title text)
- Reference: nothing is drawn in this strip; only the title's second line shows through.
- Candidate: a bold gold "Unit of measure" label is drawn directly under/through the title's second line.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `hidden="1"` on the shape's `cNvPr` means PowerPoint (and LibreOffice) never draws it. BetterOffice draws it as if visible.

### 2. Title text renders wider than the reference, overflowing the header

- Where: "Title 1" (id 2), placeholder region ≈ [0.028, 0.025, 0.855, 0.079] per the layout's `<a:xfrm>`; text overflows below this box in both renders since `<a:noAutofit/>` is set.
- Reference: "Being part of a consultancy network would give us an access to support you in" fits on line 1, with "different relevant practices to SANS project" on line 2, entirely inside the dark header band.
- Candidate: line 1 stops at "...to support" (roughly the same pixel width fits ~7 fewer words), pushing "different relevant practices to SANS project" onto lines 2-3, which extend past the header band into the white slide body (and collide with finding 1's stray label).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="2" name="Title 1"/>...<p:nvPr><p:ph type="title"/></p:nvPr></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr/>
  <p:txBody><a:bodyPr lIns="91440" rIns="91440"/><a:lstStyle/>
    <a:p><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" dirty="0"/>
      <a:t>Being part of a consultancy network would give us an access to support you in different relevant practices to SANS project</a:t>
    </a:r></a:p>
  </p:txBody></p:sp>
  ```
  (no run-level `sz`, so size comes from the master's `titleStyle` `lvl1pPr` `defRPr sz="2000"`, latin `+mj-lt` → theme majorFont "Calibri")
- Why the candidate is wrong: the placeholder box width (`cx="8208912"` from the layout) is unchanged between renders, and neither the slide nor the run overrides the inherited 20pt size, so both engines should wrap at the same point. The candidate's earlier wrap indicates it is measuring the inherited title font (by size, fallback substitution, or both) wider than the reference does.

### 4. Gold run-level highlight color dropped or misaligned

- Where: TextBox 27 (id 28) worst-affected, region ≈ [0.277, 0.134, 0.972, 0.277]; also visible in TextBox 54 (id 55), TextBox 55 (id 56), TextBox 62 (id 63)
- Reference: specific phrases ("strong regional presence", "supported KSA in its growth and have developed leading insights", "through their 670+ experienced Implementation consultants", etc.) are drawn in gold (`#A99A72`) while the surrounding text stays black, exactly matching each run's own `solidFill`.
- Candidate: in TextBox 27, all three bullets render fully black — pixel-sampling the box's rendered region turns up no `#A99A72`-family pixels at all. In TextBox 54/55/62 the gold color is present, but the black/gold boundary falls roughly one run early: e.g. "through their 670+ experienced Implementation" (which XML marks gold) renders black, while "consultants who have "been there, done that" across a range of topics including" (which XML marks mostly black) renders gold.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-GB" sz="1000" dirty="0"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="000000"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr>
    <a:t>a consultancy is the leading consulting firm with a </a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-GB" sz="1000" dirty="0"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="A99A72"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr>
    <a:t>strong regional presence</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each run carries its own explicit `solidFill`; the color a run's glyphs are painted with should match that run's own fill, not a neighboring run's.

### 5. "Partnership with Elixir" box text is mirrored (lo-suspect)

- Where: "Rectangle 2" (id 62), region ≈ [0.027, 0.796, 0.264, 0.939]
- Reference: "Partnership with Elixir, to deliver practical and locally-Compatible Solutions" is drawn left-to-right, legible, left-aligned in the purple box.
- Candidate: the same text is mirrored horizontally — every glyph is flipped, appearing as backwards, right-aligned text (confirmed at 3x crop: "Partnership with Elixir, to" reads as its mirror image).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm flipH="1"><a:off x="269874" y="5459515"/><a:ext cx="2342879" cy="978408"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="rect"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="604878"/></a:solidFill>
  </p:spPr>
  ```
- Why this is filed as `lo-suspect`: this is the only shape on the slide with `flipH="1"`, and it is a `txBox="1"` autoshape whose text lives in the same `p:sp` as the flipped geometry — real PowerPoint is known to mirror a shape's text along with its fill/outline when `flipH`/`flipV` is set directly on the shape (a frequent source of user complaints, usually worked around by flipping a group instead). BetterOffice's mirrored render is plausibly the spec-faithful one; LibreOffice's unflipped, readable text is the more likely deviation. Confidence is only medium because this LibreOffice accommodation is also common and deliberate in some engines.
