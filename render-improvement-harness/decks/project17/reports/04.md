## Reference vs candidate

A title slide introducing the a consultancy/Elixir partnership: a header, a two-circle Venn diagram (gray "a consultancy & Co." circle, light-gray "Elixir" circle, purple overlap with a white handshake icon) with three leader lines running down to three bulleted text columns, and a purple summary bar at the bottom. LibreOffice renders all of this at the sizes and positions the XML specifies, with everything fitting inside its box. BetterOffice renders the title and all three body text columns at a visibly larger font that overflows past every box (the third column's text is cut off by the summary bar, and the hidden "Unit of measure" label bleeds through under the title), replaces the entire Venn diagram with two flat rectangles and a blank white square, drops the three leader lines to the columns, and stretches the uncropped master logo image so an extra tagline shows below "ELIXIR".

## Findings

### 1. Title and all three body text boxes render far larger than specified, overflowing their boxes

- Where: "Title 1" (id 2), region ≈ [0.028, 0.025, 0.856, 0.079]; "TextBox 35/39/41" (ids 36, 40, 42), region ≈ [0.055, 0.499, 0.93, 0.844] combined.
- Reference: the title wraps to two lines that fit inside the ~20pt line-height title band; each of the three body boxes' bulleted text fits comfortably inside its bordered box with room to spare at the bottom.
- Candidate: the title's first line alone reaches to the same width the reference used for its whole first line, then a much taller second line collides with the "Unit of measure" label underneath; each body box's text overflows past the box's bottom border, and the third column's overflow text is clipped by the "Unparalled strategic oversight..." bar itself overlapping the box below it.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- TextBox 35 (also 39, 41): no sz on any <a:rPr>; only source of size is the shape's own lstStyle -->
  <a:lstStyle>
    <a:lvl2pPr marL="193675" lvl="1" indent="-192088" ...>
      <a:defRPr sz="1400" baseline="0"><a:latin typeface="+mn-lt"/></a:defRPr>
    </a:lvl2pPr>
  </a:lstStyle>
  <a:p><a:pPr marL="176213" lvl="1" .../><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" dirty="0">
    <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="A49782"/></a:solidFill><a:latin typeface="+mj-lt"/>
  </a:rPr><a:t>#1 management consulting firm </a:t></a:r>...

  <!-- Title 1: no sz anywhere on the slide or layout shape; falls through to master titleStyle -->
  <p:titleStyle><a:lvl1pPr ...><a:defRPr lang="en-US" sz="2000" b="0" .../></a:lvl1pPr></p:titleStyle>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: none of these runs set `sz` directly, so per the OOXML inheritance chain the effective size must come from the shape's own `a:lstStyle` level default (1400 = 14pt, for the paragraph's `lvl="1"`) for the three body boxes, or from the master's `p:titleStyle/a:lvl1pPr/a:defRPr` (2000 = 20pt) for the title. All four bodies also declare `<a:noAutofit/>`, so nothing should be scaling the text up either. BetterOffice is evidently not resolving one of these fallback levels and using a larger default instead, which also explains why colored runs (e.g. the second, `schemeClr tx2` run in TextBox 35's first bullet) visually land one or two words later than in the reference: the line-break and run-color boundaries appear to have been computed at the correct, smaller font metrics while the glyphs are painted at the larger, wrong size.

### 2. Hidden "Unit of measure" label is drawn anyway

- Where: "3. Unit of measure" (id 8), region ≈ [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125], directly under the title.
- Reference: nothing is drawn in this region; the title's two lines sit alone against the dark purple header.
- Candidate: "Unit of measure" is drawn at 24pt in gold, overlapping the title's second line.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>
  ...
  <a:bodyPr wrap="square" lIns="0" tIns="0" rIns="0" bIns="0"><a:spAutoFit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `hidden="1"` on `p:cNvPr` means the shape must not be rendered (it stays in the file only for later re-use). LibreOffice honors this; BetterOffice does not.

### 3. The entire Venn diagram is replaced by its shapes' bounding boxes

- Where: Group 46 (id 47) — Freeform 57 (id 48, purple overlap), Freeform 58 (id 49, "a consultancy" circle), Freeform 59 (id 50, "Elixir" circle), Freeform 16 (id 51, handshake icon) — plus Freeform 52 (id 44) and Freeform 53 (id 54), the two L-shaped leader brackets outside the group. Region ≈ [0.202, 0.14, 0.794, 0.468].
- Reference: two overlapping gray/light-gray circles (traced as ~150-segment polygons approximating an ellipse), a dark-purple lens where they overlap, a white handshake icon (cubic-bezier path) centered in the lens, and two small L-shaped, dot-terminated leader brackets connecting the circle to the two side leader lines.
- Candidate: two flat rectangles (dark gray on the left, light gray on the right) exactly matching each circle's `a:xfrm` bounding box; the purple lens is invisible (its own bounding-box rectangle is painted before the two circle rectangles and is fully covered by them); the handshake icon's bounding box paints a plain white square on top since it is painted last; each leader bracket (`noFill`, open 3-point polyline with an oval line-end) is drawn as a bordered rectangle instead of the "L" it should trace, and its `tailEnd type="oval"` dot is gone.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- Freeform 58, a consultancy circle -->
  <a:custGeom><a:pathLst><a:path w="1833958" h="2374363">
    <a:moveTo><a:pt x="1169577" y="0"/></a:moveTo>
    <a:lnTo><a:pt x="1230492" y="2061"/></a:lnTo>... (150 lnTo points, closed)
  </a:path></a:pathLst></a:custGeom>
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="C5C5C5"><a:alpha val="60000"/></a:srgbClr></a:solidFill>

  <!-- Freeform 52, leader bracket: open 3-point path, no fill, dot-terminated line -->
  <a:pathLst><a:path w="1190625" h="1304925">
    <a:moveTo><a:pt x="0" y="1304925"/></a:moveTo>
    <a:lnTo><a:pt x="0" y="0"/></a:lnTo>
    <a:lnTo><a:pt x="1190625" y="0"/></a:lnTo>
  </a:path></a:pathLst>
  <a:noFill/><a:ln w="6350"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"><a:lumMod val="75000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill><a:tailEnd type="oval"/></a:ln>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: every one of these shapes has a well-formed `a:custGeom`/`a:pathLst` with literal, absolute coordinates (no unresolved `gdLst` formula references in the path itself) — there is nothing here a compliant renderer should need to fall back on. BetterOffice is drawing each shape's `a:xfrm` bounding box instead of walking its path, for both closed multi-point polygons and open, unclosed polylines.

### 4. The three leader lines from the Venn diagram to the text columns are missing

- Where: "Straight Connector 37/40/42" (ids 38, 41, 43), region ≈ [0.052, 0.468, 0.936, 0.468] (a thin horizontal band at 46.8% height, spanning the header of all three columns).
- Reference: three plain gray horizontal `line`-preset connectors run from beneath the Venn diagram over to a point above each text column, joining the L-shaped brackets from finding 3.
- Candidate: none of the three connectors are drawn; the columns' headers have no visible line at all above them.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cxnSp><p:nvCxnSpPr><p:cNvPr id="38" name="Straight Connector 37"/></p:nvCxnSpPr>
    <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="518160" y="3207217"/><a:ext cx="3311877" cy="0"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="line"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom></p:spPr>
  </p:cxnSp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: this is a plain `p:cxnSp` with `prstGeom prst="line"` and a non-zero width/zero height extent — an ordinary straight line, unrelated to the custGeom issue in finding 3. It has no `hidden` attribute and no `noFill`/`noLine` override, so it should always be visible.

### 5. Master Elixir logo picture is rendered without its crop, exposing extra source content

- Where: "Picture 11" (id 12, defined on the slide master, not the slide itself), region ≈ [0.907, 0.011, 0.992, 0.097], top-right corner.
- Reference: the icon glyph, Arabic wordmark, and "ELIXIR" caption, cropped tightly with nothing below the caption.
- Candidate: the same icon and captions but visibly smaller within the frame and shifted up, with an extra line of small tagline text appearing below "ELIXIR" that the reference never shows.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:blipFill rotWithShape="1"><a:blip r:embed="rId24" cstate="print">...</a:blip>
    <a:srcRect l="15358" t="9779" r="21228" b="24405"/>
    <a:stretch/>
  </p:blipFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `a:srcRect` crops 15.4%/9.8%/21.2%/24.4% off the left/top/right/bottom of the source bitmap before it is stretched into the shape; the source PNG (`ppt/media/image2.png`, confirmed by extracting `source.pptx`) does contain an extra caption line in that bottom 24.4% strip. BetterOffice appears to stretch the full, uncropped source into the frame instead of applying `srcRect` first.
