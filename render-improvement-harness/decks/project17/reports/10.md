## Reference vs candidate

Slide 10 is a title-plus-bullets slide: a dark purple title band with a bold gold-underlined "Transformation" callout and a corner logo, then two rows pairing a circular photo with descriptive text, the second row's text being a bulleted list. LibreOffice renders the title cleanly across three lines inside the band, keeps the two photos circular, draws "▪" bullets with a hanging indent on every second-level list item, and leaves both `hidden="1"` shapes invisible. BetterOffice draws the title in an oversized face that only fits two garbled lines before being painted over by the wrongly-visible "Unit of measure" caption and the photo/body group below, drops bold/underline/color from the "Transformation" run, renders both photos as plain rectangles, drops every bullet glyph and hanging indent in the list, and shows extra uncropped content in the corner logo.

## Findings

### 1. Shapes marked `hidden="1"` are drawn anyway

- Where: "Rectangle 3" (id 4, region [0.0, 0.0, 0.016, 0.023], a small square in the title bar's top-left corner) and "3. Unit of measure" (id 8, region [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125], a caption line directly under the title)
- Reference: neither shape is visible; the title bar's top-left corner is a flat, uninterrupted purple, and nothing appears between the title and the body of the slide.
- Candidate: a lighter-purple square sits in the title bar's top-left corner, and the gold text "Unit of measure" is drawn overlapping the bottom of the title band, right where the title's second/third line should be.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="4" name="Rectangle 3" hidden="1"/>...
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>...
  <a:t>Unit of measure</a:t>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both `p:cNvPr` elements carry `hidden="1"`, meaning neither should be painted. The candidate renders both at full opacity, and the "Unit of measure" caption additionally collides with the overflowing title text (finding 3).

### 2. Bold/underline/color run formatting dropped from "Transformation"

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), the quoted word in "On “Transformation” practice…"
- Reference: "Transformation" is bold, underlined, and colored gold-brown (`9A743A`), visually distinct from the surrounding white title text.
- Candidate: "Transformation" renders in the same plain white weight as the rest of the title — no bold, no underline, no color.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" b="1" u="sng" dirty="0">
    <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="9A743A"/></a:solidFill>
  </a:rPr><a:t>Transformation</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the run carries explicit `b="1"`, `u="sng"`, and a `solidFill` override on the `rPr` — direct, unambiguous run-level properties that the candidate does not apply.

### 3. Title text overflows its band and is overpainted by later shapes

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), nominal placeholder box (inherited from layout) [0.028, 0.025, 0.856, 0.079], but the wrapped text visibly occupies down to roughly [0.028, 0.0, 0.86, 0.16]
- Reference: the full string wraps to three lines that fit inside the ~0.107-slide-height dark band (master's "Rectangle 10", 731520 EMU tall) with room to spare.
- Candidate: the same string wraps into visibly larger glyphs, so only about one and a half lines fit before the text is cut off by the band edge; the remainder is hidden behind the erroneously-visible "Unit of measure" caption (finding 1) and the photo/body content group ("Group 4", which starts at y-fraction 0.157) painted on top of it. The result reads as "On “Transformation” practice we would have access to distinctive pool" followed by a sliver of overlapping, unreadable glyphs — "expertise, proprietary assets and IP to ensure successful capability building and knowledge transfer" never fully appears.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:titleStyle><a:lvl1pPr ...>
    <a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc>
    <a:defRPr lang="en-US" sz="2000" b="0" ...><a:latin typeface="+mj-lt"/></a:defRPr>
  </a:lvl1pPr></p:titleStyle>
  <!-- theme1.xml -->
  <a:majorFont><a:latin typeface="Calibri"/></a:majorFont>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: nothing on the slide, layout, or master overrides the inherited 20pt Calibri / 80% line-spacing title style, so the string should wrap identically to the reference. The candidate's wrap needs visibly more width per glyph and more line height, which is a text-font/sizing mismatch, not a content difference; the overpainting by finding 1's shapes is a direct downstream consequence of the title overflowing further than the reference's does.

### 4. Ellipse-cropped photos render as plain rectangles

- Where: "Picture 6" (id 57, region [0.038, 0.157, 0.153, 0.332]) and "Picture 58" (id 59, region [0.038, 0.476, 0.153, 0.65])
- Reference: both JPEG photos are clipped to a circle by their `prstGeom prst="ellipse"`.
- Candidate: both photos are drawn as full rectangles — square corners, and background pixels/edges that the ellipse crop should remove are visible.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:pic><p:spPr><a:xfrm>.../><a:prstGeom prst="ellipse"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom></p:spPr></p:pic>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `prstGeom prst="ellipse"` on a picture shape is a clip geometry, not just an outline; the picture fill must be clipped to the ellipse. The candidate paints the full unclipped rectangle for both instances of this same shape/property combination.

### 5. Bullet glyphs and hanging indent missing from the second-level list

- Where: TextBox 60 (id 61), region [0.175, 0.348, 0.96, 0.824] — six list items under "We have developed unparalleled proprietary assets and IP in this field:"
- Reference: each item is preceded by a "▪" bullet, sits at `marL=228600`, and wrapped continuation lines hang-indent under the first word (not under the bullet).
- Candidate: no bullet glyph appears on any of the six items, and the paragraphs sit flush with the parent line above them; wrapped continuation lines (e.g. "modules" after "Library of 600+ interactive in-person modules…") align to the left margin instead of hanging under the item's first line.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- shape's own lstStyle -->
  <a:lvl2pPr marL="193675" lvl="1" indent="-192088">
    <a:buClr><a:schemeClr val="tx2"/></a:buClr>
    <a:buSzPct val="125000"/><a:buFont typeface="Arial" charset="0"/><a:buChar char="▪"/>
  </a:lvl2pPr>
  <!-- each paragraph -->
  <a:pPr marL="228600" lvl="1" indent="-165100" defTabSz="913526" fontAlgn="base"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: every one of the six paragraphs sets `lvl="1"` with its own `marL`/`indent`, and the shape's local `lstStyle` defines the level-1 bullet (`buChar="▪"`, `buFont="Arial"`) that paragraphs at that level must inherit. The candidate applies neither the paragraph's own indent nor the inherited bullet, effectively treating all seven paragraphs (including the unindented lead-in) as flush level-0 text.

### 6. Master logo picture is drawn without its source crop

- Where: "Picture 11" (id 12, slide master), region [0.907, 0.011, 0.992, 0.097]
- Reference: the logo shows the infinity mark, the Arabic wordmark, and "ELIXIR" beneath it, tightly cropped with no extra content below.
- Candidate: the same box shows an extra, blurred line of tagline text below "ELIXIR" that the reference does not show, and the retained artwork reads softer/differently proportioned.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:blipFill rotWithShape="1"><a:blip r:embed="rId24" cstate="print"/>
    <a:srcRect l="15358" t="9779" r="21228" b="24405"/>
    <a:stretch/>
  </p:blipFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `srcRect` crops the source image by the given percentages (15.358% left, 9.779% top, 21.228% right, 24.405% bottom) before it is stretched into the picture's frame. The candidate appears to skip this crop, stretching more of the uncropped source (including the tagline text meant to be cut off at the bottom) into the same frame.
