## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a two-line title over a purple-circle honeycomb diagram with "Active Client Base" at the top and "ATM /ANSPs" at the center, surrounded by six white hexagons (Airlines / Aviation, Airports, Suppliers, Military, Authorities / Governments, Aircraft/ATM equipment manufacturers). The reference (LibreOffice) renders the full two-line title, a hexagon-tiled honeycomb with clean edges, and "ATM /ANSPs" as a single line. The candidate (BetterOffice) truncates the title after its first line, shows a stray orange "Unit of measure" label under the title, renders every honeycomb cell as a plain rectangle (leaving purple/oval-colored notches at the hexagon's untraced corners), and truncates "ATM /ANSPs" to "ATM /" only.

## Findings

### 1. Hidden shape "3. Unit of measure" is rendered visible

- Where: shape id 8, "3. Unit of measure", region `[0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125]` — a text box just under the title.
- Reference: not drawn at all.
- Candidate: drawn in orange text, overlapping the title's second line.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's `cNvPr` carries `hidden="1"`, which per the spec means the shape must not be rendered. BetterOffice ignores the attribute.

### 2. Text overflowing a shape's height is clipped instead of drawn past the box

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), region `[0.028, 0.025, 0.855, 0.079]`; also Rectangle 10 "ATM /ANSPs" (id 16), region `[0.426, 0.536, 0.558, 0.573]`.
- Reference: the title's two lines (split by an explicit `<a:br/>`, "...from all main " / "stakeholders and customers of the broader ATM & Aviation industry") both render fully below the nominal title box, and "ATM /ANSPs" renders as one line at the hexagon center.
- Candidate: the title shows only "Through this partnership we can combine insights & experience from all", with the word "main" and the entire second line cut off at the box's bottom edge. "ATM /ANSPs" is wrapped ("ATM /" then a second line) and the wrapped second line ("ANSPs") is clipped away for the same reason.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- layout title placeholder -->
  <a:ext cx="8208912" cy="369332"/>
  <a:bodyPr wrap="square" lIns="0" tIns="0" rIns="0" bIns="0" anchor="ctr">
  <!-- slide title body: forced 2-line break, no autofit -->
  <a:t>Through this partnership we can combine insights &amp; experience from all main </a:t><a:br/><a:t>stakeholders and customers of the broader ATM &amp; Aviation industry</a:t>
  ```
  ```xml
  <!-- Rectangle 10 "ATM /ANSPs" -->
  <a:ext cx="1313614" cy="251159"/>
  <a:bodyPr lIns="91440" tIns="0" rIns="91440" bIns="0" anchor="ctr"><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: neither shape sets `normAutofit` or any shrink-to-fit; the title box (369332 EMU tall, roughly one line) and the "ATM /ANSPs" box (251159 EMU, one line) are both nominally sized for fewer lines than they end up holding. OOXML text does not clip to the autoshape's declared extent by default — PowerPoint and LibreOffice both let it overflow — so BetterOffice's hard clip at the shape bounds drops real title content and part of a diagram label.

### 3. Honeycomb hexagons drawn as bounding-box rectangles, not the custGeom hexagon path

- Where: Freeform 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 18 (ids 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17, 20), region `[0.24, 0.221, 0.74, 0.879]` — the full honeycomb.
- Reference: each cell is a hexagon; hexagons tile edge-to-edge over the purple oval with no visible gaps, and the center cell is a tan hexagon.
- Candidate: each cell is drawn as its full rectangular bounding box. Because a hexagon's bounding box is wider/taller than the hexagon itself, adjacent rectangles overlap along shared edges but leave the oval's purple background exposed in a cross/plus pattern at the untraced diagonal corners (most visible around the center tan cell and between the four corner cells).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:custGeom>
    <a:pathLst><a:path w="856" h="584">
      <a:moveTo><a:pt x="224" y="0"/></a:moveTo>
      <a:lnTo><a:pt x="647" y="0"/></a:lnTo>
      <a:lnTo><a:pt x="855" y="295"/></a:lnTo>
      <a:lnTo><a:pt x="647" y="583"/></a:lnTo>
      <a:lnTo><a:pt x="224" y="583"/></a:lnTo>
      <a:lnTo><a:pt x="0" y="295"/></a:lnTo>
      <a:lnTo><a:pt x="224" y="0"/></a:lnTo>
    </a:path></a:pathLst>
  </a:custGeom>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the path explicitly traces a 6-point hexagon within the shape's extent. BetterOffice fills the shape's rectangular extent instead of following the `custGeom` path, which is visibly wrong on every one of the seven affected shapes.
