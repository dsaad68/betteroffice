## Reference vs candidate

Slide 7 is a four-row capability grid: a purple label column on the left ("Broad experience...", "Customer focused innovation...", "Experience with many...", "Expertise in technology"), each paired with a bulleted evidence box on the right, inside a bordered content panel under the deck's standard dark-purple title band and gold footer. LibreOffice renders clean square/dash bullets with hanging indents in every evidence box, bold gold styling on "Business Technology office" and "Independent", a tightly cropped Elixir logo top-right, and nothing over the title. BetterOffice drops every bullet glyph and hanging indent from the four evidence boxes, fails to bold or correctly color the two highlighted phrases, draws an extra "Unit of measure" label across the title's second line, and shows an uncropped, taller logo with a stray tagline line.

## Findings

### 1. Hidden shape rendered visible, overwriting the title

- Where: "3. Unit of measure" (id 8), region [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125] — directly on top of the title's second line
- Reference: nothing is drawn there; the title's second line ("airports as they consider service design and innovation") reads cleanly.
- Candidate: the string "Unit of measure" is painted in gold, overlapping the title's second line, which becomes unreadable underneath it.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `hidden="1"` on the shape's `cNvPr` means it must not be drawn. The candidate ignores the flag for this text shape (a sibling OLE graphicFrame with the same flag, id 14, is correctly suppressed), so it paints the shape's text directly over the title.

### 2. List bullets and hanging indents dropped from all body-copy boxes

- Where: "Rectangle 17" (id 16, region ≈[0.268,0.164,0.963,0.314]), "Rectangle 18" (id 17, ≈[0.266,0.356,0.961,0.508]), "Rectangle 19" (id 18, ≈[0.264,0.733,0.959,0.884]), "Rectangle 25" (id 22, ≈[0.266,0.548,0.961,0.694])
- Reference: each top-level bullet shows a small navy square (▪) with the wrapped second line indented to align under the first line's text; the "Wide range of experience..." sub-items in Rectangle 25 show an en-dash (–) bullet at a deeper indent.
- Candidate: no bullet glyph anywhere in these four boxes; every line (including wrapped continuations and the dash sub-items) starts flush at the box's left inset with no hanging indent, so the four boxes read as plain unindented paragraphs.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- shape lstStyle -->
  <a:lvl2pPr marL="193675" indent="-192088">...<a:buChar char="▪"/>...</a:lvl2pPr>
  <a:lvl3pPr marL="457200" indent="-261938">...<a:buChar char="–"/>...</a:lvl3pPr>
  <!-- paragraph itself -->
  <a:pPr marL="196850" lvl="1" indent="-158750" defTabSz="913526">...</a:pPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: paragraphs at `lvl="1"`/`lvl="2"` with no `buNone`/`buChar` of their own must take their bullet character from the shape's `lstStyle` entry for that level (`lvl2pPr`/`lvl3pPr`), and the paragraph's own `marL`/`indent` explicitly request a hanging indent. The candidate applies neither, across all four shapes — one root cause hitting every bulleted line on the slide (the bulk of `hot_cells` r2c2/r2c3).
