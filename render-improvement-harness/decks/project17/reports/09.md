## Reference vs candidate

Slide 9 shows the title "a consultancy's Airport experience" over two think-cell pie charts, "By Geography" and "By Function", each with a purple header bar and callout labels/numbers around the wedges. LibreOffice draws both pies fully colored with their percentage labels (white-on-wedge for most, dark for the pale "Organization" slice), the title bar clean, and the "Asia-\nPacific" label on two lines. BetterOffice drops both charts entirely (leaving a dashed placeholder rectangle and only the outer callout text), which also makes the white percentage numbers invisible since there is no colored wedge behind them, renders two shapes that are marked hidden in the XML, and drops the second line of the "Asia-\nPacific" label in favor of a tofu box.

## Findings

### 1. Both OLE pie charts (think-cell) are not rendered

- Where: "Object 19" (id 44, "By Geography" pie, region [0.169, 0.417, 0.382, 0.722]) and "Object 11" (id 31, "By Function" pie, region [0.631, 0.417, 0.842, 0.722])
- Reference: two fully drawn pie charts — geography split EMEA 45 / Americas 35 / Asia-Pacific 20, function split Strategy 48 / Finance 11 / Operations 10 / Organization 16 / Marketing & Sales 12 / IT/S 3 — each wedge labeled with its number, white text on the darker wedges and dark text on the pale "Organization" wedge.
- Candidate: no pie is drawn at all; only a dashed grey rectangle (an editing-frame outline, not present in the reference) occupies the chart's bounding box. The number labels that are colored white (20, 45, 35, 12, 3, 48, 10, 11) vanish against the blank white background; only "16" (colored `tx2`, dark) stays visible, because its wedge fill isn't there to give it contrast.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:graphicFrame><p:nvGraphicFramePr><p:cNvPr id="44" name="Object 19"/>...
  <a:graphicData uri="...presentationml/2006/ole">
    <mc:AlternateContent>
      <mc:Choice Requires="v"><p:oleObj progId="MSGraph.Chart.8" r:id="rId30"><p:embed/></p:oleObj></mc:Choice>
      <mc:Fallback>
        <p:oleObj progId="MSGraph.Chart.8" r:id="rId30"><p:embed/>
          <p:pic><p:blipFill><a:blip r:embed="rId31"/></p:blipFill></p:pic>
        </p:oleObj>
      </mc:Fallback>
    </mc:AlternateContent>
  </a:graphicData></p:graphicFrame>
  <!-- rId31 -> ppt/media/image15.emf; sibling Object 11 (id 31) uses rId29 -> ppt/media/image14.emf -->
  ```
  and, for one of the now-invisible numbers:
  ```xml
  <a:rPr ...><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill></a:rPr><a:t>20</a:t>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both graphic frames carry an OLE object with a raster (EMF) fallback image in `mc:Fallback/p:pic/p:blipFill`, per the OOXML fallback rule a renderer that cannot execute the OLE object should paint that fallback picture. The candidate paints neither the fallback picture nor any chart reconstruction, and additionally draws a dashed placeholder box that has no basis in the XML at all. This is one root cause (OLE/chart graphic not handled) that also explains the missing wedge-colored backdrop behind the white data labels — it is not a separate color bug.

### 2. Shapes marked `hidden="1"` are drawn anyway

- Where: "Rectangle 3" (id 4, region [0.0, 0.0, 0.016, 0.023], a small square in the title bar's top-left corner) and "3. Unit of measure" (id 8, region [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125], a caption line directly under the title)
- Reference: neither shape is visible; the title bar's top-left corner is a flat, uninterrupted purple, and nothing appears between the title and the body of the slide.
- Candidate: a lighter-purple square (`accent5` at `lumMod 75000`) sits in the title bar's top-left corner, and the gold text "Unit of measure" is drawn overlapping the bottom of the title band.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="4" name="Rectangle 3" hidden="1"/>...
  <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="accent5"><a:lumMod val="75000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill>

  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>...
  <a:t>Unit of measure</a:t>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both `p:cNvPr` elements carry `hidden="1"`, which per spec means the shape must not be painted (it still participates in the tree for tools/automation, e.g. think-cell). The candidate ignores the flag for both shapes and renders them at full opacity.

### 3. Second line of a two-line field label is replaced by a tofu glyph

- Where: "Rectangle 23" (id 46), region [0.17, 0.391, 0.223, 0.454]
- Reference: two lines, "Asia-" then "Pacific", both in the same dark (`tx2`) color and size.
- Candidate: only "Asia-" renders; the second line is replaced by a single hollow missing-glyph box, and "Pacific" does not appear anywhere on the slide.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:fld id="{C0280477-...}" type="datetime'''''''A''s''''''i''''''a''-''''''''''&#xA;Pa''''c''if''''''i''''c'">
    <a:rPr lang="en-US" altLang="en-US" sz="1400"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx2"/></a:solidFill></a:rPr>
    <a:t>Asia-
Pacific</a:t>
  </a:fld>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: this is a think-cell field (`a:fld`) whose `type` attribute is a hacked, non-standard value the renderer cannot and should not try to interpret; per spec, a renderer that doesn't recognize the field's type must fall back to the cached `<a:t>` text, here the literal two-line string "Asia-\nPacific". The candidate appears to choke on the embedded `&#xA;` line break inside that cached text (or on the field-type string itself), rendering the text before the break correctly and substituting a notdef glyph for what follows instead of continuing to "Pacific".
