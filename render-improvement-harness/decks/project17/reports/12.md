## Reference vs candidate

Slide 12 is a title-plus-two-column slide: a dark purple title band, a full-width purple "Body copy..." subheading bar, then four rows pairing a "Question" box on the left with a "How the vendor helps address it" answer box on the right, plus two photos on the far right. LibreOffice wraps the title into two lines that fit the band, keeps the subheading bar's two lines clear of its bottom edge, wraps every question/answer box's text with visible margin inside its border, and leaves both `hidden="1"` shapes invisible. BetterOffice wraps the same strings with narrower per-line capacity throughout, so the title's second line drops into the white body area and becomes illegible (white-on-white), the subheading bar's second line crowds its own bottom edge, three of the eight list boxes need an extra line and spill their last line past their border, a hidden accent square and a hidden "Unit of measure" caption are drawn where the reference shows nothing, and the master's corner logo shows extra uncropped tagline text.

## Findings

### 1. Shapes marked `hidden="1"` are drawn anyway

- Where: "Rectangle 3" (id 4, region [0.0, 0.0, 0.016, 0.023], a small square in the title bar's top-left corner) and "3. Unit of measure" (id 8, region [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125], a caption line directly under the title band, in the white area)
- Reference: neither shape is visible; the title bar's top-left corner is flat, uninterrupted purple, and nothing appears in the white area between the title band and the subheading bar.
- Candidate: a darker square sits in the title bar's top-left corner, and the gold/orange text "Unit of measure" is drawn in the white area right under the title band, overlapping where the title's overflowed second line also lands (finding 2).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="4" name="Rectangle 3" hidden="1"/>...
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>...
  <a:t>Unit of measure</a:t>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both `p:cNvPr` elements carry `hidden="1"`, meaning neither should be painted. The candidate renders both at full opacity.

### 2. Title's second line overflows the band and becomes white-on-white

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), nominal placeholder box (inherited from layout) [0.028, 0.025, 0.855, 0.079]
- Reference: "We will also have the ability to utilize "the vendor", a consultancy's Middle East Office's" / "Leadership Development Center as part of the capability building commitments" wraps to exactly two lines, both fully inside the dark title band (master's "Rectangle 10", 731520 EMU / ~76px tall).
- Candidate: the same string wraps one word earlier — line 1 ends at "...Middle East", line 2 starts "Office's Leadership Development Center..." — and because the line needs more vertical room, line 2's baseline falls below the purple band into the white body area, where the white title-colored glyphs become illegible against the white background; only "Unit of measure" (finding 1) is visible on top of it.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:titleStyle><a:lvl1pPr ...>
    <a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc>
    <a:defRPr lang="en-US" sz="2000" b="0" ...><a:latin typeface="+mj-lt"/></a:defRPr>
  </a:lvl1pPr></p:titleStyle>
  <!-- theme1.xml -->
  <a:majorFont><a:latin typeface="Calibri"/></a:majorFont>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: nothing on the slide, layout, or master overrides the inherited 20pt Calibri / 80% line-spacing title style, so the string should wrap identically to the reference. The earlier wrap point is a text-measurement/sizing mismatch, not a content difference, and it directly causes the second line to fall outside the band the master provides for it.

### 3. Subheading bar's second line crowds its own bottom edge

- Where: Rectangle 5 (id 430), region [0.027, 0.133, 0.972, 0.209], the "the vendor was launched in 2013..." purple bar
- Reference: both wrapped lines ("...their 4 most pressing human" / "capital questions") sit inside the bar with visible white-space margin below the second line before the bar's fill ends.
- Candidate: the same two lines wrap at effectively the same point, but the second line's descenders sit right at (and visually touch) the bar's bottom fill edge, with no margin left.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="269875" y="911729"/><a:ext cx="9361487" cy="521837"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="48365A"/></a:solidFill></p:spPr>
  <a:bodyPr ... anchor="ctr" anchorCtr="0" compatLnSpc="1"><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape is a fixed-height (`cy="521837"`), `noAutofit`, center-anchored box; for the same two lines to reach the shape's floor, the candidate must be rendering the lines taller (or with wider glyphs pushing wrap) than the reference's Calibri metrics, a sizing mismatch consistent with findings 2 and 4.

### 4. Question/answer list boxes wrap one word earlier and three overflow their border

- Where: left column — "Rectangle 5" (id 435, region [0.027, 0.257, 0.277, 0.39]), "Rectangle 5" (id 438, region [0.027, 0.411, 0.277, 0.574]), "Rectangle 13" (id 441, region [0.027, 0.595, 0.277, 0.789]), "Rectangle 13" (id 444, region [0.027, 0.81, 0.276, 0.929]); right column — "Rectangle 435" (id 436), "Rectangle 438" (id 439), "Rectangle 441" (id 442), "Rectangle 444" (id 445, region [0.346, 0.814, 0.647, 0.926])
- Reference: every box wraps its text with the last line sitting clearly inside the box's border, with visible margin (e.g. id 435 fits "How can I identify and develop / a pipeline of exceptional / leaders in my organization?" in 3 lines; id 445 fits its text in 3 lines ending "...(e.g. People Analytics)" well inside the border).
- Candidate: the same strings wrap one word earlier per line throughout (e.g. id 435 becomes "How can I identify and / develop a pipeline of / exceptional leaders in my / organization?", now 4 lines; id 441 grows from 5 to 6 lines; id 445 grows from 3 to 4 lines). Because each box has a fixed, `noAutofit` height, the extra line's text ("organization?" in 435, "performance?" in 441, "Analytics)" in 445) sits at or past the box's own border instead of inside it with margin.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:ext cx="2473325" cy="913566"/></a:xfrm>...<a:ln w="6350">...</a:ln></p:spPr>
  <a:bodyPr ... anchor="t" ...><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: none of these boxes' paragraphs or `lstStyle` set autofit or a different font size; the text content and run-level colors match the reference exactly (verified pixel-for-pixel on the tan `A99A72` runs), only the line-break positions and resulting line counts differ. This is the same glyph-width/text-measurement mismatch as findings 2 and 3, here repeated across every list box on the slide, which accounts for the top-left-heavy `hot_cells` (r1c1, r1c2, r2c1, r3c1).

### 5. Master logo picture is drawn without its source crop

- Where: "Picture 11" (id 12, slide master), region [0.907, 0.011, 0.992, 0.097]
- Reference: the logo shows the infinity mark, the Arabic wordmark, and "ELIXIR" beneath it, tightly cropped with no extra content below.
- Candidate: the same box shows an extra, blurred line of tagline text below "ELIXIR" that the reference does not show, and the retained artwork reads softer/differently proportioned.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:blipFill rotWithShape="1"><a:blip r:embed="rId24" cstate="print"/>
    <a:srcRect l="15358" t="9779" r="21228" b="24405"/>
    <a:stretch/>
  </p:blipFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `srcRect` crops the source image by the given percentages before it is stretched into the picture's frame. The candidate appears to skip this crop, stretching more of the uncropped source (including the tagline text meant to be cut off) into the same frame.
