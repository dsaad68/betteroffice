## Reference vs candidate

A content slide: a three-line title in white on a dark-purple header band, an Elixir logo top-right, and a single text box of gold-lead/black-body bullet points about aviation-industry expertise, with two nested sub-lists. LibreOffice renders the title compactly inside the header band, draws every bullet and sub-bullet glyph, colors only each paragraph's bold lead-in phrase gold, and keeps a hidden "Unit of measure" label and picture tagline off-screen. BetterOffice renders the title larger so it overflows the header band and collides with the body text, shows the hidden "Unit of measure" label, drops every bullet glyph, colors entire first lines gold instead of just the lead-in, and leaks an uncropped tagline line under the logo.

## Findings

### 1. Hidden shape rendered

- Where: "3. Unit of measure" (id 8), region ≈ [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125]
- Reference: nothing drawn in this region below the title.
- Candidate: a gold "Unit of measure" line (`Sakkal Majalla` fallback, `srgbClr C19859`) is drawn directly under the title, overlapping the header band and the top of the body text.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `hidden="1"` on `cNvPr` means the shape must not be drawn; LibreOffice honors this, BetterOffice does not.

### 2. Title text overflows the header band

- Where: "Title 1" (id 2), region ≈ [0.027, 0.029, 0.856, 0.083] (placeholder box; visible overflow extends to roughly y=0.21 in the candidate)
- Reference: "W" cap-height measures ~20px; line 1 reads "When it comes to Aviation industry we will have an access to a unique blend of", and all three wrapped lines sit inside the 731520-EMU-tall dark header rectangle.
- Candidate: "W" cap-height measures ~25px (~25% taller); line 1 stops at "…a unique" (fewer words fit at the larger size), and the wrapped lines run past the header rectangle's bottom edge onto the white background, overlapping the "Unit of measure" text and the first body bullet.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvPr><p:ph type="title"/></p:nvPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="269875" y="201414"/><a:ext cx="8208912" cy="369332"/></a:xfrm></p:spPr>
  <p:txBody><a:bodyPr/><a:lstStyle/><a:p><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" dirty="0"/><a:t>When it comes to Aviation industry...</a:t></a:r></a:p></p:txBody>
  ```
  The run carries no explicit `sz` or `latin` typeface, so it must resolve through the layout (`<a:bodyPr .../><a:noAutofit/>`, empty `lvl1pPr`) to the master's `p:titleStyle` (`sz="2000"`, theme major font Calibri). Both engines should therefore render an unscaled 20pt title with no shrink-to-fit.
- Why the candidate is wrong: with `noAutofit` and an explicit sz nowhere overridden, both renderers should agree on the resolved size; the ~25% cap-height difference indicates BetterOffice resolves a larger effective size (or substitute font metrics) for the inherited title style, and that alone is what pushes text past the fixed-height header shape the deck was authored against.

### 3. No bullet glyphs drawn

- Where: "Rectangle 8" (id 40), region ≈ [0.027, 0.133, 0.972, 0.937] — every paragraph in the box (7 level-1 items, 7 level-2 sub-items)
- Reference: every level-1 paragraph has a gold "•" at the hanging-indent position; every level-2 paragraph has a navy "–".
- Candidate: no bullet character is drawn for any paragraph at either level; text starts flush at the paragraph's `marL` position with no glyph in the reserved hanging-indent gap.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:pPr marL="228600" indent="-180975" ...><a:buClr><a:srgbClr val="9A743A"/></a:buClr>

    <a:buSzPct val="120000"/><a:buFont typeface="Arial"/><a:buChar char="–"/>...</a:pPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: every affected paragraph explicitly sets `buFont`/`buChar`/`buClr`; there is no `buNone` anywhere in this text box, so a bullet glyph is required at each of the 14 bulleted paragraphs.

### 4. Run color leaks across the first visual line of each paragraph

- Where: "Rectangle 8" (id 40) — every two/three-run paragraph, e.g. "Industry expertise: Serving a wide range…", "Experts: Dedicated group…", "Specific tools: Proprietary databases…", "Functional expertise and service lines: Strategy, operations…"
- Reference: only the bold lead-in phrase before the colon (e.g. "Industry expertise") is gold (`9A743A`); the colon and the rest of the sentence render black (`000000`/`002960`), including single-line paragraphs like "Experts: Dedicated group of international industry experts with wide experience in serving the industry".
- Candidate: the entire first visual line is gold, including the black run's text that fits on that line (e.g. "Industry expertise: Serving a wide range of airport operators, aviation authorities, ground handlers, air" is all gold). Once that same black run wraps onto a second line, the continuation ("air traffic control organizations across multiple functional topics in long term client relationships") correctly renders black. Single-line paragraphs such as "Experts: …" never recover the black color at all, since the whole run stays on line 1.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr ...><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="9A743A"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr><a:t>Industry expertise</a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr ...><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="002960"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr><a:t>: </a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr ...><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="000000"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr><a:t>Serving a wide range of airport operators...</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each run carries its own explicit `solidFill`; nothing in the XML makes a run's color depend on which visual line it lands on. The candidate's line-1-only gold tint has no basis in the markup.

- Why the candidate is wrong: `srcRect` with `b="24405"` (~24.4%) crops the bottom quarter of the source image out of the fill; a 24% bottom crop is well beyond a rounding or hinting difference, so the extra tagline text should not be visible at all.
