## Reference vs candidate

This is the deck's title slide: an Elixir/SANS logo header, "SANS Proposal" as the title, and a dark purple footer band holding the date and a confidentiality notice. LibreOffice draws the title at 40pt in dark purple (theme accent5 at 75% luminance) and the date at 28pt in gold (accent6), with "th" raised as a superscript. BetterOffice draws both text runs far smaller and in a flat dark grey, so the title is undersized and the date is almost unreadable against the purple band; "th" is not superscripted, and the confidentiality line ends with a stray missing-glyph box.

## Findings

### 1. Placeholder text loses the layout's size/color override, falls back to the master default

- Where: "Text Placeholder 3" (id 4, title, region ≈ [0.042, 0.479, 0.964, 0.657]) and "Text Placeholder 4" (id 5, date, region ≈ [0.042, 0.741, 0.958, 0.878])
- Reference: title "SANS Proposal" at 40pt, dark purple (measured RGB 72,54,90 = accent5 `604878` at `lumMod 75000`, exact match); date "15th Jan 2018" at 28pt, gold (accent6 `C19859`).
- Candidate: both strings render markedly smaller (title cap-height ≈25px vs. reference ≈46px; date ≈18px vs. ≈27px — both close to the 20pt/40pt and 20pt/28pt ratios) and in flat grey (measured RGB 50,50,50 = `323232` exactly).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- slideLayout1.xml, idx=11 (title) -->
  <p:ph type="body" sz="quarter" idx="11" hasCustomPrompt="1"/>
  <a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr><a:defRPr sz="4000" b="0" baseline="0">
    <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="accent5"><a:lumMod val="75000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill>
  </a:defRPr></a:lvl1pPr></a:lstStyle>

  <!-- slideLayout1.xml, idx=12 (date) -->
  <p:ph type="body" sz="quarter" idx="12" hasCustomPrompt="1"/>
  <a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr><a:defRPr sz="2800" b="0" baseline="0">
    <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="accent6"/></a:solidFill>
  </a:defRPr></a:lvl1pPr></a:lstStyle>

  <!-- slideMaster1.xml, bodyStyle lvl1pPr (what the candidate actually renders with) -->
  <a:defRPr sz="2000" b="0"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx2"/></a:solidFill></a:defRPr>
  <!-- clrMap: tx2 -> dk2 -> srgbClr 323232, which is the grey the candidate paints -->
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the slide's `<p:sp>` for both placeholders has an empty `<p:spPr/>` and a run with no `rPr` size/fill, so both properties must resolve through inheritance: slide → layout placeholder `lstStyle` → master `txStyles/bodyStyle`. The layout placeholder explicitly overrides `sz` and `solidFill` for both idx 11 and idx 12. The candidate's measured size and color exactly match the master's generic `bodyStyle` `lvl1pPr` (sz 2000, `tx2`→`323232`) instead, meaning it skips the layout-level override and falls straight through to the master default. This is one root cause hitting two shapes. On the date placeholder specifically this crosses from cosmetic into `high` severity: `323232` grey text on the footer's `accent5 lumMod 50000` (`302040`-ish) purple background has almost no contrast, so a reader effectively cannot read the date.

### 2. Ordinal superscript not applied

- Where: "Text Placeholder 4" (id 5), the "th" in "15th Jan 2018", region ≈ [0.09, 0.74, 0.14, 0.79]
- Reference: "th" is raised and reduced in size relative to "15", the standard ordinal superscript treatment.
- Candidate: "th" sits on the same baseline as "15" at the same size — reads as plain "15th".
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" dirty="0"/><a:t>15</a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" baseline="30000" dirty="0"/><a:t>th</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `baseline="30000"` on the "th" run requests a 30%-of-line-height superscript raise (which PowerPoint/LibreOffice also shrink proportionally); the candidate ignores it entirely and paints the run inline.

### 3. Stray tofu glyph for a literal tab character

- Where: footer confidentiality text ("This document is confidential..."), region ≈ [0.04, 0.962, 0.991, 0.987]
- Reference: the line ends cleanly after "...is prohibited." with no visible trailing mark.
- Candidate: a visible missing-glyph box (☐/tofu) is drawn immediately after the trailing period.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1100" dirty="0">...</a:rPr>
  <a:t>This document is confidential and any unauthorized use without the permission of Elixir is prohibited.	</a:t></a:r>
  ```
  (the run's text ends with a literal tab character, `\t`, after the period)
- Why the candidate is wrong: a raw tab character embedded in `<a:t>` has no printable glyph — it should advance the cursor (or be effectively invisible at end-of-line) rather than be drawn with a notdef/tofu glyph. LibreOffice renders nothing visible for it; the candidate renders a visible replacement-character box, which is new marginal content not present in the reference.

## Not reported

- The date placeholder's text starts a few pixels further right in the candidate than the reference (55px vs. 47px) — a byproduct of the smaller fallback font's different left side-bearing, not an independent position bug; already covered by finding 1.
- Ordinary anti-aliasing/hinting differences on the logo artwork (Elixir infinity mark, SANS triangle) between the two renderers.
- Minor sub-pixel edge softness on the purple footer band boundary.
