## Reference vs candidate

Slide 11 is a two-panel comparison: a pie chart ("Aspects of transformations a consultancy focus on") on the left and a world map with regional percentages ("Geographic experience") on the right, under a title banner. LibreOffice renders the title on two full lines, keeps the "3. Unit of measure" caption hidden, draws the pie chart as a complete colored wheel with readable single-line callouts, and draws a recognizable, correctly outlined world map. BetterOffice truncates the title's second line to invisibility, shows the "Unit of measure" caption that should be hidden, drops the pie chart's chart image entirely (leaving only floating, incorrectly-wrapped text labels), and renders the world map as a jumble of rectangular blobs instead of country outlines.

## Findings

### 1. Hidden "Unit of measure" caption is drawn

- Where: shape "3. Unit of measure" (id 8), region [0.051, 0.083, 0.938, 0.125], directly under the title banner.
- Reference: not drawn (correctly hidden).
- Candidate: drawn in gold/accent text, overlapping the space where the title's second line should be.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cNvPr id="8" name="3. Unit of measure" hidden="1"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's `cNvPr` carries `hidden="1"`. Per the spec this shape must not be rendered on the slide surface; BetterOffice ignores the flag.

### 2. Title uses the wrong font, changing the line break

- Where: "Title 1" (id 2), region [0.027, 0.025, 0.855, 0.079].
- Reference: title text rendered in a Calibri-like face; "Where a consultancy have extensive experience in delivering transformation" fits on line 1.
- Candidate: rendered in a visibly wider, more geometric sans-serif; only "Where a consultancy have extensive experience in delivering" fits on line 1 before wrapping.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- master.xml titleStyle -->
  <a:defRPr ...><a:latin typeface="+mj-lt"/></a:defRPr>
  <!-- theme.xml majorFont -->
  <a:latin typeface="Calibri Light"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the title placeholder has no run-level `latin` override, so it must resolve to the theme major font (Calibri Light, or a metric-compatible substitute such as Carlito). The candidate's substitute is noticeably wider, which pushes the line-break point earlier than the reference.

### 3. Title's wrapped second line is clipped to a sliver

- Where: "Title 1" (id 2), region [0.027, 0.025, 0.855, 0.12] (the band just below the visible first line, before "3. Unit of measure").
- Reference: both wrapped lines of the title are fully visible, extending past the placeholder's declared height into the banner.
- Candidate: pixel sampling of that band (y≈30–55px, x 60–650px) shows almost entirely background-color pixels (48,36,60) with only sparse pure-white (255,255,255) specks — the tips of ascenders/dots — instead of full glyphs.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- slideLayout4.xml, ph type="title" -->
  <a:xfrm><a:off x="272480" y="170934"/><a:ext cx="8208912" cy="369332"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:bodyPr wrap="square" anchor="ctr"><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the placeholder box is only tall enough for one line (cy = 369332 EMU) and explicitly declares `noAutofit`, meaning text must not be shrunk to fit — the second wrapped line is expected to overflow past the box bounds and remain visible, as LibreOffice does. The candidate appears to clip rendering to the shape's bounding box instead of letting the overflow draw.

### 4. Pie chart (embedded OLE chart) is not drawn

- Where: graphicFrame "Object 426" (id 457), region [0.092, 0.283, 0.455, 0.811].
- Reference: full colored pie chart (56/22/9/12/2 wedges) with labels tied to their wedges.
- Candidate: no wedges at all — just a dashed bounding-box outline and the same text labels floating with no chart behind them.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:graphicFrame><p:cNvPr id="457" name="Object 426"/>
    <a:graphicData uri=".../ole">
      <p:oleObj name="Chart" progId="MSGraph.Chart.8">
        <p:pic><p:blipFill><a:blip r:embed="rId418"/></p:blipFill></p:pic>
      </p:oleObj>
  ```
  `rId418` → `ppt/media/image18.emf`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: this graphicFrame is not hidden and its OLE fallback picture is an EMF image that should be decoded and drawn like any other picture fill; the candidate renders nothing for it.

### 5. World map country shapes render as bounding-box rectangles

- Where: "Group 5" (id 6) and its ~150 "Freeform NN" children, e.g. "Freeform 41" (id 36), region [0.552, 0.241, 0.955, 0.825].
- Reference: recognizable continents with thin, curved coastline outlines.
- Candidate: a cluster of overlapping, axis-aligned rectangular blobs roughly where each country/landmass should be.
- XML: "Freeform 41"'s `custGeom` `pathLst` contains 246 `lnTo` segments describing a real coastline outline (not a rectangle), e.g.:
  ```xml
  <a:pathLst><a:path w="1170" h="865"><a:moveTo><a:pt x="846" y="825"/></a:moveTo><a:lnTo><a:pt x="846" y="844"/></a:lnTo>...(246 segments)...
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the path data is a genuine multi-vertex outline; drawing it as its bounding rectangle instead means the custGeom path is not being parsed/rasterized for these shapes.

### 6. `wrap="none"` labels are wrapped and split mid-word

- Where: pie-chart callout labels "Program management" (464), "Capability building" (460), "Organizational diagnostics" (461), "Culture transformation" (458), "Transformational change" (466), and their adjacent number labels (465, 463, 462, 459, 467). Region [0.06, 0.34, 0.52, 0.81] (scattered around the pie chart).
- Reference: each label renders on a single line (box auto-sized wider than its declared width via `spAutoFit`-adjacent think-cell layout), e.g. "Program management" as one line.
- Candidate: labels wrap onto 2–3 lines, sometimes splitting a single word ("Capability" → "Capabilit" / "y", "Organizational" → "Organization" / "al", "Transformational" → "Transformatio" / "nal").
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:bodyPr vert="horz" wrap="none" ... anchor="ctr"><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `wrap="none"` means the text box must not wrap — text should extend past the shape's declared width on a single line, exactly as LibreOffice does. The candidate wraps to the shape's literal width, and its line-breaking additionally breaks inside words rather than only at spaces.
