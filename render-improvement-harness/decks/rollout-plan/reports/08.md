## Reference vs candidate

Slide 8, "Roles & Responsibilities", is a three-column layout: a colored header band per column ("Business Lead" purple, "Business Contact" teal, "Team Member" blue) with white title text, a light-gray body panel underneath holding a bulleted responsibility list, and a small EMF clip-art illustration of three people under the first column. LibreOffice renders all of this as designed. BetterOffice draws the correct panel positions, fills, and body text content, but the header text renders in an unreadable dark color instead of white, every bulleted list loses its bullet glyphs and hanging indent, and the people illustration is dropped entirely.

## Findings

### 1. Header text renders dark instead of white

- Where: the three header bands "Business Lead" (id 7), "Business Contact" (id 8), "Team Member" (id 9), region x 0.022–0.978, y 0.228–0.33.
- Reference: "Business Lead", "Business Contact", "Team Member" render in solid white, clearly legible against the purple/teal/blue fills.
- Candidate: the same labels render in a dark gray/near-black tone, barely distinguishable from their colored backgrounds.
- XML:
```xml
<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2400" dirty="0">
  <a:gradFill>
    <a:gsLst>
      <a:gs pos="0"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
      <a:gs pos="100000"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
    </a:gsLst>
    <a:lin ang="5400000" scaled="0"/>
  </a:gradFill>
  <a:ea typeface="Segoe UI" pitchFamily="34" charset="0"/>
  <a:cs typeface="Segoe UI" pitchFamily="34" charset="0"/>
</a:rPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the run carries an explicit `a:gradFill` whose two stops are both `FFFFFF` — a degenerate (flat) white gradient, i.e. solid white text. The shape's `p:style/a:fontRef` also points at `schemeClr val="lt1"` (light/white) as a fallback. The candidate appears to ignore the run-level `gradFill` (and the `fontRef` fallback) and falls back to a default dark text color instead, which the XML does not support under any inheritance path.

### 2. Bulleted lists lose their bullet glyphs and hanging indent

- Where: all three body panels "Rectangle 10" (id 11), "Rectangle 11" (id 12), "Rectangle 12" (id 13), region x 0.022–0.978, y 0.33–0.901.
- Reference: each paragraph is a bulleted list item — a "•" glyph sits at the left edge of the panel, and wrapped continuation lines align under the first line's text (a classic hanging indent), e.g. "Determine when items" / "need escalation".
- Candidate: no bullet glyph is drawn anywhere, and the first line of each paragraph starts at the same x-position as its wrapped continuation lines — the hanging indent collapses so every line sits flush at one margin.
- XML:
```xml
<a:pPr marL="342900" indent="-342900">
  <a:buFont typeface="Arial" panose="020B0604020202020204" pitchFamily="34" charset="0"/>
  <a:buChar char="•"/>
</a:pPr>
<a:r>
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2000" dirty="0">
    <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:solidFill>
  </a:rPr>
  <a:t>Determine when items need escalation</a:t>
</a:r>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `marL="342900" indent="-342900"` together with `buChar char="•"` is a standard first-line-outdent bullet: the first line starts at `marL + indent = 0` (where the bullet is drawn) and wrapped lines start at `marL`. The candidate applies neither the negative first-line indent nor the bullet character, rendering an unbulleted, non-hanging block of text instead. All 15 list paragraphs across the three panels share this same `buChar`/`marL`/`indent` pattern, so this is one systemic cause.
