## Reference vs candidate

Slide 5, "Governance Recommendations," is a two-column layout: a purple "Escalations" header over a bulleted conditions/process list on the left, and a blue "Project Status" header over a status-legend table and a Risk/Issue table on the right. LibreOffice renders both header labels in white, draws the bulleted list with "•" glyphs and hanging indents, and fully renders both tables with their row text, fills, and banding. BetterOffice renders the header labels in the dark default text color, drops the bullet glyphs and hanging indent from the list (while also mis-indenting the two non-bulleted lead-in lines), and renders neither table's content at all — each shows only as an empty dashed box with the literal word "Table" centered in it.

## Findings

### 1. Both tables render as empty placeholder boxes

- Where: Table 17 (id 18), the green/yellow/red status legend table at roughly x 0.511–0.978, y 0.299–0.725; Table 18 (id 19), the Risk/Issue table at roughly x 0.511–0.978, y 0.774–0.882.
- Reference: Table 17 shows its three rows of status descriptions ("Has no blockers...", "Has a blocker...", "Has a major obstacle...") next to the colored circles. Table 18 shows a purple "Risk"/"Issue" header column next to "Something bad that might happen" / "Something bad that did happen", with row banding from the table style.
- Candidate: both graphic frames render as a light dashed-border rectangle with a small gray "Table" label centered in it — no cell text, no fills, no borders, no table style applied.
- XML:
```xml
<a:graphicData uri="http://schemas.openxmlformats.org/drawingml/2006/table">
  <a:tbl>
    <a:tblPr firstRow="1" bandRow="1">
      <a:tableStyleId>{2D5ABB26-0587-4C30-8999-92F81FD0307C}</a:tableStyleId>
    </a:tblPr>
    ...
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both graphic frames carry a complete `a:tbl` with grid, rows, cell text runs, and a `tableStyleId`. The candidate draws neither the cell content nor any styling, drawing a generic "Table" placeholder instead — table rendering appears unimplemented for this deck's table style rather than merely mis-rendered.

### 2. Header labels render in the dark theme text color instead of white

- Where: Rectangle 5 "Escalations" (id 6) and Rectangle 10 "Project Status" (id 11), header bars at roughly y 0.196–0.279.
- Reference: both labels render in solid white against their purple/blue fills.
- Candidate: both labels render in the dark default text color, low-contrast against the same fills.
- XML:
```xml
<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2400" dirty="0">
  <a:gradFill>
    <a:gsLst>
      <a:gs pos="0"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
      <a:gs pos="100000"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
    </a:gsLst>
    <a:lin ang="5400000" scaled="0"/>
  </a:gradFill>
</a:rPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each run has an explicit `a:gradFill` that is a degenerate white-to-white gradient (i.e. solid white), rather than `a:solidFill`. The candidate appears to only resolve `a:solidFill` on run properties and falls back to the inherited default color when the run specifies a gradient fill instead.

### 3. Bulleted list loses its bullet glyphs and hanging indent

- Where: Text Placeholder 1 (id 2), the four bulleted paragraphs under "Prerequisite Conditions:" and "Process:", region roughly x 0.022–0.489, y 0.279–0.882.
- Reference: each of the four items ("Amber and Camille are to track...", "Amber and Camille are to determine...", "Business contact or team member updates...", "Business contact raises...", "Amber and Camille facilitate...") is prefixed with a "•" bullet, with wrapped continuation lines indented to align under the first word rather than the bullet.
- Candidate: no bullet glyph is drawn on any of these paragraphs, and every line — including wrapped continuation lines — starts flush at the same left inset as the non-bulleted lead-in lines, so the list items are no longer visually separated from "Prerequisite Conditions:" and "Process:".
- XML:
```xml
<a:pPr marL="342900" lvl="1" indent="-342900">
  <a:buFont typeface="Arial" panose="020B0604020202020204" pitchFamily="34" charset="0"/>
  <a:buChar char="•"/>
</a:pPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each bulleted paragraph declares an explicit `a:buChar` bullet and a hanging indent (`marL="342900"` with `indent="-342900"`) local to the paragraph. The candidate renders neither the bullet nor the hanging indent, left-aligning every line at the box's left edge instead.

### 4. Lead-in lines are indented instead of flush left

- Where: Text Placeholder 1 (id 2), the "Prerequisite Conditions:" and "Process:" paragraphs (`<a:pPr lvl="1"/>` with no local override).
- Reference: both lines sit flush against the placeholder's left inset, at the same x position as the box edge.
- Candidate: both lines are indented roughly one tab-stop to the right of the box edge — further right than the (also mis-rendered flush-left) bulleted items below them.
- XML: `<a:lvl2pPr marL="0" indent="0"><a:buClr><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:buClr><a:buFont typeface="Wingdings".../><a:buNone/><a:defRPr sz="1961"/></a:lvl2pPr>` (slideLayout17.xml, placeholder idx="10")
- Why the candidate is wrong: these paragraphs carry no local `marL`/`indent`, so they should inherit `marL="0" indent="0"` from the layout's `lvl2pPr` for this exact placeholder (idx 10). The candidate instead applies some non-zero indent, suggesting the layout-level list-style override for this placeholder/level is not being consulted.
