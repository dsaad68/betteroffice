## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a title ("Quarterly Plan | Partner Engines") over a single large table listing partner engines, their descriptions, a fiscal year H2 activities, outcomes, assumptions/dependencies, and revenue projections. LibreOffice renders the full 6-column, 10-row table with a dark-blue header row, white header text, banded body rows, and all cell text. BetterOffice renders the title and slide-number placeholder correctly but draws only an empty box with a dashed border and the placeholder label "Table" where the table should be — none of the header, cell fills, borders, or text content is drawn.
