## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a donut-chart org diagram: a white centre circle ("PARTNER STRATEGY & PROGRAMS") ringed by three colored wedge bands (COMMUNITIES/GROWTH/INCENTIVES), an outer light-tint ring of category labels, and an outermost gray ring of names, under a "Partner Strategy & Programs Team" title and a "Team Charter" caption bar. LibreOffice renders the full curved wedge geometry with arced text following the rings. BetterOffice renders every wedge as its rectangular bounding box, ignores the arched-text warp on the three ring labels, and clips the multi-line title to its shape frame.

## Findings

### 1. Donut wedges rendered as bounding-box rectangles

- Where: all `custGeom` shapes forming the donut (outer name-ring wedges ids 3-20, inner colored-ring wedges ids 21-23 inside Group 52), region roughly the whole diagram, x0.2-0.8 y0.14-0.91.
- Reference: each shape is a curved pie/ring wedge (bezier-defined outline) with the correct fill and a thin white/gray stroke, tiling seamlessly into the ring.
- Candidate: each shape draws as a plain axis-aligned rectangle sized to its `a:ext`/bounding box, with the correct fill color but no curvature — the rectangles visibly overlap their neighbors (e.g. the navy "COMMUNITIES" wedge covers into the light-blue "Events (Inspire)" rectangle, and the outer gray name boxes overlap each other in a stepped pattern).
- XML: shape id 3 ("Freeform 6"):
  ```xml
  <a:custGeom>
    <a:pathLst>
      <a:path w="2982" h="1441">
        <a:moveTo><a:pt x="0" y="0"/></a:moveTo>
        <a:cubicBezTo><a:pt x="1091" y="0"/><a:pt x="2147" y="385"/><a:pt x="2982" y="1086"/></a:cubicBezTo>
        <a:lnTo><a:pt x="2684" y="1441"/></a:lnTo>
        <a:cubicBezTo><a:pt x="1932" y="810"/><a:pt x="982" y="464"/><a:pt x="0" y="464"/></a:cubicBezTo>
        <a:lnTo><a:pt x="0" y="0"/></a:lnTo>
        <a:close/>
      </a:path>
    </a:pathLst>
  </a:custGeom>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the path is explicitly a curved quadrilateral (two `cubicBezTo` arcs joined by straight edges), not a rectangle; the candidate is falling back to the shape's bounding box rather than parsing/drawing the `custGeom` path. This single defect accounts for the majority of the slide's diff (all four hot cells sit inside the diagram area).

### 2. Arched ring-label text drawn flat instead of warped

- Where: TextBox 25 "COMMUNITIES" (id 26, region ~x0.37-0.5 y0.33-0.46), TextBox 26 "INCENTIVES" (id 27, region ~x0.42-0.57 y0.51-0.66), TextBox 38 "GROWTH" (id 39, rotated 64.75°, region ~x0.5-0.63 y0.33-0.48).
- Reference: text follows the curvature of its ring band ("COMMUNITIES" and "INCENTIVES" curve concave-down/up along the donut, "GROWTH" arcs along its wedge), fully spelled out and confined to the band.
- Candidate: text is laid out as a straight line at the shape's un-warped position. "INCENTIVES" (no rotation on this shape) prints horizontally directly under "PARTNER STRATEGY & PROGRAMS" inside the white centre circle instead of curving along the bottom cyan band, and "COMMUNITIES" is truncated to "COMMUNITI" because the straight run doesn't fit/curve within its box.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:bodyPr spcFirstLastPara="1" wrap="square" lIns="0" tIns="0" rIns="0" bIns="0" numCol="1" rtlCol="0">
    <a:prstTxWarp prst="textArchDown"><a:avLst/></a:prstTxWarp>
    <a:spAutoFit/>
  </a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `a:prstTxWarp` with `prst="textArchDown"`/`"textArchUp"` is a WordArt-style text-on-a-path transform defined in the schema (§20.1.9.19); the candidate ignores it and lays the run out as ordinary rectangular text, which both mispositions and visually truncates the label.

### 3. Title text clipped to its shape frame instead of overflowing

- Where: Title 1 (id 54), region x0.022-0.308 y0.042-0.173.
- Reference: "Partner Strategy & Programs Team" wraps to 4 lines at 47pt and overflows below the shape's nominal 899665 EMU (~0.98in) height, fully visible against the white background.
- Candidate: only the first line ("Partner") is fully visible; the second line is cut off partway through its ascenders, and lines 3-4 are invisible entirely — the text is being clipped to the shape's bounding box.
- XML: `<p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="269240" y="289511"/><a:ext cx="3491990" cy="899665"/></a:xfrm><a:prstGeom prst="rect"/></p:spPr><p:txBody><a:bodyPr/>` — no `a:normAutofit`, `a:spAutoFit`, or explicit clipping directive is present.
- Why the candidate is wrong: with no autofit element, OOXML text boxes shrink neither font nor box and simply let overflowing text spill outside the shape's frame (this is the standard PowerPoint/LibreOffice behavior, confirmed by the reference render); the candidate instead clips paint to the shape bounds, hiding 75% of the title.

## Not reported

- Anti-aliasing / sub-pixel edge softness differs slightly on every gray outer-ring boundary — expected renderer difference, ignored.
- Minor kerning/glyph-width differences on the small rotated name labels (e.g. "Sarah Broadbent/Sharon Lee") — consistent with normal font-fallback rasterization variance, not reported as a separate finding beyond finding 4.
