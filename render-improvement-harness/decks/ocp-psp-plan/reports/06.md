## Reference vs candidate

The slide is titled "Dependencies" and consists of a title text box plus one large table (7 columns x 9 rows) covering nearly the whole slide body, listing partner-program dependencies with a dark-blue header row and per-cell text. LibreOffice renders the title and the full table with header fill, borders, and all cell text. BetterOffice renders only the title correctly; the entire table area is a blank rectangle with a thin dashed border and a single small gray "Table" caption centered in it — none of the header fill, borders, or the ~50 text runs of cell content are drawn.
