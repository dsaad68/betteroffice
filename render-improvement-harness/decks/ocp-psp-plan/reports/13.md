## Reference vs candidate

Slide 13 is a section-divider slide: a solid dark-blue background (`01178F`) with a single title placeholder reading "Budget & Resources Needed". LibreOffice renders the title very large (about 60pt), bold-weight, and pure white, occupying roughly the left two-thirds of the upper-middle band of the slide. BetterOffice renders the same text far smaller (about 19-20pt) and in solid black, which is almost unreadable against the dark blue background.

## Findings

### 1. Title text is tiny and black instead of large and white

- Where: "Title 1" (id 2), the sole shape on the slide, placeholder type `title`. Region (layout-defined box, slide fraction): `[0.022, 0.304, 0.967, 0.465]`.
- Reference: LibreOffice draws "Budget & Resources Needed" at a large size (~60pt) in solid white, matching the slide layout's own text style for this placeholder.
- Candidate: BetterOffice draws the same text at a much smaller size (~19-20pt, measured cap-height ≈23px in the 1280x720 render) in solid black (sampled core pixel `(0,0,0)`), which barely registers against the `01178F` background.
- XML: The slide's title shape carries no local text formatting at all:

```xml
<!-- slide13.xml -->
<p:sp><p:nvSpPr>...<p:nvPr><p:ph type="title"/></p:nvPr></p:nvSpPr>
<p:spPr/>
<p:txBody><a:bodyPr/><a:lstStyle/>
  <a:p><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" dirty="0"/><a:t>Budget &amp; Resources Needed</a:t></a:r></a:p>
</p:txBody></p:sp>
```

  so the run's size and fill must come from inheritance. The slide layout (`slideLayout29.xml`) defines this exact placeholder with an explicit size and fill override:

```xml
<!-- layout: title placeholder lstStyle -->
<a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr sz="6000" spc="-89" baseline="0">
  <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:solidFill>
</a:defRPr></a:lvl1pPr></a:lstStyle>
```

  The slide master's `titleStyle` (the next fallback level) instead specifies a much smaller size and a gradient fill:

```xml
<!-- master2.xml titleStyle -->
<a:defRPr sz="1962" ...>
  <a:gradFill><a:gsLst>
    <a:gs pos="0"><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:gs>
    <a:gs pos="100000"><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:gs>
  </a:gsLst><a:lin ang="5400000" scaled="0"/></a:gradFill>
</a:defRPr>
```

  The rendered size (~19-20pt) matches the master's `sz="1962"`, not the layout's `sz="6000"` — evidence that BetterOffice is skipping the layout's placeholder `lstStyle` and resolving text properties straight from the master `titleStyle`. The rendered fill (pure black) matches neither the master's `tx1` scheme color (which resolves to `505050` dark gray under the master's own `clrMap`) nor the layout's `tx1` (which resolves to white `FFFFFF` under the layout's `overrideClrMapping bg1="dk1" tx1="lt1"`) — consistent with the master's `gradFill` on text not being resolved at all and falling back to an engine default black.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the layout's own `lstStyle` for this placeholder (`sz="6000"`, `solidFill` to `tx1`) is more specific than the master `titleStyle` and must win per placeholder-inheritance rules; LibreOffice's output (large, white) is exactly what that layout override plus the layout's `clrMapOvr` produce. BetterOffice appears to bypass the layout placeholder style entirely for this shape, using the master's smaller size and failing to resolve the master's unsupported `gradFill` into any color.

## Not reported

- A few pixels of horizontal offset between the two title text starting positions — expected given the drastically different rendered font size and resulting font-metric side bearings, not an independent bug.
- Minor anti-aliasing differences along the background/border edge of the slide.
