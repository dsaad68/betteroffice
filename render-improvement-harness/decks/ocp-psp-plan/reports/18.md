## Reference vs candidate

Slide 18 is the same "Quarterly Plan | Partner Engines" table seen on slide 17, this time with a 10-row body (Cloud Ready, MPN, P-Seller, Storytelling, Microsoft Partner Events, Comms, Global Incentives, Local Accelerators, PIE, and a tenth row cut off at the bottom of the frame), each row pairing a dark navy "Engine" label cell with bulleted "a fiscal year Big Rocks" text. A large translucent "OLD VERSION" watermark and a green "Updated from OneNote 9/11 8:40p" callout sit on top. The BetterOffice candidate draws the title, the green callout, and the watermark text correctly, but the table graphic frame itself is empty: only a dashed placeholder outline, a small gray "Engine" caption near the top edge of the frame, and a centered gray "Table" caption appear — no grid lines, cell fills, header row, or any of the ten rows of body text.

## Findings

### 1. Table graphic frame not rendered at all

- Where: `Table 3` (graphicFrame id 4), region `[0.007, 0.07, 0.988, 1.02]` — the entire content area below the title, extending slightly past the slide's bottom edge
- Reference: full 2-column × 10-row table with a dark navy header row ("Engine" / "a fiscal year Big Rocks"), navy row-header cells for each engine name, and bulleted/sub-bulleted body text in every row (including bold sub-headers "Inspire:" and "Partner Briefings:" inside the "Microsoft Partner Events" row, and nested sub-bullets under "Global Incentives" and "Local Accelerators").
- Candidate: an empty dashed content box with no grid, no fills, and no body text; only two small gray captions are visible — "Engine" (the literal text of the header row's first cell, position-bled to the top of the frame) and "Table" (a generic placeholder caption, centered in the frame, identical to what slide 17's identically-typed table shows).
