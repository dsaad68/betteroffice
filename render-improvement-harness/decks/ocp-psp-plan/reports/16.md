## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a single-table summary of the quarterly partner plan, with a title ("Quarterly Plan | Partner Engines") and a 7-column, 8-row table filling most of the slide body. LibreOffice renders the title plus the fully populated, styled table (banded rows, dark-blue header row, borders, all cell text). BetterOffice renders only the title and slide number; the table's bounding box is drawn as a dashed placeholder outline with the literal word "Table" centered in it, and none of the table's rows, cells, borders, fills, or text are drawn.
