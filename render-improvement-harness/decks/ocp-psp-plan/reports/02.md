## Reference vs candidate

Slide 2 lists the Partner Strategy & Programs org across two pods: "Partner Experience (PX) Pod" on the left and "Incentives & Investments Pod" on the right, each a column of name/photo rectangles paired with a bulleted responsibilities list. The reference (LibreOffice) draws every bullet with its "•" glyph and hanging indent, which pushes the PX-pod lists into extra wrapped lines that visibly overlap the pod header, the dashed row separators, and the "MACH – xxx Lead" captions above/below. The candidate (BetterOffice) drops the bullet glyphs and the reserved hanging-indent space entirely, so the same text wraps into fewer/longer lines and the overlap most of the reference shows is not reproduced; it also renders the pod headers non-bold and the Incentives-pod role subtitles non-italic where the XML asks for both.

## Findings

### 1. Bullet glyphs and hanging indent missing on every responsibilities list

- Where: PX pod bullet text boxes (ids 43, 44, 45, 95, 96, region ≈ [0.206, 0.302, 0.468, 0.715]) and Incentives pod bullet text boxes (ids 128, 129, 130, 131, region ≈ [0.664, 0.311, 1.011, 0.784]).
- Reference: each paragraph starts with a "•" bullet at the left margin, with the wrapped continuation lines indented under the first character (hanging indent from `marL`/`indent`).
- Candidate: no bullet character is drawn anywhere in either pod, and the text starts flush at the paragraph's `marL=0` edge instead of being indented past the bullet position; because the reserved bullet column disappears, lines fit more characters and the text boxes wrap into fewer lines than the reference.
- XML:
```
<a:pPr marL="171450" indent="-171450" ...>
  <a:buFont typeface="Arial" .../>
  <a:buChar char="&#8226;"/>
</a:pPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `buChar` explicitly requests a bullet glyph and the negative `indent` explicitly reserves a hanging-indent column for it; BetterOffice renders as if the paragraph had `buNone` and `indent="0"`, which changes both the visual bullet list and the line-wrap geometry for every one of these nine text boxes.

### 2. Pod section headers lose their bold weight

- Where: "Partner Experience (PX) Pod" header (id 75) and "Incentives & Investments Pod" header (id 76), region [0.019, 0.267, 0.992, 0.31].
- Reference: both headers render in a heavy/bold weight, clearly distinct from the body text below.
- Candidate: both headers render in the same regular weight as the body text; only the larger 14pt size distinguishes them.
- XML:
```
<a:rPr sz="1400" b="1" i="0" ...>
  <a:latin typeface="Segoe UI"/>
</a:rPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `b="1"` is set on the run; nothing in the layout/master/theme overrides it. The candidate should draw the header text with a bold weight (synthesized if the exact bold face isn't available), matching the reference.

### 3. Incentives-pod role subtitles lose italic styling

- Where: the second line under each Incentives-pod name — "Incentives/Investment Strategy Lead" (id 107), "Enterprise & Cloud Incentive Lead" (id 108), "SMB, CSP & Local Accelerator Lead" (id 115), "Partner Investment Engine (PIE) Lead" (id 116); region [0.485, 0.318, 0.66, 0.778].
- Reference: these role lines render in italic.
- Candidate: the same lines render upright (roman).
- XML:
```
<a:rPr sz="1000" b="0" i="1" ...>
  <a:latin typeface="Segoe UI"/>
</a:rPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `i="1"` is explicit on every run in these paragraphs; the equivalent PX-pod name boxes (e.g. id 77) use `i="0"` throughout and render correctly upright in both engines, so the loss is specific to italic runs, not a font-substitution side effect.

## Not reported

- JPEG headshot photos show minor compression/resampling differences between engines — not a rendering bug.
- Hairline gray borders (`ln w="6350"`) around each pod panel differ by roughly a pixel in weight/antialiasing.
- Overall text baseline/kerning shifts of a pixel or two from font-metric rounding, independent of the bullet/indent issue above.
