## Reference vs candidate

Slide 10 ("Vendor Support") has a title and a single 6-column, 3-row table listing vendor contract details (Program, Vendor Name, Vendor Company/Supplier, Roles & Responsibilities, Contract Dates Needed, Cost per Month). LibreOffice renders the full table: a dark navy header row with white bold text, and two data rows with the P-Seller vendor entries (Beth Harvey/Mactus Group, Anny Boupathong/Aegis) and their costs. BetterOffice renders the title correctly but draws only an empty dashed rectangle with the literal caption "Table" in its place — none of the header fill, borders, or cell text appear.
