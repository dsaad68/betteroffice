## Reference vs candidate

Slide 13 is a "Partner Questionnaire" slide: a dark header bar with a title and a thin white accent line, a blue "Selling" sidebar, and a white panel holding nine numbered questions over a light-gray card. LibreOffice renders bold title/sidebar text, a visible white accent line under the title, and a cleanly numbered 1-9 list occupying the full card height. BetterOffice renders the same layout and colors but drops the bold weight on the title and "Selling", omits the accent line entirely, drops all nine list numbers, and — because one paragraph wraps to one fewer line — the whole list block sits noticeably lower in its card than the reference.

## Findings

### 1. Auto-numbered list bullets not drawn

- Where: Rectangle 7 (id 8), the light-gray card, region `[0.253, 0.187, 0.979, 0.966]`
- Reference: Each of the nine paragraphs is prefixed with "1." through "9." in the theme major font, in the hanging-indent gutter left of the text.
- Candidate: No bullet glyph is drawn for any paragraph; the hanging-indent gutter is left blank and the text starts at the same `marL` position it would have followed the number.
- XML:
```xml
<a:pPr marL="514350" indent="-514350" fontAlgn="ctr">
  <a:buFont typeface="+mj-lt"/>
  <a:buAutoNum type="arabicPeriod"/>
</a:pPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `buAutoNum type="arabicPeriod"` is explicit on every paragraph in the shape; the numbering is required content, not decoration.

### 2. Bold weight ignored on two text runs

- Where: Rectangle 2 (id 3, title "Partner Questionnaire"), region `[0.022, 0.053, 0.979, 0.139]`; Rectangle 5 (id 6, "Selling"), region `[0.0, 0.175, 0.227, 0.982]`
- Reference: Both strings render in a heavy/bold weight.
- Candidate: Both render at a visibly lighter, regular weight; letterforms otherwise match (same face, same width), so this is a weight-selection issue rather than a font-substitution issue.
- XML: `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" spc="-100">...</a:rPr>` (title, id 3); `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2800" b="1">...</a:rPr>` (Selling, id 6)
- Why the candidate is wrong: `b="1"` is explicit on both runs. Neither run specifies an `a:latin` typeface, so both fall back to the theme minor font ("Segoe UI"); the candidate is not honoring the bold flag for the fallback face (no bold face selected, no synthetic/faux bold applied).

### 3. Title accent line missing

- Where: Straight Connector 3 (id 4), region `[0.023, 0.036, 0.253, 0.036]`
- Reference: A short, thick white horizontal line sits just above "Partner Questionnaire" in the header bar.
- Candidate: No line is drawn; the header bar is empty at that position.
- XML:
```xml
<a:ln w="57150" cap="rnd" cmpd="sng" algn="ctr">
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:solidFill>
  <a:prstDash val="solid"/>
</a:ln>
```
with `<a:ext cx="2797810" cy="0"/>` (a straight, zero-height connector — not a degenerate/invisible shape).
- Why the candidate is wrong: the connector has a non-zero width, an explicit 4.5pt white solid line, and no `noFill`/hidden flag; it should be visible.

### 4. List wraps one line shorter, shifting the whole block down

- Where: Rectangle 7 (id 8), paragraph 8, region `[0.253, 0.187, 0.979, 0.966]`
- Reference: "e.g. some established role or email, local geo based contact list, etc." wraps after "con-" onto a second line ("etc."), for 18 total wrapped lines in the shape.
- Candidate: The same paragraph fits "e.g. some established role or email, local geo based contact list, etc." on one line, for 17 total wrapped lines.
- XML: `<a:bodyPr wrap="square" ... anchor="ctr"><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>`; run `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2000"/>` for the paragraph 8 text.
- Why the candidate is wrong: measured directly from the renders, LibreOffice's first line of text starts at y=133px and the candidate's at y=159px (both out of a 720px-tall slide, same shape geometry), a ~53px/7% downward shift fully explained by the one-fewer wrapped line combined with `anchor="ctr"` vertical centering. The line-breaking of paragraph 8 differs by a small amount of available width or character measurement; since the rest of the paragraphs wrap identically, the direction of the fault (LibreOffice vs BetterOffice) is not settled by the XML alone.
