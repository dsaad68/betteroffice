## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a four-column "Workstreams" table: a navy title bar with a white rule and bold white "Workstreams" heading, above four columns (Playbook, Conversation Guides, Partner Solutions, Video), each with a bold white label on a blue header, an icon, a dark-navy body paragraph in white text, and a dark-navy footer caption. LibreOffice renders all of that as specified — bold white title and headers, fully white body text, white rule under the title. BetterOffice renders the title and all four header labels in a thin, muddy dark-gray weight instead of bold white, renders most body-paragraph text in the same dark gray instead of white (leaving it barely legible against the navy boxes), drops the white rule above the title entirely, and in two body boxes mishandles one run's explicit white color.

## Findings

### 1. Scheme-color text fills resolve to theme dk1 instead of the requested color

- Where: title "Workstreams" (id 33), the four header labels "Playbook"/"Conversation Guides"/"Partner Solutions"/"Video" (ids 16, 4, 5, 26), and the four body paragraphs (ids 18, 30, 32, 25).
- Reference: title and header labels are pure white (#FFFFFF) on navy/blue; body paragraphs are pure white on navy.
- Candidate: all of the above render in a flat gray. Sampling the candidate PNG confirms the exact color is `rgb(80,80,80)` = `#505050`.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- title, id 33: explicit scheme color -->
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" spc="-100"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill>...
  <!-- header, id 16: no run fill, relies on shape style -->
  <p:style><a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef></p:style>
  <!-- theme.xml -->
  <a:clrScheme><a:dk1><a:srgbClr val="505050"/></a:dk1><a:lt1><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:lt1>...
  <!-- master.xml -->
  <p:clrMap bg1="lt1" tx1="dk1" .../>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `bg1` maps through the master's `clrMap` to `lt1`, and the theme's `lt1` is `#FFFFFF`; the body/header shapes' own `p:style/a:fontRef` likewise names `lt1`. `#505050` is exactly the theme's `dk1`/`tx1` value, so BetterOffice is substituting the "dark text" scheme color for whatever scheme color was actually requested. Runs with a literal `a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"` (e.g. the footer captions "15-20 page doc", "3-4 minute video") render correctly white, so the bug is specific to `schemeClr`-based fills (explicit or inherited via `fontRef`), not solid fills in general.

### 2. Bold not applied to title and column headers

- Where: title "Workstreams" (id 33) and the four header labels (ids 16, 4, 5, 26).
- Reference: heavy bold weight for all five text runs.
- Candidate: regular/thin weight; no visible boldening.
- XML: `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" .../>` (title, no `latin` override) and `<a:rPr lang="en-US" b="1"><a:latin typeface="Century" .../></a:rPr>` (headers). The title has no font-family override, ruling out a Century-specific fallback as the sole cause — `b="1"` itself is being dropped.
- Why the candidate is wrong: `b="1"` is explicit on every one of these runs; nothing in the layout/master overrides it back to non-bold.

### 3. Title rule connector not drawn

- Where: "Straight Connector 35" (id 36), a horizontal line above the title, off `(285275, 248161)` ext `(2797810, 0)`.
- Reference: a thick (4.5pt) white horizontal rule spanning the left ~25% of the slide, just above "Workstreams".
- Candidate: no line is drawn; the navy title bar is empty above the heading.
- XML: `<p:cxnSp>...<a:ln w="57150" cap="rnd" cmpd="sng" algn="ctr"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:solidFill><a:prstDash val="solid"/></a:ln></p:spPr></p:cxnSp>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the connector has an explicit non-zero line width and solid white fill; there is no condition in the XML that would hide it.

### 4. Run-level white fill applied to the wrong text (or dropped)

- Where: body paragraphs of "Rectangle 17" (id 18, Playbook) and "Rectangle 29" (id 30, Conversation Guides).
- Reference: uniformly white paragraph text (aside from finding 1's global default-color bug, LibreOffice shows no visible run boundary here since both the default and the override resolve to white).
- Candidate: in id 30, the words "each retail, finserv, and" render bright white while the actual explicitly-colored run "One for each " does not (its own leading words "One for" stay in the finding-1 gray); the words "retail, finserv, and" that follow it — which carry no color override in the XML — pick up the white instead. In id 18, the equivalent explicit-white run "Covers " does not render white at all; every word in that paragraph is gray.
- XML (id 30):
  ```xml
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1500"/><a:t>...messaging creation. </a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1600" kern="0"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:solidFill></a:rPr><a:t>One for each </a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1600"/><a:t>retail, </a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1600" err="1"/><a:t>finserv</a:t></a:r>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1600"/><a:t>, and state &amp; local.</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the `a:solidFill` of `FFFFFF` is scoped to the run containing the text "One for each "; nothing in the XML attaches that fill to "retail, finserv, and" or omits it from "Covers " in the sibling shape with the identical pattern.
