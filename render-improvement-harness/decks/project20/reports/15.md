## Reference vs candidate

Slide 15 is a section-divider "VIDEO" card: a navy square (from the layout, with a laptop/film-strip icon and a faint mountain-texture watermark) on the left and a large "VIDEO" title on the right. LibreOffice renders the title bold, 66pt, in the deck's navy brand color (`24265D`), and lets the layout's semi-transparent navy rectangle show a faint grayscale texture underneath. BetterOffice renders the same title small, regular-weight, and in a flat gray, and renders the navy square as a fully opaque flat fill with no texture showing through.

## Findings

### 1. "VIDEO" text is small, unbold, and the wrong color

- Where: Text Placeholder 4 (id 5, placeholder `type="body" sz="quarter" idx="10"`), region roughly `[0.401, 0.447, 0.948, 0.553]` (box inherited from the layout).
- Reference: bold navy (`rgb(36,38,93)` = `24265D`) text, measured glyph height ≈73px in the 1280×720 render (font size 66pt).
- Candidate: thin gray (`rgb(80,80,80)`) text, measured glyph height ≈37px (roughly half), not bold.
- XML: the slide run (`<a:r><a:rPr lang="en-IN"/><a:t>VIDEO</a:t></a:r>`) carries no direct formatting, so it must inherit from the layout placeholder with the same `idx="10"`:
  ```
  <!-- slideLayout7.xml, placeholder idx="10" -->
  <a:lvl1pPr marL="0" indent="0" algn="l">
    <a:defRPr sz="6600" b="1">
      <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"/></a:solidFill>
      <a:latin typeface="+mn-lt"/>
    </a:defRPr>
  </a:lvl1pPr>
  ```
  Instead the rendered text matches the master's generic `p:bodyStyle` fallback almost exactly:
  ```
  <!-- slideMaster1.xml, p:bodyStyle lvl1pPr -->
  <a:defRPr sz="3921" ...><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:solidFill></a:defRPr>
  ```
  `tx1` maps to `dk1` = `505050` = `rgb(80,80,80)`, exactly the sampled candidate color, and 39.21pt is close to the candidate's roughly-halved glyph height relative to the reference's 66pt.
- Why the candidate is wrong: per the placeholder-inheritance chain (slide → layout placeholder → master placeholder → master `txStyles`), a layout placeholder's own `lstStyle` override must win over the master's generic body style. BetterOffice is skipping the layout-level override for this placeholder and falling straight through to the master default, losing the size, weight, and color the deck author set.

### 2. Navy square's alpha fill hides its texture picture

- Where: layout shapes `Rectangle 6` (id 7, the navy fill) stacked over `Picture 5` (id 6, a bi-level-thresholded texture image), region roughly `[0.0, 0.184, 0.355, 0.816]`.
- Reference: the navy square shows a faint grayscale mountain-silhouette texture bleeding through, especially visible bottom-left. Sampling a flat area gives `rgb(42,44,97)` and a textured area gives `rgb(35,37,90)` — both consistent with `24265D` blended at 97% opacity over the white-ish/dark picture beneath (0.97×36+0.03×255 ≈ 42, etc.).
- Candidate: uniformly flat `rgb(36,38,93)` everywhere in the square — exactly the raw `24265D` fill with no blending and no texture visible.
- XML:
  ```
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"><a:alpha val="97000"/></a:srgbClr></a:solidFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's own fill specifies `alpha val="97000"` (97% opacity), which should let the `Picture 5` biLevel texture underneath show through slightly. BetterOffice paints the rectangle fully opaque, ignoring the alpha component of the `srgbClr` fill.
