## Reference vs candidate

Slide 4 is a "Workstream Tracker" timeline: a title bar, a row of overlapping blue chevron/pentagon banners giving week-ending dates, a status row with two diamond milestones joined by a dashed arrow, a second row of dark-navy chevron/pentagon banners naming workstream phases, and eight date columns of bulleted phase details below. LibreOffice renders a continuous, seamlessly-overlapping chevron ribbon with bold white date labels, a white rule under the bold title, dashed connector lines running from each phase banner's tip down to its diamond, soft drop shadows on every diamond, and properly indented, bulleted phase-detail text. BetterOffice renders the same layout and colors in broad strokes, but drops every straight/arrow connector shape outright, draws the top chevron/pentagon row with exaggerated notches that leave large gaps at each seam, colors that row's date labels dark gray instead of white, loses bold weight everywhere it is requested, strips bullets/indent/spacing from the phase-detail lists, and omits the diamonds' drop shadows.

## Findings

### 1. Connector shapes (cxnSp) never rendered

- Where: `Straight Connector 41` (id 42, the white rule under the title, region [0.023, 0.036, 0.253, 0.036]); `Straight Arrow Connector 87` (id 88, the green dashed status arrow, region [0.279, 0.439, 0.365, 0.439]); four more `Straight Connector` shapes (ids 5, 63, 64, 65) forming the dotted vertical leaders from each phase banner's point down to its diamond, e.g. region [0.875, 0.552, 0.875, 0.605].
- Reference: LibreOffice draws a heavy white rule above "Workstream Tracker", a green dotted arrow between the two status diamonds, and thin dotted vertical lines connecting each banner tip to the diamond beneath it.
- Candidate: none of these six shapes produce any visible mark; the title has no rule, the status row shows only the two diamonds with nothing between them, and the diamonds under "June 7"/"June 15" etc. float with no leader line to the banner above.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cxnSp><p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="285275" y="248161"/><a:ext cx="2797810" cy="0"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="line"/><a:ln w="57150"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:solidFill></a:ln>
  </p:spPr></p:cxnSp>
  <p:cxnSp><p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="3403039" y="3013144"/><a:ext cx="1048531" cy="0"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="straightConnector1"/>
    <a:ln w="28575"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="00B050"/></a:solidFill><a:prstDash val="sysDot"/><a:tailEnd type="triangle"/></a:ln>
  </p:spPr></p:cxnSp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both shapes carry explicit, fully-specified line properties (width, color/dash, arrowheads) and non-zero extents; there is no `noFill`/hidden flag that would justify omitting them. All six `p:cxnSp` instances on this slide are missing, indicating the shape kind itself is not drawn.

### 2. Chevron/homePlate notch depth is exaggerated, breaking the overlapping-ribbon seam

- Where: `Arrow: Pentagon 60` (id 61, "April 27") and `Arrow: Chevron 47`…`Arrow: Chevron 52`/`Arrow: Chevron 43` (ids 48, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 44, "May 4" through "June 15"), region [0.057, 0.258, 0.994, 0.336].
- Reference: each shape's bounding box overlaps the next by design (e.g. id 61 right edge at x=2331245 EMU, id 48 left edge at x=2097392 EMU), so the point of one shape tucks exactly into the notch of the next, producing one continuous blue ribbon with only a hairline white seam at each joint.
- Candidate: the point/notch geometry recedes much further from the shape's outer edge than in the reference, so instead of a hairline seam each boundary shows a large white bowtie-shaped gap exposing the slide background — clearly visible at every one of the six joints in the row.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:prstGeom prst="chevron"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom>                          <!-- id 48, default adj -->
  <a:prstGeom prst="homePlate"><a:avLst><a:gd name="adj" fmla="val 47414"/></a:avLst></a:prstGeom>  <!-- id 61 -->
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both the default chevron `adj` (50000) and the pentagon's explicit `adj` (47414, close to its own 50000 default) call for a moderate, roughly-centered notch depth — not the deep, gap-producing point seen in the candidate. The defect is uniform across every shape in the row regardless of `adj` value, so the preset's guide formula/point placement, not the data, looks off.

### 3. Style fontRef color (white) not inherited by runs on the top week-ending row

- Where: same shapes as finding 2 — `Arrow: Pentagon 60` (id 61) and `Arrow: Chevron 47`…`52`/`43` (ids 48–53, 44), region [0.057, 0.258, 0.994, 0.336].
- Reference: text renders solid white, matching the shape's `p:style/a:fontRef` (`schemeClr val="lt1"` → theme `lt1` = `FFFFFF`) since the runs set no explicit color of their own.
- Candidate: pixel sampling inside the "April 27" glyphs returns `(80, 80, 80)` (`#505050`), which is the theme's `dk1`/`tx1` default text color, not `lt1`. Against the `tx2`→`dk2` (`#0072C6`) chevron fill this produces very low contrast. The same shapes' fill color (also a `schemeClr`, `tx2`) resolves correctly, isolating the bug to font-color inheritance from `p:style`.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:style>...<a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef></p:style>
  <p:txBody>...<a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1200" b="1"/><a:t>May 4</a:t></a:r></p:txBody>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: with no run-level `a:solidFill`, OOXML text color must fall back to the shape's `p:style/a:fontRef` scheme color (`lt1`, white); BetterOffice instead falls back to the theme's plain default text color. Elsewhere on the slide (e.g. `Arrow: Pentagon 45`/id 46, "Content Aggregation") the identical `fontRef`-only pattern renders correctly white — the difference is that all the broken shapes' runs also carry `b="1"`, suggesting the bold+no-explicit-color combination specifically skips fontRef resolution.

### 4. Bold weight (b="1") not applied

- Where: `Rectangle 40` (id 41, "Workstream Tracker" title, region [0.022, 0.053, 0.979, 0.139]); `Rectangle 104` (id 105, "May 10" heading) and the equivalent date headings in every other phase column; `Arrow: Pentagon 60`/`Arrow: Chevron 47`–`52`/`43` (ids 61, 48–53, 44).
- Reference: all of the above render in a clearly heavier, bold stroke weight.
- Candidate: every one of these runs renders at the thinner, regular weight despite an explicit `b="1"`. This is visible independent of the color bug (finding 3) — the title has an explicit, correctly-resolved white color yet is still not bold.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" spc="-100"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr><a:t>Workstream Tracker</a:t>
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1100" b="1"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr><a:t>May 10</a:t>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `b="1"` is an explicit, unambiguous request for bold; BetterOffice ignores it on every run sampled, on shapes both with and without an explicit run color.

### 5. Bullet glyph, hanging indent, and paragraph spacing dropped from phase-detail lists

- Where: `Rectangle 104` (id 105, "May 10" column, region [0.275, 0.622, 0.381, 0.947]) and the matching bulleted text boxes under every other date (May 11, May 23, May 27, etc.).
- Reference: each detail line is a separate bulleted paragraph — "•" glyph, hanging indent (text wraps aligned under the first word, not under the bullet), and a visible gap between bullet items.
- Candidate: no bullet glyphs are drawn, the hanging indent collapses so wrapped lines start flush with the bullet position, and the gap between paragraphs disappears — "All Content reviewed and rationalized" runs straight into "Gaps identified and shared with corp to close" as one continuous block.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:pPr marL="171450" indent="-117475">
    <a:spcBef><a:spcPts val="200"/></a:spcBef><a:spcAft><a:spcPts val="300"/></a:spcAft>
    <a:buFont typeface="Arial" pitchFamily="34" charset="0"/><a:buChar char="•"/>
  </a:pPr><a:r><a:t>All Content reviewed and rationalized</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `marL`/`indent` and `buChar` are explicit paragraph properties requesting a hanging bullet; `spcBef`/`spcAft` explicitly request inter-paragraph spacing. None of the three are honored, and the effect compounds — with no separation, the two bullet paragraphs read as one paragraph.

### 6. Diamond marker drop shadow missing

- Where: `Diamond 85` (id 86) and `Diamond 86` (id 87) in the status row (region [0.278, 0.383, 0.297, 0.417] and [0.346, 0.383, 0.364, 0.417]), plus the phase-row milestone diamonds (ids 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 102, 62, 60).
- Reference: every diamond has a soft, offset drop shadow (down-right, semi-transparent dark).
- Candidate: diamonds render as flat, hard-edged shapes with no shadow.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:effectLst><a:outerShdw blurRad="25400" dist="25400" dir="2700000" algn="tl" rotWithShape="0">
    <a:schemeClr val="bg1"><a:lumMod val="50000"/><a:alpha val="40000"/></a:schemeClr>
  </a:outerShdw></a:effectLst>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `a:effectLst/a:outerShdw` is present and fully specified on every diamond shape; the effect is simply not drawn.
