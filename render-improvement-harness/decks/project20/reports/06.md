## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a section-divider title card: a navy square (from the layout) containing a white book-with-pie-chart pictogram, next to the bold "PLAYBOOK" title. LibreOffice draws the pictogram as a white document icon (with a navy pie chart, two navy tick-mark lines, and two navy dots cut out of it) sitting on a subtly mottled navy background, next to large (66pt), bold, dark-navy (#24265D) "PLAYBOOK" text. BetterOffice draws the square as perfectly flat navy with no pictogram at all, and renders the title as small, thin-weight, gray text positioned differently — none of the layout's placeholder overrides or the group's icon graphic came through.

## Findings

### 1. Title placeholder position and style not inherited from the layout

- Where: Text Placeholder 4 (id 5), type `body` idx `10`. Region [0.401, 0.447, 0.948, 0.553] (from the layout's own xfrm).
- Reference: bold, 66pt, dark navy (#24265D) "PLAYBOOK", positioned starting at roughly x≈0.40 of slide width.
- Candidate: thin/regular weight, visibly smaller size, gray text, positioned further left/lower and not matching the layout's box.
- XML: the slide shape carries no overrides —
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="5" name="Text Placeholder 4"/>...<p:nvPr><p:ph type="body" sz="quarter" idx="10"/></p:nvPr></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr/><p:txBody><a:bodyPr/><a:lstStyle/><a:p><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-IN"/><a:t>PLAYBOOK</a:t></a:r></a:p></p:txBody></p:sp>
  ```
  while the layout placeholder (same type+idx) fully specifies box and run formatting —
  ```xml
  <p:ph type="body" sz="quarter" idx="10" hasCustomPrompt="1"/>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="4884058" y="3065150"/><a:ext cx="6674464" cy="727700"/></a:xfrm></p:spPr>
  ...
  <a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr marL="0" indent="0" algn="l">...<a:defRPr sz="6600" b="1">
    <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"/></a:solidFill><a:latin typeface="+mn-lt"/></a:defRPr></a:lvl1pPr>
  ```
  The candidate's gray color and lighter weight match the slide **master**'s generic `p:bodyStyle` instead (`sz="3921"`, no `b`, `<a:schemeClr val="tx1"/>`, `latin typeface="+mj-lt"`), and `tx1`→`dk1` resolves to `srgbClr 505050` in the theme — exactly the gray seen in the candidate, and `+mj-lt` is "Segoe UI Light", the thinner face seen in the candidate.
- Why the candidate is wrong: per OOXML placeholder resolution, a slide placeholder with empty `<p:spPr/>`/`<a:lstStyle/>` inherits from the matching layout placeholder (same `type`+`idx`) before falling back to the master's `txStyles`. BetterOffice is skipping the layout placeholder's own `lstStyle`/`xfrm` and falling straight through to the master's generic body style.

### 2. Book/pie-chart pictogram not drawn at all

- Where: "Rectangle 83" (id 6, the pictogram outline/pie/lines/dots as one `custGeom`) and "Rectangle 6" (id 7, a thin band directly above it), both children of "Group 3" (id 4). Combined region [0.118, 0.386, 0.215, 0.614].
- Reference: a white document icon silhouette (book cover + header bar) with a navy pie chart, two navy tick-lines, and two navy dots showing through as negative space, sitting inside the navy square.
- Candidate: solid, uniform navy — no white silhouette, no pie chart, nothing. Confirmed by sampling the pictogram's bounding region: it is a single flat color in the candidate render (RGB 36,38,93), identical everywhere.
- XML: both shapes rely entirely on the group for their fill:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="6" name="Rectangle 83"/></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr bwMode="auto"><a:xfrm .../><a:custGeom>...</a:custGeom><a:grpFill/><a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln></p:spPr>
  ```
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="7" name="Rectangle 6"/></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr bwMode="auto"><a:xfrm .../><a:prstGeom prst="rect"/><a:grpFill/><a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln></p:spPr>
  ```
  and the group supplies the fill via `grpSpPr`:
  ```xml
  <p:grpSp>...<p:grpSpPr><a:xfrm .../><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
  ```
  `bg1` resolves through the master's `<p:clrMap bg1="lt1" .../>` to theme `<a:lt1><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:lt1>` — white, matching the reference's silhouette color.
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:grpFill/>` means "use the fill inherited from the parent group," which here is an explicit white `solidFill`. Both shapes are otherwise fully defined (valid geometry, no line), so the only missing piece is resolving `grpFill` against the group's own `solidFill`; without it nothing is painted and the shapes vanish into the background.

### 3. Layout background texture/alpha not composited

- Where: layout-level "Rectangle 6" (id 7, the 97%-opaque navy fill) over layout-level "Picture 5" (id 6, a thresholded "banner" image), both at box [0.0, 0.175, 0.338, 0.775].
- Reference: the navy square has a faint, low-contrast mottled texture in its lower-left/upper-left, and its flat-fill areas sample slightly lighter than the raw hex (RGB 42,44,97 vs pure 36,38,93), consistent with a 97%-navy / 3%-picture blend.
- Candidate: the square samples as exactly the raw `24265D` hex (RGB 36,38,93) everywhere, with zero texture — a fully opaque flat fill.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:pic><p:nvPicPr><p:cNvPr id="6" name="Picture 5" descr="banner"/></p:nvPicPr>
  <p:blipFill><a:blip r:embed="rId2"><a:biLevel thresh="25000"/></a:blip>...</p:blipFill>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="0" y="1262743"/><a:ext cx="4325257" cy="4332514"/></a:xfrm></p:spPr></p:pic>
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="7" name="Rectangle 6"/></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="0" y="1262743"/><a:ext cx="4325257" cy="4332514"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"><a:alpha val="97000"/></a:srgbClr></a:solidFill></p:spPr></p:sp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the pixel evidence points at either (or both) of two spec-mandated behaviors not being applied: the `<a:alpha val="97000"/>` on the navy fill (3% see-through), and/or the `Picture 5` blip underneath it. The candidate's exact match to the raw, unblended hex is the tell. This is a very low-visibility, low-severity cosmetic gap, listed separately from finding 2 because it affects a different (layout-level) shape pair with a different XML cause.
