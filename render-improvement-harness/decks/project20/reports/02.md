## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a section-divider title card: a navy square (from the layout) containing an arc of white dots, next to the bold "PROJECT UPDATE" title. LibreOffice draws the dots as a graduated arc of white circles inside the square and the title as large (66pt), bold, dark-navy (#24265D) text wrapped onto two lines ("PROJECT" / "UPDATE") starting just right of the square. BetterOffice draws the square with its border but no dots at all, and renders the title as small, thin-weight, gray, single-line text sitting further right and lower — none of the layout's placeholder overrides came through.

## Findings

### 1. Title placeholder position, size, and style not inherited from the layout

- Where: Text Placeholder 4 (id 5), type `body` idx `10`. Approximate region [0.401, 0.447, 0.948, 0.553] (from the layout's own xfrm).
- Reference: bold, 66pt, dark navy (#24265D) text, box positioned starting at x≈0.40 of slide width and only ≈0.55 wide, which forces "PROJECT UPDATE" to wrap onto two lines.
- Candidate: thin/regular weight, small (looks like a generic default size), gray text, on a single line, positioned differently and clearly not constrained to the layout's narrow box (no wrap occurs).
- XML: slide has no overriding geometry or style —
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="5" name="Text Placeholder 4"/>...<p:nvPr><p:ph type="body" sz="quarter" idx="10"/></p:nvPr></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr/><p:txBody><a:bodyPr/><a:lstStyle/><a:p><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-IN"/><a:t>PROJECT UPDATE</a:t></a:r></a:p></p:txBody></p:sp>
  ```
  and the layout supplies exactly what the reference shows —
  ```xml
  <p:ph type="body" sz="quarter" idx="10" hasCustomPrompt="1"/>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="4884058" y="3065150"/><a:ext cx="6674464" cy="727700"/></a:xfrm></p:spPr>
  ...
  <a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr marL="0" indent="0" algn="l">...<a:defRPr sz="6600" b="1">
    <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"/></a:solidFill><a:latin typeface="+mn-lt"/></a:defRPr>
  </a:lvl1pPr>...</a:lstStyle>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: per OOXML placeholder resolution, a slide placeholder with an empty `<p:spPr/>`/`<a:lstStyle/>` must inherit geometry and text formatting from the matching layout placeholder (same `type`+`idx`), which here fully specifies box, size, weight, and color. BetterOffice's output matches none of that, and instead looks like an unstyled default text box — the layout-to-slide placeholder chain isn't being resolved for this shape.

### 2. Arc-of-dots freeform shapes not drawn (missing `grpFill` inheritance)

- Where: nine `custGeom` freeform shapes ("Freeform 100" through "Freeform 108", ids 7–15), children of "Group 3" (id 4). Group region ≈ [0.081, 0.382, 0.286, 0.618] inside the navy square.
- Reference: a graduated arc of nine white filled circles (small to large) climbing from lower-left to upper-right inside the square.
- Candidate: the square (drawn by the layout) and its inset border are present, but none of the nine dots are drawn — the square interior is empty.
- XML: each freeform has no direct fill and relies on the group:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="7" name="Freeform 100"/>...
  <p:spPr>...<a:custGeom>...</a:custGeom><a:grpFill/><a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln></p:spPr>
  ```
  and the group supplies the fill via `grpSpPr`:
  ```xml
  <p:grpSp>...<p:grpSpPr><a:xfrm .../><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
  ```
  `bg1` resolves through the master's `<p:clrMap bg1="lt1" .../>` to theme `<a:lt1><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:lt1>` — white, matching the reference dots.
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:grpFill/>` means "use the fill inherited from the parent group," which here is an explicit white `solidFill`. The shapes are otherwise fully defined (valid `custGeom` paths, no line), so the only missing piece is resolving `grpFill` against the group's own `solidFill`; without it the shapes presumably fall back to no fill and vanish.

- Slight sub-pixel offset of the square's drop-shadow-like gray bar to its right, consistent with normal rasterization variance.
