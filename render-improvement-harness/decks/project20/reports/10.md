## Reference vs candidate

Slide 10 is a section-divider slide: a navy square with a white "team" icon on the left, a bold two-line "PARTNER SOLUTIONS" title on the right. LibreOffice renders the title large, bold, dark navy, wrapped across two lines, and shows a faint mottled texture inside the navy square. BetterOffice renders the title as small, regular-weight, gray text on a single line, and the navy square is a flat, slightly darker solid color with no visible texture.

## Findings

### 1. Title text uses the wrong style (size, weight, color, wrap)

- Where: "Text Placeholder 4" (id 5, `type="body" idx="10"`), region `[0.401, 0.447, 0.948, 0.553]` (box from the layout, slide has no `spPr` override)
- Reference: bold, ~66pt, color `#24265D` (dark navy), left-aligned, wraps to two lines ("PARTNER" / "SOLUTIONS") that overflow the shape's small height above and below since `noAutofit` is set
- Candidate: regular weight, visibly smaller (looks close to the master default of 39.21pt), gray (`tx1`-ish), single line "PARTNER SOLUTIONS" that fits inside the box without wrapping
- XML: slide10.xml's own run has no `rPr` overrides (`<a:rPr lang="en-IN"/>`) and no `lstStyle`, so everything must come from the placeholder's inheritance chain. The layout (`slideLayout7.xml`) defines the *same* placeholder (`type="body" idx="10"`, shape "Text Placeholder 11") with its own `<a:lstStyle>`:
  ```xml
  <a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr marL="0" indent="0" algn="l">
    <a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="100000"/></a:lnSpc><a:buNone/>
    <a:defRPr sz="6600" b="1">
      <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"/></a:solidFill>
      <a:latin typeface="+mn-lt"/>
    </a:defRPr>
  </a:lvl1pPr></a:lstStyle>
  ```
  The master's `p:bodyStyle` lvl1 (`sz="3921"`, no `b`, `<a:schemeClr val="tx1"/>`, `marL="336145" indent="-336145" algn="l"`) is what the candidate's output matches instead.
- Why the candidate is wrong: per the placeholder-inheritance rules, a placeholder's own `lstStyle`/`rPr` on the *slide* is checked first, then the matching placeholder shape's `lstStyle` in the *layout*, and only then the master `txStyles`. The layout here defines a full `lvl1pPr` override (size, bold, color, margins, bullet suppression) for exactly this placeholder idx, so it must win over the master body default. The candidate is skipping the layout-level override entirely and falling through to the master default, which also explains the single-line wrap (39pt text fits on one line at this box width where 66pt bold does not).

### 2. Navy square fill ignores its 97% alpha, hiding the underlying texture

- Where: "Rectangle 6", a `userDrawn` background shape defined in `slideLayout7.xml` (id 7), region `[0.0, 0.184, 0.355, 0.816]` — sits directly behind the "Graphic 15" team-icon picture
- Reference: solid navy sampled at `(400,500)` and `(440,300)` in `lo-img/10.png` reads RGB `(42, 44, 97)`, and a faint mottled/marbled texture is visible through the fill (strongest bottom-left)
- Candidate: same pixels in `bo-img/10.png` read RGB `(36, 38, 93)` — flat, no texture visible anywhere in the square
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="24265D"><a:alpha val="97000"/></a:srgbClr></a:solidFill>
  ```
  `24265D` = RGB(36,38,93) exactly matches the candidate's flat color. Compositing that same color at 97% opacity over the white slide background (and the `biLevel`-filtered "Picture 5" banner sitting underneath at `z`-order before this rectangle) gives `0.97*36+0.03*255 ≈ 43`, `0.97*38+0.03*255 ≈ 45`, `0.97*93+0.03*255 ≈ 98` — matching the reference's `(42, 44, 97)` almost exactly.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's fill color carries an explicit `<a:alpha val="97000"/>` child that the candidate drops, painting the `srgbClr` fully opaque. That both shifts the perceived color and fully occludes the grayscale banner picture ("Picture 5", same size/position, `<a:biLevel thresh="25000"/>`) that is supposed to show through the 3% transparency as the mottled texture.
