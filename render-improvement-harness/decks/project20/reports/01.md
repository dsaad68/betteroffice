## Reference vs candidate

Slide 1 is the deck's title slide: a full-bleed mountain-photo banner (from the slide layout) tinted by three translucent overlay rectangles, with "Partnership" / "Project Workstreams, Strategy, and Execution Plan" / "May 8, 2018" placeholders on the left, the Frontpoint Strategy logo picture top-center, and a red sticky-note rectangle with white instructional text on the right. LibreOffice renders the photo through the tinted overlays, all title text in place, and white instructional text on the red box. BetterOffice renders the three overlay rectangles fully opaque (hiding the photo entirely, leaving flat dark-gray/light-gray fields), the instructional text in dark gray instead of white, and truncates the "Project Workstreams..." placeholder after two lines, dropping the rest of its text.

## Findings

### 1. Overlay rectangle alpha/opacity ignored, hiding the layout's banner picture

- Where: `Rectangle 13` (id 14, x 0.0–0.056, full height), `Rectangle 1` (id 2, x 0.426–1.0, full height) — both in the layout, fill `srgbClr val="353535"` with `alpha val="30196"` (~30%); `Rectangle 4` (id 5, x 0.056–0.426, full height) — fill `schemeClr val="bg1"` with `lumMod val="95000"` and `alpha val="80000"` (80%). Together the three rectangles tile the full slide width.
- Reference: LibreOffice blends each rectangle's color at its stated alpha over the full-bleed banner picture (`Picture 2`, `image15.jpeg`, the mountain photo) inherited from `slideLayout26.xml`, producing a dark-tinted photo on the right, a light frosted-glass panel in the middle, and a thin dark strip on the left.
- Candidate: BetterOffice paints all three rectangles at full opacity. Pixel sampling confirms this exactly: BO pixels under `Rectangle 13`/`Rectangle 1` read `(53, 53, 53)` — exactly unblended `#353535` — at every sampled point (e.g. (30,50), (900,100), (700,600)), and BO pixels under `Rectangle 4` read `(242, 242, 242)` — exactly unblended `bg1 × 0.95` — at every sampled point (e.g. (200,50), (200,650), (500,650)). LibreOffice's corresponding pixels vary with the photo underneath (e.g. `(109,136,158)`, `(148,149,153)`), confirming the reference blends while the candidate does not. Since the three rectangles cover the entire slide width, this single bug fully occludes the banner picture and dominates the diff (`hot_cells` r1c3 97.4%, r2c3 95.6%, r3c4 94.3%).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="353535"><a:alpha val="30196"/></a:srgbClr></a:solidFill>
  <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"><a:lumMod val="95000"/><a:alpha val="80000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both fills declare an explicit `a:alpha` child on the color; BetterOffice's rendered pixels are the raw, unmodified color at 100% opacity, so the alpha modifier is being dropped during fill resolution.

### 2. Run-level gradFill (solid white) resolves to gray text

- Where: `Rectangle 1` (id 2, slide, the red "Please work on noted slides only. / Use existing template please" box), region [0.843, 0.082, 1.103, 0.475].
- Reference: LibreOffice renders both lines of text in solid white, matching a two-stop gradient whose stops are both `FFFFFF` (a degenerate, effectively-solid-white gradient) and also matching the shape style's `fontRef` (`schemeClr val="lt1"`, white).
- Candidate: BetterOffice renders the text in flat gray. Sampling a stroke of the "P" in "Please" gives `(80, 80, 80)` (`#505050`), not white.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2400">
    <a:gradFill><a:gsLst>
      <a:gs pos="0"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
      <a:gs pos="100000"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
    </a:gsLst><a:lin ang="5400000" scaled="0"/></a:gradFill>
  </a:rPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the run explicitly sets a `gradFill` whose two stops are identical (`FFFFFF`), which must render as solid white regardless of gradient angle; BetterOffice instead falls back to an unrelated default gray, meaning `gradFill` on run text properties is not being evaluated (or not falling back to a sane approximation when unsupported).

### 3. Placeholder text clipped at box bottom instead of overflowing

- Where: `Text Placeholder 3` (id 4, slide, placeholder idx 11), nominal box off `(1280159, 3260028)` ext `(3682365, 1319656)` EMU → frac [0.105, 0.475, 0.407, 0.668].
- Reference: LibreOffice lays out all three paragraphs — "Project Workstreams, Strategy, and Execution Plan", a blank line, and "May 8, 2018" — wrapping to 4 lines that visibly extend below the shape's nominal bottom edge (y≈0.668) down to roughly y≈0.81, since the shape's `bodyPr` has no `normAutofit`/`spAutoFit` and no `vertOverflow="clip"`.
- Candidate: BetterOffice renders only "Project" and "Workstreams," (2 wrapped lines) and stops exactly at the shape's nominal bottom edge; "Strategy, and Execution Plan", the blank paragraph, and "May 8, 2018" are never drawn.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- layout, inherited: slide's own <p:txBody><a:bodyPr/> is empty -->
  <a:bodyPr lIns="91440" anchor="t"><a:noAutofit/></a:bodyPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `noAutofit` plus the default `vertOverflow` value (`overflow`, not `clip`) means text that exceeds the shape's height must spill below it, not be clipped. BetterOffice appears to bound text layout/painting to the shape's declared extent instead of allowing vertical overflow.
