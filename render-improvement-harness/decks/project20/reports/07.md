## Reference vs candidate

Slide 7 is a two-column "Playbook Outline" navigation slide: a dark title bar with a white rule, a "Full" outline (a long hyperlinked topic tree) on the left flowing into a "Simplified" outline on the right via a blue arrow, a left rail of four category labels (three with line-art icons), and a red instructional callout bleeding off the right edge. LibreOffice draws the title bold with its underline rule, gray-blue hyperlinked outline text, bold "Comprehensive Across" with an underline and three icons (money/dollar, shopping bag, government building) beside the category labels, a tall navy divider line, and white instructional text on the red callout. BetterOffice is missing the white title rule, the "Comprehensive Across" underline, the vertical divider, and all three category icons; it renders the title and "Comprehensive Across" unbolded; it renders the red callout's text in dark gray instead of white; and it renders the outline hyperlinks in dark gray rather than LibreOffice's blue.

## Findings

### 1. Degenerate straight connectors (zero-height/zero-width lines) not drawn

- Where: `Straight Connector 41` (id 42, frac [0.023, 0.036, 0.253, 0.036], the white rule above "Playbook Outline"), `Straight Connector 18` (id 19, frac [0.012, 0.297, 0.075, 0.297], the underline below "Comprehensive Across"), `Straight Connector 20` (id 21, frac [0.108, 0.158, 0.108, 1.0], the tall navy divider left of the "Full" column).
- Reference: LibreOffice draws all three as visible lines — a thick white rounded-cap rule, a thin dark underline, and a thick navy vertical divider running the full height of the content area.
- Candidate: BetterOffice draws none of them; the title bar has no rule above the text, "Comprehensive Across" has no underline, and there is no vertical divider anywhere in the frame.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cxnSp><p:cNvPr id="42" name="Straight Connector 41"/>
    <a:xfrm><a:off x="285275" y="248161"/><a:ext cx="2797810" cy="0"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="line"/>
    <a:ln w="57150" cap="rnd"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:solidFill></a:ln>
  </p:cxnSp>
  <p:cxnSp><p:cNvPr id="19" name="Straight Connector 18"/>
    <a:xfrm><a:off x="144235" y="2038743"/><a:ext cx="771534" cy="0"/></a:xfrm>
  </p:cxnSp>
  <p:cxnSp><p:cNvPr id="21" name="Straight Connector 20"/>
    <a:xfrm><a:off x="1313234" y="1085553"/><a:ext cx="0" cy="5772447"/></a:xfrm>
  </p:cxnSp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: all three are ordinary `p:cxnSp` straight connectors with `prstGeom prst="line"` and a fully specified `a:ln` (width, fill); the only thing they share is a zero-length dimension on one axis (`cx="0"` or `cy="0"`). BetterOffice fails to render any connector whose bounding box is degenerate on one axis, even though the line geometry itself is well-defined.

### 2. Sidebar category icons (custGeom freeform groups) missing

- Where: `Group 109` (id 110, Financial Services icon, frac [0.021, 0.325, 0.065, 0.381]), `Group 126` (id 127, Retail icon, frac [0.032, 0.521, 0.063, 0.59]), `Group 115` (id 116, Government icon, frac [0.026, 0.718, 0.065, 0.784]).
- Reference: LibreOffice draws a blue-outlined money/dollar-bill icon next to "Financial Services", a shopping-bag-with-globe icon next to "Retail", and a columned-building-with-flag icon next to "Government".
- Candidate: BetterOffice draws nothing at any of the three positions; only the text labels appear.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:grpSp><p:cNvPr id="110" name="Group 109"/>
    <p:sp><p:cNvPr id="113" name="Freeform 18"/>
      <a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst>...</a:gdLst><a:ahLst/><a:cxnLst/><a:rect .../><a:pathLst>...</a:pathLst></a:custGeom>
      ...
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each icon is a `p:grpSp` containing several `custGeom` freeform shapes (the multi-part outline strokes of each glyph); none of the group's children are drawn, so the whole group is silently dropped rather than partially rendered.

### 3. Explicit run-level bold ignored on title and "Comprehensive Across"

- Where: `Rectangle 40` (id 41, "Playbook Outline" title, frac [0.022, 0.053, 0.979, 0.139]); `TextBox 15` (id 16, "Comprehensive Across" label, frac [-0.004, 0.213, 0.12, 0.298]).
- Reference: LibreOffice renders both in a heavy bold weight (`Playbook Outline` at a very thick stroke, and "Comprehensive Across" bold enough that it visibly wraps as "Comprehensiv/e Across").
- Candidate: BetterOffice renders both at plain/regular weight; "Comprehensive Across" consequently wraps differently ("Comprehensive/Across") because the narrower unbolded glyphs fit more per line.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" spc="-100"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill>...</a:rPr>  <!-- title -->
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1050" b="1"><a:gradFill>...</a:gradFill></a:rPr>  <!-- Comprehensive Across -->
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both runs carry an explicit `b="1"`; every other text run on the slide that lacks `b="1"` (e.g. "Full", "Simplified", the category labels) correctly renders unbolded in both engines, isolating this to the bold flag specifically not being honored.

### 4. Run-level gradFill (solid white) resolves to gray on the red callout

- Where: `Rectangle 65` (id 66, the "This is meant to be an outline..." callout), frac [0.841, 0.066, 1.244, 0.872].
- Reference: LibreOffice renders all of the callout's text in solid white against the red fill.
- Candidate: BetterOffice renders the same text in flat dark gray, matching the theme's `dk1`/`tx1` default (`#505050`) rather than white.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2400">
    <a:gradFill><a:gsLst>
      <a:gs pos="0"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
      <a:gs pos="100000"><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:gs>
    </a:gsLst><a:lin ang="5400000" scaled="0"/></a:gradFill>
  </a:rPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the two gradient stops are identical (`FFFFFF`), so the gradient must render as solid white regardless of angle; BetterOffice instead falls back to the default text color, indicating run-level `gradFill` is not evaluated at all. (Same root cause as the `project20/01/2` finding on slide 1.)

### 5. Hyperlink runs: explicit tx1 fill vs LibreOffice's automatic hyperlink color

- Where: every hyperlinked line in `Rectangle 8` (id 11, "Full" outline) and `Rectangle 9` (id 13, "Simplified" outline), e.g. "Executive Summary", "a fiscal year Business Opportunity", "Solution".
- Reference: LibreOffice renders every hyperlinked run in the theme's hyperlink blue (`clrScheme/hlink` = `#0072C6`).
- Candidate: BetterOffice renders the same runs in dark gray, matching `schemeClr val="tx1"` resolved through `clrMap` to `dk1` = `#505050`.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:rPr sz="1100" b="0" u="none"><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:solidFill>...<a:hlinkClick r:id="rId3"/></a:rPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong (tentative): every run has an explicit `<a:solidFill>` set to `tx1`, distinct from and overriding the theme's automatic hyperlink coloring; per the OOXML fill-resolution order, an explicit run-level `solidFill` should take precedence over the implicit hyperlink color. It is plausible the original author added the hyperlink to a run that already had explicit black/gray formatting (a common pattern for outline/TOC slides where links should look like normal body text). Filed as `lo-suspect` rather than a hard bug because real PowerPoint's actual precedence between an explicit run fill and `hlinkClick` auto-coloring was not independently verified here.

## Not reported

- Cumulative vertical drift in the "Full" outline list (`Rectangle 8`, id 11): BetterOffice's list runs slightly taller than LibreOffice's, so later items ("GDPR", "Customers", "Selling") sit a few pixels lower than in the reference. Likely a font-metric/line-height difference from `Calibri` substitution rather than a line-spacing property bug (paragraph `lnSpc` is 100%/explicit `spcPct` values that match between visible runs), so not filed as a discrete finding.
- General glyph-shape and kerning differences between the two engines' font substitutes for `Calibri` / `Segoe UI` / `Segoe UI Semilight`, visible on close inspection of every text block but not tied to a specific XML property.
- Anti-aliasing and sub-pixel edge differences on the blue arrow and on wrapped-text line edges.
