## Reference vs candidate

Slide 16 ("Video") is a two-column outline slide: a dark header band, a blue sidebar with the "Tentative Outline" label and a checklist icon, and a light content panel listing a numbered, partly-bulleted agenda. The LibreOffice reference renders both headings bold, a fully numbered/bulleted list, and a decorative partial-rectangle accent line around the content panel. BetterOffice renders the same text and layout but drops the bold weight on both headings, drops all list numbering and bullet glyphs, draws the accent line as a plain full rectangle, and shifts the sidebar heading text noticeably lower within its box.

## Findings

### 1. Bold headings render as regular weight

- Where: "Video" title (Rectangle 2, id 3, top header band) and "Tentative Outline" (Rectangle 5, id 6, blue sidebar)
- Reference: both render as heavy/bold glyphs.
- Candidate: both render as thin/regular glyphs, visibly lighter-weight than the reference.
- XML: `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" spc="-100">` for "Video"; `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2800" b="1">` for "Tentative Outline".
- Why the candidate is wrong: both runs carry an explicit `b="1"`, so OOXML requires the latin font to be synthesized or resolved bold; BetterOffice ignores the flag and falls back to the regular weight.

### 2. List numbering and bullets are not drawn

- Where: content panel (Rectangle 7, id 8), all 10 paragraphs.
- Reference: level-0 items are prefixed "1.", "2.", "3.", "4." (arabic-period auto-numbering); level-1 items are prefixed with a "•" bullet, each hung at its `marL`/`indent` position.
- Candidate: no numbers and no bullet glyphs anywhere; every line is plain text. The hanging-indent text position itself (`marL`) is correct — only the marker glyph is missing.
- XML: level-0 paragraphs use `<a:buFont typeface="+mj-lt"/><a:buAutoNum type="arabicPeriod"/>`; level-1 paragraphs use `<a:buFont typeface="Arial".../><a:buChar char="•"/>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: both bullet types are explicitly declared per-paragraph (no `buNone`), so PowerPoint semantics require the markers to be rendered; dropping them removes the sequencing information the slide is built around (steps 1–4 with timings).

### 3. Content-panel accent border drawn as a full rectangle

- Where: Freeform: Shape 6 (id 7), the borderless custGeom shape framing the content panel.
- Reference: a subtle partial "L" accent — a short top segment near the top-left, ending in a small oval dot, a line down the left edge, and a line across the bottom ending in another oval dot at the bottom-right — not a full rectangle.
- Candidate: a plain, complete thin rectangle outline around the whole content panel; no oval end-cap dots.
- XML: `<a:path w="11643360" h="3850640"><a:moveTo><a:pt x="1178560" y="0"/></a:moveTo><a:lnTo x="0" y="0"/><a:lnTo x="0" y="3850640"/><a:lnTo x="11643360" y="3850640"/></a:path>` — the path only covers three of four sides and starts 10% in from the top-left corner; `<a:headEnd type="oval".../><a:tailEnd type="oval".../>` add the dots at the path's two open ends.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the path data does not close into a rectangle (it omits the right edge and most of the top edge), so drawing a full rectangle instead of following the explicit point list is a custGeom parsing/drawing bug, not a style choice.

### 4. "Tentative Outline" sits lower than in the reference

- Where: Rectangle 5 (id 6), blue sidebar, the "Tentative Outline" heading.
- Reference: the two wrapped lines ("Tentative" / "Outline") sit in the upper-middle of the tall sidebar box.
- Candidate: the same two lines sit about 40px (~5.5% of slide height) lower, still within the same box.
- XML: the text box has `anchor="ctr"` and contains the "Tentative Outline" paragraph followed by three empty trailing paragraphs with `<a:endParaRPr .../>` at `sz="2800"`, `sz="3600"`, `sz="3600"`.
- Why the candidate is wrong (medium confidence): with vertical centering, the visible text's on-screen position depends on the total height of all paragraphs including the empty trailing ones. If BetterOffice under-computes the line height of an empty paragraph (e.g. ignoring the `endParaRPr` size), the whole block is shorter than it should be, and centering shifts the visible lines further down — consistent with what's observed. This is inferred from the visual shift plus the box's `anchor="ctr"` and trailing empty paragraphs; it isn't as directly provable from the XML as findings 1–3.

## Not reported

- Minor sub-pixel/anti-aliasing differences on all text edges (both renders use different font rasterizers).
- The red "Minor cleanup" annotation box bleeding off the right edge of the slide — identical position and size in both renders; it's an authoring artifact (shape id 10 sits partly outside the slide bounds in the source XML), not a rendering bug.
- Very slight gray-level differences on the light content-panel fill (`bg1` with `lumMod 95000`) between renderers — within normal color-management tolerance.
