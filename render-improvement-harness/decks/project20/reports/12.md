## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a "Partner Questionnaire" section divider: a navy title bar over a blue side panel ("Solution Applicability") and a light-gray content panel listing 11 numbered questions, plus an off-slide red "Minor cleanup" note shape that both engines correctly clip/overlap identically. The reference (LibreOffice) shows the title and side-panel label in bold, a white accent rule above the title, and the 11 questions as a properly numbered, hanging-indented list. The candidate (BetterOffice) renders the same text content but drops the bold weight on the title and side-panel label, omits the white rule above the title entirely, and renders the numbered list as unnumbered, non-indented paragraphs — which also shifts line wrapping and the vertical centering of the block.

## Findings

### 1. Auto-numbered list and hanging indent not applied

- Where: Rectangle 7 (id 8), the light-gray content box, region `[0.253, 0.187, 0.979, 0.966]` (hot cells r2c2, r3c2, r3c3, r4c3 all fall inside this box).
- Reference: 11 paragraphs each prefixed with "1." through "11.", with a hanging indent so wrapped continuation lines align under the text (not the number).
- Candidate: The same 11 paragraphs with no bullet/number glyph and no hanging indent — text starts flush at the box's left inset. Because the reserved marL/indent width is not subtracted, several paragraphs wrap one line shorter than the reference (e.g. the "trigger issues/questions" paragraph is 3 lines in the reference, 2 in the candidate), which also changes where the vertically-centered (`anchor="ctr"`) block lands in the box.
- XML:
```xml
<a:pPr marL="514350" indent="-514350" fontAlgn="ctr">
  <a:buFont typeface="+mj-lt"/>
  <a:buAutoNum type="arabicPeriod"/>
</a:pPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: every one of the 11 paragraphs carries `buAutoNum type="arabicPeriod"` plus a `marL`/negative-`indent` hanging pair; none of that is reflected in the render, so both the numbering glyphs and the layout width they imply are missing.

### 2. Explicit run-level bold ignored

- Where: Rectangle 2 (id 3), the title "Partner Questionnaire", region `[0.022, 0.053, 0.979, 0.139]`; and Rectangle 9 (id 10), the "Solution Applicability" side-panel label, region `[0.0, 0.175, 0.227, 0.982]`.
- Reference: Both strings render in a heavy/bold weight. In "Solution Applicability" the bold text is wide enough that LibreOffice wraps it "Solution" / "Applicabilit" / "y" across three lines.
- Candidate: Both strings render in a regular (non-bold) weight. The now-narrower "Solution Applicability" fits cleanly on two lines ("Solution" / "Applicability") instead of three.
- XML:
```xml
<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="3600" b="1" spc="-100">...<a:t>Partner Questionnaire </a:t></a:rPr>
```
```xml
<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="2800" b="1"><a:solidFill>...</a:solidFill>...</a:rPr><a:t>Solution Applicability</a:t>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both runs set `b="1"` directly on the run properties; bold is a direct, unambiguous instruction independent of which physical font family the "Segoe UI"/"Segoe UI Black" fallback resolves to.

### 3. Missing accent rule above the title

- Where: Straight Connector 3 (id 4), region `[0.023, 0.036, 0.253, 0.036]`.
- Reference: A short horizontal white rule (57150 EMU / ~4.5pt wide) sits above "Partner Questionnaire".
- Candidate: No line is drawn in that region at all; the navy title bar is empty above the text.
- XML:
```xml
<p:cxnSp>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="285275" y="248161"/><a:ext cx="2797810" cy="0"/></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="line"/>
    <a:ln w="57150" cap="rnd"><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:solidFill></a:ln>
  </p:spPr>
</p:cxnSp>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the connector has a non-zero width (`cx="2797810"`), zero height (a horizontal line), an explicit white solid line fill, and no hidden flag — there is nothing in the XML that would suppress it.
