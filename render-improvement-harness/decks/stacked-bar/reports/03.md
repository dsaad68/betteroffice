## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a single embedded bar chart ("INDUSTRY TRENDS") showing four categories, each a horizontal stacked bar of three series (Series 1/2/3), with a bottom-centered legend and a numeric value axis. The reference (LibreOffice) draws the category axis fully spelled out on the left, top-to-bottom "Category 4"→"Category 1" (i.e. Category 1 nearest the origin at the bottom), the value axis with ticks 0–14 (step 2) along the bottom, a bottom-centered horizontal legend, and no data labels. The candidate (BetterOffice) stacks the value-axis ticks (0, 3.1, 6.2, 9.2, 12.3) into the same left-hand column as the category labels — truncating every category label to "Categ" — lists the categories in the opposite vertical order (Category 1 at top, Category 4 at bottom), puts the legend as a vertical list top-right, and shows small numeric labels next to the end of each bar that the chart XML explicitly turns off.

## Findings

### 1. Data labels shown despite `showVal`/`showCatName`/etc. all being 0

- Where: Chart 10 (id 11), small digits immediately right of each bar's final (light-gray) segment, at roughly (0.63, 0.25), (0.64, 0.40), (0.60, 0.54), (0.85, 0.69)
- Reference: no data labels anywhere on the chart.
- Candidate: a one-digit numeric label ("2", "2", "3", "5") appears past the end of every bar.
- XML:
```xml
<c:dLbls>
  <c:showLegendKey val="0"/><c:showVal val="0"/><c:showCatName val="0"/>
  <c:showSerName val="0"/><c:showPercent val="0"/><c:showBubbleSize val="0"/>
</c:dLbls>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: every `show*` flag on the chart-level `c:dLbls` is `0`, so no data label of any kind should be rendered on any series or point.

### 2. Value axis rendered on the left instead of the bottom, truncating category labels

- Where: Chart 10 (id 11), left label column, x∈[0.064, 0.092], y∈[0.14, 0.76]
- Reference: category names ("Category 1"…"Category 4") shown in full on the left; value-axis ticks (0, 2, 4, …, 14) shown once, horizontally, along the bottom.
- Candidate: value-axis tick values (0, 3.1, 6.2, 9.2, 12.3) are interleaved vertically with the category labels in the same left column, and no value ticks appear along the bottom at all; the category text is squeezed to "Categ" because the column no longer has room for it.
- XML:
```xml
<c:catAx><c:axId val="579760832"/>...<c:axPos val="l"/>...<c:crossAx val="579764440"/></c:catAx>
<c:valAx><c:axId val="579764440"/>...<c:axPos val="b"/>...<c:crossAx val="579760832"/></c:valAx>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `c:valAx` has `c:axPos val="b"` — it must be laid out along the bottom edge of the plot area, not stacked into the category axis's left-hand label column.

### 3. Category order reversed top-to-bottom

- Where: Chart 10 (id 11), whole plot area, bar rows at y≈0.25, 0.40, 0.54, 0.69
- Reference: rows top-to-bottom are Category 4 (sum 12.3), Category 3 (8.3), Category 2 (8.9), Category 1 (8.7) — Category 1 sits nearest the value-axis origin at the bottom.
- Candidate: rows top-to-bottom are Category 1 (8.7), Category 2 (8.9), Category 3 (8.3), Category 4 (12.3) — the exact reverse. Confirmed by measuring each bar's fill-edge pixel against the axis scale (0 at x=119px, 12.3 at x=1088px in the 1280×720 render): row y=180 ends at 8.7, y=285 at 8.9, y=390 at 8.3, y=495 at 12.3.
- XML: `<c:catAx>...<c:scaling><c:orientation val="minMax"/></c:scaling>...<c:axPos val="l"/>...</c:catAx>`
- Why the candidate is wrong: the category axis scaling orientation is `minMax` (not `maxMin`). For a horizontal bar chart, `minMax` orientation places the first category at the axis origin (bottom) and subsequent categories going up, which is what the reference does; the candidate instead places the first category at the top, effectively rendering as if orientation were `maxMin`.

### 4. Legend placed top-right instead of bottom-center

- Where: Chart 10 (id 11), region x∈[0.863, 0.910], y∈[0.183, 0.240] in the candidate; reference legend sits centered below the plot area (roughly x∈[0.40, 0.61], y∈[0.78, 0.81])
- Reference: a single horizontal row "■ Series 1  ■ Series 2  ■ Series 3" centered below the plot area.
- Candidate: the three legend entries are stacked vertically in the top-right corner of the chart frame.
- XML: `<c:legend><c:legendPos val="b"/>...</c:legend>`
- Why the candidate is wrong: `c:legendPos` is explicitly `"b"` (bottom); the candidate's placement matches neither a bottom nor even a right layout with correct margins.

### 5. Value axis auto-scale doesn't round to a "nice" max/step

- Where: Chart 10 (id 11), left label column, tick values 0/3.1/6.2/9.2/12.3
- Reference: axis runs 0 to 14 with a major unit of 2 (0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14) — a conventional rounded range above the true data max (12.3, from Category 4's 4.5+2.8+5 stack).
- Candidate: axis runs 0 to 12.3 with a major unit of ≈3.075, i.e. exactly the data max split into 4 equal ticks, with no rounding.
- XML: neither `c:valAx/c:scaling` nor the axis itself specifies `c:max`, `c:min`, or `c:majorUnit`, so both are auto-computed; the underlying data max across categories is 12.3 (Category 4: 4.5 + 2.8 + 5).
- Why the candidate is wrong: OOXML doesn't mandate a specific auto-scale algorithm, but not rounding the axis bound/step to a human-friendly number is a plausible renderer bug rather than a deliberate choice, and it changes the on-screen bar-length ratios between the two renders.
