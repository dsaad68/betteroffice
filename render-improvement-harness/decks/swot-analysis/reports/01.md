## Reference vs candidate

The slide is a SWOT-analysis quad with four flat-icon circles (S/W/O/T) arranged in a square, each paired with a heading and Lorem Ipsum body text, under a "SWOT ANALYSIS" title. LibreOffice draws each circle as a clean disc with only a thin, rim-hugging darker crescent for shading. BetterOffice draws the same circles but the shading shape balloons into a large diamond/pin that pokes out from each circle, overlapping the neighboring text. Separately, LibreOffice mis-wraps two pieces of text that BetterOffice renders correctly (or at least more plausibly) given the box widths in the XML.

## Findings

### 1. Icon shading crescent renders as an oversized diamond on all four circles

- Where: "Freeform: Shape 21" (id 22) inside "Group 24" (id 25), and its three siblings "Freeform: Shape 27" (id 28), "Freeform: Shape 31" (id 32), "Freeform: Shape 35" (id 36) inside groups 26/30/34 — region [0.307, 0.174, 0.707, 0.86] covering all four SWOT icons.
- Reference: a thin, darker-blue/green/orange/purple crescent sits flush against the bottom-right rim of each circle, giving a subtle 3D shading look. It never extends past the circle's own silhouette.
- Candidate: the same shape renders as a large kite/diamond that extends far below and to the side of the circle, overlapping the connecting whitespace and, for the W and T icons, nearly touching the "WEAKNESS"/"THREAT" labels.
- XML (Group 24, representative of all four):
  ```xml
  <p:grpSp><p:nvGrpSpPr><p:cNvPr id="25" name="Group 24"/></p:nvGrpSpPr>
  <p:grpSpPr><a:xfrm>
    <a:off x="3743039" y="1190173"/><a:ext cx="2351315" cy="2104570"/>
    <a:chOff x="3788228" y="1190173"/><a:chExt cx="4063689" cy="3744686"/>
  </a:xfrm></p:grpSpPr>
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="22" name="Freeform: Shape 21"/></p:nvSpPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm rot="18900000">
    <a:off x="4464751" y="2391876"/><a:ext cx="3387166" cy="2336802"/>
  </a:xfrm><a:custGeom>...</a:custGeom>
  <a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="0094C8"/></a:solidFill></p:spPr></p:sp>
  ```
  All four groups share the identical `chOff`/`chExt` (4063689 x 3744686) against a different, smaller `ext` (2351315 x 2104570), i.e. an anisotropic child-space scale of sx=0.579 / sy=0.562, and each freeform child carries its own `rot="18900000"` (315°) independent of the group.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the freeform's own path is `w="3387166" h="2336802"`, almost as large as the circle it shades (`Oval 20`'s `ext cx="3744686" cy="3744686"`). For it to end up as a thin rim crescent (as in the reference, and as the design — a subtle shading detail, not a giant pointer — clearly intends), the non-uniform group child scale must be applied to the already-rotated shape, shearing it down to a sliver. The candidate's render matches what you'd get by scaling/rotating the shape as if the child-space scale were closer to isotropic, or by not composing the shape's own rotation with the group's anisotropic `chOff`/`chExt` scale — either way it preserves far more of the freeform's raw size than the reference and the geometry support.

### 2. LibreOffice drops the trailing "Y" of "OPPORTUNITY" onto an overlapping line

- Where: "TextBox 43" (id 44), region [0.107, 0.621, 0.283, 0.7]
- Reference: renders "OPPORTUNIT" on the heading's line, then a stray orange "Y" appears one line down, overlapping the "LOREM IPSUM DOLOR SIT" body text below it (reads like "...DOLOR SIY").
- Candidate: "OPPORTUNITY" renders whole, right-aligned, on its own single line — no overlap with the body text underneath.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="1306127" y="4256907"/><a:ext cx="2148114" cy="369332"/></a:xfrm></p:spPr>
  <p:txBody><a:bodyPr wrap="square" rtlCol="0"><a:spAutoFit/></a:bodyPr>
  <a:p><a:pPr algn="r"/><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-IN" spc="300" .../><a:t>OPPORTUNITY</a:t></a:r></a:p>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong (i.e. why this is filed as lo-suspect): the box is 2148114 EMU (≈225.6px at this render size) wide, and the rendered word — measured in both images — is only 169-198px wide even with the `spc="300"` tracking applied. "OPPORTUNITY" is a single word with no space to break on, so word-wrap should never fire, and the word plainly fits the box width with margin to spare. LibreOffice's line break mid-word, followed by drawing the wrapped remainder at a wrong vertical offset that overlaps unrelated text below, is not something the XML supports.

### 3. LibreOffice wraps the "SWOT ANALYSIS" title to two lines where it fits on one

- Where: "TextBox 49" (id 50), region [0.012, 0.0, 0.496, 0.135]
- Reference: "SWOT ANALYSIS" wraps at the space, rendering as "SWOT" over "ANALYSIS" on two lines.
- Candidate: renders "SWOT ANALYSIS" as a single line.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:xfrm><a:off x="145143" y="0"/><a:ext cx="5904022" cy="923330"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:bodyPr wrap="square" rtlCol="0"><a:spAutoFit/></a:bodyPr>
  <a:rPr lang="en-IN" sz="5400" .../><a:latin typeface="Open Sans Extrabold" .../>
  ```
- Why this is filed as lo-suspect rather than a plain finding: the box is 5904022 EMU (≈620px at this render size); the candidate's single-line render measures ≈575px, comfortably inside the box. Confidence is medium rather than high because the exact width depends on which font is actually substituted for "Open Sans Extrabold" on each engine, but the same deck already shows LibreOffice over-wrapping text that fits (finding 2), which corroborates that this is the same LibreOffice-side quirk rather than a BetterOffice text-layout defect.
