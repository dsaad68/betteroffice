## Reference vs candidate

Slide 16 shows the title "Automatically classify, detect and remediate content uploaded to cloud apps" over a light-grey content panel with four yellow document icons (PCI, PII, PHI, Source code — each a plain white "?" glyph combined with a topic-specific graphic) and four orange numbered step pills below them. An off-canvas red production note bleeds onto the left edge in both renders. LibreOffice draws both title lines cleanly above the panel, all four icons with their full combined artwork (padlock/briefcase, "SSN" tag with a gold border, a caduceus, and "{ }" plus a magnifying glass), all four step pills solid orange with legible white numbers and text, and white numbers/captions throughout. BetterOffice drops the title's second line into the panel, renders every label and number that relies on the shape-style font color as dark grey instead of white, loses the orange fill on step boxes 3 and 4, drops the PHI caduceus picture entirely, replaces the magnifying-glass freeform with a solid grey box, and strips the border off the "SSN" tag.

## Findings

### 1. Title's second line sags into the content panel

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), region ≈ [0.03, 0.04, 0.92, 0.23].
- Reference: both title lines sit fully above the grey content panel.
- Candidate: line 1 lands in the same place as the reference, but line 2 is pushed much further down, ending up largely hidden behind the top edge of the grey content panel.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:titleStyle><a:lvl1pPr ...>
    <a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc>
    ...
  ```
  (from `slideMaster1.xml`; the slide's own `Title 1` has empty `<p:spPr/>`/`<a:bodyPr/>`, so line spacing is fully inherited.)
- Why the candidate is wrong: the master's title style specifies 80% line spacing. With line 1 anchored the same way in both renders, the much larger gap before line 2 in the candidate is consistent with rendering at 100% line spacing instead of the inherited 80% (same defect as slide 15 of this deck).

### 2. Shape-style font color (schemeClr "lt1") resolves to the theme's dark color instead of white

- Where: the four step-number labels ("1"-"4", ids 76/80/84/87), the four icon captions ("PCI"/"PII"/"PHI"/"Source code", ids 19/102/103/106), the "{ }" label (id 107), and the off-slide note (id 131) — region ≈ [0.17, 0.407, 0.97, 0.866] for the icon/step rows.
- Reference: all of these render pure white (`FFFFFF`) text, per each shape's `p:style/a:fontRef` scheme color `lt1`.
- Candidate: the same text renders in a dark grey — sampling the pixels under the "1" number and the "PCI" caption gives RGB `(103, 103, 103)`, which is exactly `0x676767`.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:style>...<a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef></p:style>
  <a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" .../><a:t>PCI</a:t></a:r>   <!-- no run-level solidFill -->
  ```
  and in `theme1.xml`: `<a:dk1><a:srgbClr val="676767"/></a:dk1>` / `<a:lt1><a:srgbClr val="FFFFFF"/></a:lt1>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: `676767` is precisely theme's `dk1`, not `lt1`. Runs with no explicit color must take their fill from the shape style's `fontRef` scheme color, which here is `lt1` (white); the candidate substitutes `dk1` instead — a scheme-color-slot mixup, not a missing-fill issue (boxes 1 and 2 in finding 3 keep their orange fill yet their "1"/"2" numbers are still grey, ruling out a background-only explanation).

### 3. Step boxes 3 and 4 lose their orange fill

- Where: Rounded Rectangle 92/100 (ids 82, 86) and their number tabs (ids 84, 87), region ≈ [0.585, 0.693, 0.97, 0.866].
- Reference: all four step pills (1-4) are solid orange (`FFBC30`).
- Candidate: pills 1 and 2 stay orange; pills 3 and 4 render with no fill, showing the grey panel through them.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:grpSpPr>...<a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFBC30"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
  <p:sp><!-- Rounded Rectangle 92 --><p:spPr>...<a:grpFill/>...</p:spPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:grpFill/>` means "inherit the enclosing group's fill" (ECMA-376 §20.1.8.35); `Group 91`/`Group 99`'s `p:grpSpPr` explicitly sets `FFBC30`. Boxes 1 and 2 use a direct `a:solidFill` instead of `a:grpFill` and render correctly, isolating the bug to group-fill inheritance specifically.

### 4. PHI icon's caduceus picture is missing entirely

- Where: Picture 2 (id 105), region ≈ [0.641, 0.419, 0.697, 0.5].
- Reference: a white caduceus/medical-symbol graphic sits inside the "?" glyph, forming the combined PHI icon.
- Candidate: nothing is drawn there; only the bare "?" glyph shows.

- Why the candidate is wrong: `bo-log.json` records `"skipped_images": 1` for this slide, matching a single failed picture — the WMF metafile is not being rasterized/rendered at all rather than being drawn incorrectly.

### 5. Magnifying-glass freeform renders as a solid grey box

- Where: Freeform 15 (id 72), region ≈ [0.876, 0.481, 0.964, 0.639].
- Reference: a grey magnifying-glass silhouette (handle + ring) overlapping the bottom-right of the "Source code" icon.
- Candidate: the same area is filled edge-to-edge with a flat grey rectangle, i.e. the shape's plain bounding box.
- XML: `<a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst><a:gd name="T0" fmla="*/ 858 w 1484"/>...` followed later by `<a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:solidFill>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's fill (`tx1`, a dark grey) is correct, but it is being applied to the shape's full `a:ext` bounding rectangle instead of the `custGeom` path built from the guide list (`a:gdLst`) — the guide-formula path is not being parsed/drawn, so the renderer falls back to the bounding box.

### 6. "SSN" tag loses its orange border

- Where: Rectangle 19 (id 20), region ≈ [0.465, 0.431, 0.52, 0.478].
- Reference: the white "SSN" tag has a visible gold/orange (`FFC000`) outline.
- Candidate: no outline is drawn; the tag is a plain white patch.
- XML: `<a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill><a:ln><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFC000"/></a:solidFill></a:ln>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's fill (`bg1`, white) is rendered, but its explicit `a:ln` outline color is dropped.

### 7. Off-slide production-note callout wraps differently

- Where: Rectangle 130 (id 131), region ≈ [-0.105, 0.362, 0.123, 0.762] (box extends off-slide to the left).
- Reference: visible fragments read "e replace / n new / umb boxes / slide 23".
- Candidate: visible fragments read "eplace with / crumb boxes / slide 23" — different line breaks reveal different words in the on-slide sliver.
- XML: `<a:bodyPr rtlCol="0" anchor="ctr"/>` with no explicit wrap/size override; run text `Please replace with new breadcrumb boxes from slide 23` in a 2087880 EMU-wide box.
- Why the candidate is wrong: with no explicit size/wrap override, the two engines should agree on line breaks for the same font/size; the differing breaks point to a font-metrics mismatch for this run (same pattern as slide 15 of this deck).
