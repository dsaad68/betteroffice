## Reference vs candidate

Slide 7 is Cisco's "Cloud Access Security" funnel diagram: a large custom-geometry trapezoid stack (Audit/Analyze & Control/Detect/Protect/Investigate) surrounded by dozens of small vector and raster icons, cloud shapes, and rotated side labels. LibreOffice renders it as a clean, mostly white/light-gray diagram with colorful icons and readable rotated labels. BetterOffice renders the same diagram with a large opaque gray wash covering nearly the whole slide, most small icon glyphs missing (leaving bare gray/white squares), the "Cloud Apps" cloud shape filled as a plain box, the vertical "Before/During/After" labels mirrored, and the pattern-filled "Analyze & Control" band and elastica logo missing.

## Findings

### 1. Hidden decorative group rendered fully opaque, covering most of the slide

- Where: Group 5 (id 6), region roughly [0.035, 0.183, 1.08, 1.55] (extends past the slide edges, as expected for a decorative motif)
- Reference: not drawn at all — the group is marked hidden.
- Candidate: two large "wing" freeforms (ids 264, 265) are drawn at full opacity in a flat mid-gray, blanketing almost the entire diagram area and explaining the bulk of the 41% pixel diff (hot cells r4c4 97.9%, r3c4 84.8%, r2c1 63.2%, r2c4 60.2%).
- XML:
```xml
<p:grpSp><p:nvGrpSpPr><p:cNvPr id="6" name="Group 5" hidden="1"/>
...
<p:sp><p:cNvPr id="264" name="Freeform 263"/>
...<a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="accent3"><a:alpha val="67000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `hidden="1"` on the group means it must not be rendered; BetterOffice ignores the flag. It also ignores the 67% alpha on the fill (accent3 = `A3A3A3`), drawing it fully opaque — measured pixel (900,180), which should be the green "Audit" trapezoid (176,215,96 in the reference), is a flat (163,163,163) in the candidate, matching opaque `A3A3A3` exactly rather than a 67%-alpha blend.

### 2. `grpFill`-inherited icon glyphs render with no fill (icons disappear)

- Where: Group 10 (id 11), region [0.391, 0.29, 0.43, 0.361] — the "crate/box" icon in the Audit row; the same `<a:grpFill/>` pattern occurs 51 times across this slide's icon glyphs (magnifying glass, chat bubble, key, film reel, cloud, etc.)
- Reference: a white box/crate glyph drawn on the light-gray rounded-square backdrop.
- Candidate: the backdrop square (Rounded Rectangle 28, id 29, `schemeClr bg1` lumMod 85%) renders correctly, but the glyph itself is entirely absent — the square is empty.
- XML:
```xml
<p:grpSp><p:cNvPr id="305" name="Group 95"/>
<p:grpSpPr>...<a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg2"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
<p:sp><p:cNvPr id="306" name="Freeform 37"/>
...<a:grpFill/><a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the glyph shape has no fill of its own and declares `<a:grpFill/>`, which per the spec means "use the parent group's fill" (here `bg2` → white). BetterOffice appears not to resolve `grpFill`, leaving the shape unfilled/transparent instead of white, so it disappears against its backdrop. Given 51 occurrences on this slide alone, this is the second-largest contributor to the diff after finding 1.

### 3. "Cloud Apps" cloud outline fills as its bounding rectangle

- Where: Freeform 13 (id 180), region [0.305, 0.086, 0.899, 0.236]
- Reference: a scalloped cloud silhouette (light blue) with white showing through the concave notches between cloud lobes.
- Candidate: a solid light-blue rectangle spanning the shape's full bounding box, with no scalloped edge — the notches are filled in.
- XML: `<a:custGeom><a:pathLst>...</a:pathLst></a:custGeom><a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="accent2"><a:lumMod val="20000"/><a:lumOff val="80000"/></a:schemeClr></a:solidFill>`
- Why the candidate is wrong: the shape's outline is defined by an explicit `custGeom` path (cloud-lobe cubic Béziers); the fill should follow that path, not the shape's axis-aligned bounding box.

### 4. Rotated side labels render mirrored instead of upright

- Where: "Round Same Side Corner Rectangle" shapes carrying the labels Before (id 296, [0.92, 0.319, 1.03, 0.366]), During (id 451, [0.903, 0.693, 1.047, 0.74]), After (id 359, [0.94, 0.888, 1.01, 0.935]), and Other Appliances (id 317, [0.025, 0.402, 0.081, 0.546])
- Reference: text reads correctly top-to-bottom when the shape is rotated 90°, e.g. "Before" upright and legible.
- Candidate: the glyphs are mirrored/upside-down (e.g. "Before" reads as a horizontally-flipped jumble, confirmed by cropping the region).
- XML: `<a:xfrm rot="5400000" flipH="1"><a:off .../><a:ext .../></a:xfrm>` with `<a:t>During</a:t>` inside.
- Why the candidate is wrong: `rot="5400000"` (90°) combined with `flipH="1"` is the standard OOXML idiom for rotating a shape while keeping its text upright (the flip cancels out visually for the frame, not the glyphs). BetterOffice appears to apply the `flipH` to the rendered text itself, mirroring the glyphs, instead of only flipping the shape geometry.

### 5. Pattern fill missing on "Analyze & Control" band

- Where: Freeform 388 (id 389), region [0.324, 0.447, 0.805, 0.58]
- Reference: a light 30%-dot checker pattern over the band.
- Candidate: flat white, no pattern.
- XML: `<a:pattFill prst="pct30"><a:fgClr><a:schemeClr val="accent3"><a:lumMod val="20000"/><a:lumOff val="80000"/></a:schemeClr></a:fgClr><a:bgClr><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:bgClr></a:pattFill>`
