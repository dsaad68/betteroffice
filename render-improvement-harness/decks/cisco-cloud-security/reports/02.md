## Reference vs candidate

Slide 2 is a two-panel "you wouldn't run email/cloud without security" illustration under a two-line title. LibreOffice shows the full title, a padlock-and-envelope icon on the left, and a white cloud dotted with colored app-icon circles and question marks on the right. BetterOffice shows the same layout and text boxes, but the title's second line is cut off, both hand-drawn icons (the padlock/envelope and the cloud outline) collapse into plain rectangles, two of the app-icon glyphs lose their intended monochrome treatment, and the footer slide number prints as an unresolved field code.

## Findings

### 1. custGeom shapes built from guide formulas render as bounding-box rectangles

- Where: left icon "Rounded Rectangle 2" (id 3) content — Freeform 115/116/119 (ids 30, 31, 34) and Group 4's Freeform 117/118 (ids 32, 33), region ~[0.225, 0.363, 0.428, 0.817]; right icon "Rounded Rectangle 57" (id 58) content — Group 14's Freeform 16/17 (ids 17, 18), region ~[0.585, 0.513, 0.787, 0.667].
- Reference: a white padlock clasping a mail envelope on the left, and a white cloud outline (two overlapping cloud lobes) on the right, both with soft rounded contours.
- Candidate: the left icon is a plain white rectangle with two small white squares near the bottom (the two small freeforms shrunk to their own tiny bounding boxes); the right icon is two overlapping plain white rectangles with square corners, no cloud silhouette at all.
- XML: 
  ```
  <a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst>
    <a:gd name="T0" fmla="*/ 12 w 329"/><a:gd name="T1" fmla="*/ 233 h 233"/> ... (id 30)
  ```
  ```
  <a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst>
    <a:gd name="connsiteX0" fmla="*/ 1899343 w 4528033"/><a:gd name="connsiteY0" fmla="*/ 0 h 2516401"/> ... (id 17)
  ```
  Both shapes' `<a:pathLst>` moveTo/lnTo/cubicBezTo/arcTo points reference these named guides (`T0..Tn` or `connsiteX/Y..`) rather than literal coordinates.
- Why the candidate is wrong: every affected shape is a `custGeom` whose path coordinates are indirected through `gdLst` guide formulas instead of literal `x`/`y` values. All seven of them render as their untransformed rectangular bounds, which strongly suggests the renderer isn't resolving named guides when building the path and is falling back to the shape's bounding box. This is the single largest visible defect on the slide — it deletes the entire hand-drawn iconography.

### 2. Title's second line is clipped at the placeholder's bottom edge

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), inherits geometry from `slideLayout15.xml`/master: `off (310855, 341313)`, `ext (8469608, 731837)` → box ≈ [0.034, 0.066, 0.96, 0.209] of the slide.
- Reference: both lines of "Every time you adopt a new technology, you have to secure it" are fully visible; the second line's descenders extend a little below the nominal placeholder box.
- Candidate: only the top ~half of the second line's glyphs are drawn; everything below y≈113px (the placeholder's exact bottom edge, 0.2086 × 540) is hard-clipped away.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:sp><p:nvPr><p:ph type="title"/></p:nvPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm><a:off x="310855" y="341313"/><a:ext cx="8469608" cy="731837"/></a:xfrm>
  <p:txBody><a:bodyPr vert="horz" wrap="square" ... anchor="ctr" .../> <!-- no normAutofit / spAutoFit anywhere in slide, layout, or master -->
  ```
  titleStyle: `sz="3200"` with `<a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: nothing in the slide/layout/master chain sets `normAutofit` or `spAutoFit` on this placeholder, and two 32pt lines at 80% line spacing (~51pt) don't fit inside the 57.6pt-tall box once vertical insets are added. PowerPoint/LibreOffice's default behavior for a plain `wrap="square"` textbox is to let excess text overflow the shape unclipped, not to crop it. The candidate instead clips exactly at the box's bottom edge, dropping half the second line.

### 3. Duotone/biLevel picture effects not applied to two app-icon glyphs

- Where: Picture 6 (id 28, Google Drive glyph, region ~[0.607, 0.616, 0.623, 0.645]); Picture 10 (id 26, Yammer glyph, region ~[0.631, 0.55, 0.643, 0.57]). Both sit inside Group 14's dark ovals alongside four other picture glyphs that have no blip effect and already render correctly (as plain white icons) in both engines.
- Reference: both glyphs render as flat, mostly-white silhouettes matching the uniform dark-circle/white-glyph style of the other four app icons.
- Candidate: the Google Drive glyph shows its native blue/green/yellow triangle, and the Yammer glyph shows its native teal-blue "Y:" mark — i.e. the source PNGs are drawn unmodified.
- XML:
  ```
  <a:blip r:embed="rId11" cstate="print">
    <a:duotone><a:schemeClr val="bg2"><a:shade val="45000"/><a:satMod val="135000"/></a:schemeClr><a:prstClr val="white"/></a:duotone>
  </a:blip>
  ```
  ```
  <a:blip r:embed="rId12" cstate="print"><a:biLevel thresh="25000"/></a:blip>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `a:duotone` and `a:biLevel` are documented DrawingML blip color-transform effects (ECMA-376 §20.1.8.16/20.1.8.24). Both are present only on these two blips and are the only thing that distinguishes them from the four sibling glyphs that already render as flat white icons — the candidate is dropping both effects and drawing the raw bitmap.

### 4. Slide-number field prints its placeholder code instead of the resolved number

- Where: footer, bottom-right corner, region ~[0.953, 0.931, 0.971, 0.944].
- Reference: prints "2" (the slide's 1-based index).
- Candidate: prints the literal field-code glyph "«#»".
- XML: `master.xml`: `<a:fld id="{4ABDCABE-3F10-B64C-92F1-862014417034}" type="slidenum"><a:rPr .../><a:t>2</a:t></a:fld>` — the cached `<a:t>` text is itself "2", the correct value, but the candidate isn't using it (or the field-substitution path).
- Why the candidate is wrong: `type="slidenum"` fields must be rendered as the evaluated slide number, and the XML even carries the correct cached fallback text ("2"); showing the raw placeholder glyph is a field-evaluation gap, not an XML content difference.
