## Reference vs candidate

The slide shows a branching tree of person-icon nodes (a "sharing" network fanning out from one user into blue/purple/yellow/green/gray groups, plus a red "Competitor" cluster), connected by thin lines, alongside a "Payroll.docx" file icon, a stat callout, and a caption. The reference (LibreOffice) draws each node as a person silhouette inside two overlapping circles, connects every node with a colored line, and keeps the title fully above the graphic. The candidate (BetterOffice) draws the same circles but replaces every silhouette with a plain colored square, omits every connector line entirely, and its title's second line sits low enough to run into and behind the top row of nodes.

## Findings

### 1. custGeom shapes drawn as their bounding-box rectangle

- Where: every `custGeom` Freeform on the slide (131 total) — person silhouettes inside every node group (e.g. Freeform 994 id 6, region `[0.062,0.432,0.119,0.55]`), the small upload-arrow icon under "Payroll.docx" (Freeform 5/6, ids 196/197), and the red silhouettes in the "Competitor" box.
- Reference: each renders its defined path — a head-and-shoulders person silhouette, or a small arrow/document glyph.
- Candidate: each renders as a plain filled square/rectangle spanning the shape's `a:off`/`a:ext` box, in the shape's correct fill color but with none of the path detail.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="6" name="Freeform 994"/>...
  <a:xfrm><a:off x="707724" y="1423012"/><a:ext cx="839836" cy="974813"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst>...T0="*/ 433 w 447" ... (guide-formula geometry)</a:gdLst>
  <a:pathLst>...</a:pathLst></a:custGeom>
  <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="accent1"/></a:solidFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `custGeom` requires evaluating the `<a:gdLst>` guide formulas and drawing the resulting `<a:pathLst>` commands, not filling the shape's overall extent. This is the same failure mode already reported on slide 3 of this deck and is the dominant cause of the three top hot cells (r1c1 17.0%, r1c2 20.4%, r1c3 16.5%), all of which sit over the node grid.

### 2. Connector lines invisible (no line color resolved from shape style)

- Where: all 93 `cxnSp` straight connectors joining the person nodes, e.g. Straight Connector 20 (id 21, region `[0.137,0.42,0.188,0.491]`), Straight Connector 22 (id 23), Straight Connector 1000 (id 164, inside the "Competitor" group).
- Reference: draws a thin `accent1`-colored line between each pair of connected nodes.
- Candidate: draws nothing — no stroke of any color or width appears along any connector's path.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:cxnSp><p:nvCxnSpPr><p:cNvPr id="21" name="Straight Connector 20"/>
  <a:stCxn id="31" idx="6"/><a:endCxn id="93" idx="2"/></p:cNvCxnSpPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm flipV="1"><a:off .../><a:ext .../></a:xfrm><a:prstGeom prst="line"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom></p:spPr>
  <p:style><a:lnRef idx="1"><a:schemeClr val="accent1"/></a:lnRef>...<a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="tx1"/></a:fontRef></p:style></p:cxnSp>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: none of the 93 connectors carry an explicit `<a:ln>` in `spPr`; their stroke color and weight come solely from the shape's `p:style/a:lnRef idx="1"` (which resolves to theme `accent1`). The candidate is evidently not resolving this style-matrix reference for connector lines, so they render with no stroke at all.

### 3. "Competitor" label loses its white text color

- Where: Rectangle 1006 (id 170), the "Competitor" banner text, region `[0.789,0.365,0.929,0.405]`.
- Reference: white text on the red (`C00000`) banner.
- Candidate: dark gray/near-black text on the same red banner, low contrast.
- XML: `<a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1400" dirty="0"/><a:t>Competitor</a:t>` — no run-level `<a:solidFill>`; the shape's `p:style` carries `<a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef>` (white).
- Why the candidate is wrong: same style-matrix resolution gap as finding 2, applied to text color this time — `a:fontRef`'s scheme color should supply the run's default color when no direct run fill is present; the candidate instead falls back to the slide's default text color.

### 4. Title line spacing wider than spec, causing overlap with the graphic

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), region `[0.034,0.066,0.96,0.22]`.
- Reference: "Users are sharing information freely and it / could lead to a breach" renders as two tightly-spaced lines (measured top-to-top pitch 41px) that end (row ~106 of 540) above where the topmost network nodes begin (row ~110+).
- Candidate: the same two lines are spaced further apart (line 1 itself starts 13px lower, and the line pitch measures ~50px), so the second line extends down to around row 112+ and visually collides with/is partly painted over by the top row of nodes starting at the same rows.
- XML: `slideMaster1.xml` `titleStyle/a:lvl1pPr`: `<a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc>` (80% line spacing), `sz="3200"`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: 50px / 41px ≈ 1.22, matching the ratio between 100% and the spec's 80% line spacing (1/0.8 = 1.25) far better than any font-metric rounding would explain; the master's `lnSpc` override is not being applied to the title placeholder.

### 5. "Payroll.docx" label wraps despite `wrap="none"` (lo-suspect)

- Where: Shape 635 (id 198), region `[0.098,0.629,0.199,0.683]`.
- Reference: wraps to two lines, "Payroll.doc" then "x" — splitting the filename's extension mid-word.
- Candidate: keeps "Payroll.docx" on one line.
- XML: `<a:bodyPr wrap="none" lIns="45719" rIns="45719"><a:spAutoFit/></a:bodyPr>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong (it isn't, marked lo-suspect): `wrap="none"` combined with `<a:spAutoFit/>` means the shape is meant to grow to fit unwrapped text; the reference's word-wrap here contradicts the explicit XML and is the more likely bug.

### 6. "Cloud apps move..." caption wraps at different words

- Where: Rectangle 167 (id 168), region `[0.725,0.668,0.972,0.861]`.
- Reference: "Cloud apps move / sharing permissions / outside of IT control".
- Candidate: "Cloud apps move sharing / permissions outside of IT / control" — same 3 lines, one extra word fits on line 1.
- XML: `<a:bodyPr rtlCol="0" anchor="ctr"/>` with no run-level size override; theme `minorFont` is Arial for both engines.
- Why the candidate is wrong: unclear from the XML alone — no explicit sizing/spacing property differs, so this reads as a font-metric/text-measurement difference between the two engines' Arial rather than a spec violation. Flagged for reference; drives part of hot cell r3c4 (10.5%).
