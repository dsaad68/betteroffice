## Reference vs candidate

Slide 22 is the deck's closing slide: `slide22.xml` carries no shapes of its own, so everything visible comes from slideLayout38.xml ("2_Closing_Photo") — a full-bleed sky photo behind a Cisco logo-plus-tagline graphic. LibreOffice draws both: the sky photo and, centered lower-left, a grey-duotone Cisco logo with the italic tagline "TOMORROW starts here." BetterOffice draws the sky photo correctly but omits the logo/tagline picture entirely, leaving that area blank sky.
