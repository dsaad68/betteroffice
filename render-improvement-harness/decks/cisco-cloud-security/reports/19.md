## Reference vs candidate

Slide 19 is a dense "network of users" diagram illustrating how a document ("Payroll.docx") spreads across an organization, built from ~190 shapes: two nested translucent ovals plus a custGeom silhouette (person, folder, or check icon) per node, straight connector lines wiring the tree together, a few octagon "X" stop-icons, and a red "Competitor" callout box, all under a two-line title. The reference draws every node as a colored circle with a crisp person/folder silhouette inside, wired together by thin connector lines, with a fully legible two-line title. The candidate draws the same circles but with a plain rectangle in place of each silhouette, omits every connector line so the tree structure is gone, shifts the title down and clips the top of its second line to illegibility, and renders a couple of style-driven white text labels ("Competitor", the octagon "X") in dark gray instead.

## Findings

### 1. custGeom icons collapse to bounding-box rectangles

- Where: every "person/folder/document" silhouette in the diagram, e.g. Freeform 994 (id 161) inside Group 991, region `[0.042, 0.212, 0.929, 0.98]` covering the whole diagram.
- Reference: draws the authored outline — a rounded head-and-shoulders silhouette, a folder icon, or a checkmark, depending on the node.
- Candidate: draws a flat, axis-aligned rectangle exactly matching the shape's bounding box, in the correct fill color but with none of the outline detail.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst>...</a:gdLst>
  <a:pathLst><a:path w="447" h="521">
    <a:moveTo><a:pt x="434" y="387"/></a:moveTo>
    <a:lnTo><a:pt x="434" y="387"/></a:lnTo>
    <a:lnTo><a:pt x="433" y="381"/></a:lnTo>
    ... (151 explicit line-to points total)
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the path has 151 literal `<a:pt>` coordinates tracing a silhouette, not a rectangle. This is the single largest contributor to the slide's diff — 131 of the slide's shapes use `<a:custGeom>` and every one of them is affected, which is why the hot cells span nearly the whole diagram area (r1c1/r1c2/r1c3/r2c3).

### 2. All connector lines are invisible

- Where: every `cxnSp` "Straight Connector" wiring the diagram together, e.g. Straight Connector 161 (id 162, `stCxn`/`endCxn` to ids 160 and 174), region `[0.062, 0.212, 0.929, 0.941]`.
- Reference: draws a thin colored line between each pair of connected nodes, showing the tree structure.
- Candidate: draws nothing at all where the connectors should be — no line, in any color — even though the connector shapes are present in the XML with valid `off`/`ext` geometry.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:cxnSp><p:nvCxnSpPr>...<a:stCxn id="160" idx="6"/><a:endCxn id="174" idx="2"/></p:nvCxnSpPr>
  <p:spPr><a:xfrm flipV="1"><a:off .../><a:ext .../></a:xfrm>
    <a:prstGeom prst="line"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom></p:spPr>
  <p:style><a:lnRef idx="1"><a:schemeClr val="accent1"/></a:lnRef>...</p:style>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: none of the ~92 connectors on this slide carry an explicit `<a:ln>` in `spPr`; their only stroke definition is the shape-style reference `<p:style><a:lnRef idx="1">`, which resolves through the theme's line-style matrix to a solid accent1 line. The reference draws that line; the candidate treats the connector as having no stroke at all, dropping every connecting line on the slide.

### 3. Title text shifted down and second line clipped

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), region `[0.034, 0.066, 0.96, 0.209]` (the inherited master/layout title box).
- Reference: both lines render at full size, vertically centered in the box, with the first line starting at y≈34px and the second line fully visible down to y≈107px.
- Candidate: the whole two-line block starts ~14px lower (first line at y≈48px), and the second line ("control needed to secure cloud apps") is hard-clipped at roughly the x-height line (~y=97-105px) — only the bottom half of each letter (e.g. the lower arcs of "c"/"o"/"n") is drawn, making the line effectively unreadable.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- slideMaster1.xml, title placeholder -->
  <a:bodyPr vert="horz" wrap="square" lIns="91424" tIns="45712" rIns="91424" bIns="45712"
            numCol="1" anchor="ctr" anchorCtr="0" compatLnSpc="1"/>
  <!-- slideLayout15.xml -->
  <a:xfrm><a:off x="310855" y="341313"/><a:ext cx="8469608" cy="731837"/></a:xfrm>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the box is 731837 EMU (57.6px) tall, too short for two lines of this title's font size, and nothing in the slide/layout/master inheritance chain (`bodyPr`) defines `normAutofit` or `spAutoFit`. Per OOXML defaults, unfitted text should simply overflow the shape unclipped — which is what the reference does. The candidate appears to clip text to the shape bounds instead of letting it overflow, identical to the mechanism already documented on slide 02 of this deck.

### 4. Style-referenced text color (fontRef) not applied

- Where: Rectangle 1006 "Competitor" label (id 210), region `[0.789, 0.403, 0.929, 0.443]`; also Octagon 21's "X" label (id 22).
- Reference: draws both labels in white, legible against their dark-red fills.
- Candidate: draws both labels in the default dark gray body-text color, which is low-contrast but still legible against the red.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:style>...<a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef></p:style>
  <p:txBody>...<a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1400" dirty="0"/><a:t>Competitor</a:t></a:r>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: neither run has an explicit color override, so per OOXML inheritance the run's fill must come from the shape's style `<a:fontRef>`, which points at scheme color `lt1` (white via the master's `clrMap`). The candidate instead falls back to a generic gray, ignoring the style reference — the same class of "shape-style-reference not resolved" gap as finding 2's missing connector strokes (`lnRef`), just for `fontRef`.
