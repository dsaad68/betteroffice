## Reference vs candidate

Slide 12 shows the title "Monitor cloud users, accounts and find malicious activity" above an iMac mockup displaying a "Detect" security-dashboard screenshot, with a four-item numbered list of orange rounded-rectangle bars to its right ("Extract user activity through Product X", "Learn normal per-user behavior", "Detect and record intrusions", "Assign a ThreatScore™..."), plus an off-slide "replace with new breadcrumb boxes" draft callout bleeding in from the left edge. LibreOffice renders the full two-line title, a clean cropped screenshot inside the monitor, four solid-orange bars with white bold numbers and white body text, and the correct page number "12" in the footer. BetterOffice clips the title's second line mid-glyph, shows extra uncropped UI chrome above and below the screenshot content, drops the orange fill from all four list bars (leaving their text all but invisible), renders the digits '1'-'4' in a dark color instead of white, and prints the literal field code "«#»" instead of "12" in the footer.

## Findings

### 1. Title's second line hard-clipped instead of overflowing the placeholder

- Where: `Title 1` (id 2), region `[0.034, 0.066, 0.96, 0.209]`.
- Reference: both title lines render in full, extending slightly past the nominal placeholder box with no visible clipping (anchor is centered, and no autofit is defined so PowerPoint-family renderers let overflow show).
- Candidate: "Monitor cloud users, accounts and find" renders fully, but "malicious activity" is cut off approximately at half glyph-height, right at the placeholder's bottom edge; pixel inspection of the candidate render shows the second line's dark pixel count drops from ~70/row to 0 between row 112 and row 113 of 540, which is exactly the placeholder's computed bottom edge (`341313+731837` EMU / `5143500` EMU slide height × 540px ≈ row 112.6).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <!-- slideMaster1.xml, title placeholder -->
  <a:bodyPr vert="horz" wrap="square" lIns="91424" tIns="45712" rIns="91424" bIns="45712"
            numCol="1" anchor="ctr" anchorCtr="0" compatLnSpc="1">
    <a:prstTxWarp prst="textNoShape"><a:avLst/></a:prstTxWarp>
  </a:bodyPr>
  <!-- title xfrm (layout): off y="341313" ext cy="731837" (0.8in) -->
  <!-- titleStyle: sz="3200" lnSpc spcPct 80000 -->
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: there is no `a:normAutofit`/`a:spAutoFit` anywhere in the placeholder's inheritance chain, so per ECMA-376 the shape does not shrink text to fit — it is expected to overflow visibly rather than clip. Two 32pt lines at 80% line spacing plus insets need slightly more than the 0.8in box height, which is why LibreOffice shows a small, harmless overflow. BetterOffice instead clips text precisely at the shape's bounding rectangle, destroying half of the second line. The same bug was already reported on this deck's slide 2 (`reports/02.md`, finding 2) with an identical box and title style.

### 2. `a:grpFill` shapes render with no fill

- Where: the four rounded-rectangle list bars — `Rounded Rectangle 75` (id 53), `83` (id 56), `91` (id 59), `125` (id 62) — each the sole visible fill-bearing shape in its own group (`Group 74`/`82`/`90`/`124`). Region `[0.665, 0.283, 0.956, 0.84]`.
- Reference: each bar is solid orange (`F9771D`), matching the enclosing group's `p:grpSpPr` fill.
- Candidate: each bar has no fill at all — the light-gray page background shows straight through, leaving only a bare outline-less rectangle where the bar should be.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:grpSp><p:grpSpPr>...<a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="F9771D"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
    <p:sp><p:cNvPr id="53" name="Rounded Rectangle 75"/>
      <p:spPr><a:prstGeom prst="roundRect"/><a:grpFill/><a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln></p:spPr>
      ...
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:grpFill/>` means "use the containing group's fill," here an explicit orange `solidFill` on `p:grpSpPr`. The candidate leaves the shape unfilled instead of inheriting it. This is the single largest contributor to this slide's diff score — it accounts for the `r2c4`/`r3c4`/`r3c3`/`r4c4` hot cells, which line up exactly with the four bars' column/row span.

### 3. Number digits don't inherit the shape style's white font color

- Where: the small square number labels `Rectangle 76/84/92/126` (ids 55, 58, 61, 64), holding "1"-"4". Region `[0.665, 0.308, 0.701, 0.815]`.
- Reference: bold white digits, readable against the orange bar.
- Candidate: bold dark-gray/black digits. Because finding 2 also removes the orange fill, the digits are technically still visible (dark-on-light), but the wrong color — they should be white, matching the sibling body-copy runs one level over which *do* carry an explicit `<a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill>` and *do* render white correctly (they're just invisible for the separate reason of the missing orange fill in finding 2).
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:sp><p:cNvPr id="55" name="Rectangle 76"/>
    <p:style><a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef></p:style>
    <p:txBody><a:p><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1100" b="1" dirty="0"/><a:t>1</a:t></a:r></a:p></p:txBody>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: the run has no `rPr` fill override, so its color must come from the shape's style matrix `<p:style><a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef>`, i.e. theme `lt1` (white). The candidate falls back to a default dark text color instead of resolving this style reference, distinct from finding 2's group-fill bug.

### 4. Picture `a:srcRect` crops ignored on the composited screenshot

- Where: `Picture 8` (id 72, rId9, `image38.png`) inside the monitor bezel group, plus `Picture 10` (id 73, rId10, `image40.png`) and `Picture 114` (id 74, rId11, `image54.png`) nested in `Group 3` (id 4) layered on top of it. Region `[0.244, 0.309, 0.628, 0.695]`.
- Reference: a clean "Detect" dashboard screenshot — thin browser chrome/URL bar at top, app content below, nothing else.
- Candidate: the same content but visibly compressed, with an extra colorful timeline/heatmap strip appearing above the browser chrome and the OS X dock (with app icons) appearing at the bottom of the "screen" — both come from parts of the source bitmaps that the reference crops away.
- XML:
  ```xml
  <p:blipFill rotWithShape="1"><a:blip r:embed="rId9">...</a:blip>
    <a:srcRect t="251" b="19194"/><a:stretch/>
  </p:blipFill>
  <!-- and, in Group 3: -->
  <a:blip r:embed="rId10"/><a:srcRect l="189" t="183" b="90152"/>
  <a:blip r:embed="rId11"/><a:srcRect t="10784" b="5403"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: each of these three pictures defines `a:srcRect` to crop off a top and/or bottom strip of its source bitmap before stretching the remainder into the shape. The candidate appears to skip the crop step and stretch the entire uncropped source into the same frame, pulling extra unrelated content from outside the intended crop region into view.

### 5. Slide-number field shows its literal placeholder text

- Where: the master's slide-number placeholder (inherited on slide 12, no shape override in `slide12.xml`), bottom-right footer, region `[0.953, 0.931, 0.971, 0.944]`.
- Reference: computes and draws the actual slide number, "12".
- Candidate: draws the field's literal fallback text, "«#»", unresolved.
- XML: `master.xml`: `<a:fld id="{4ABDCABE-3F10-B64C-92F1-862014417034}" type="slidenum">...<a:t>«#»</a:t></a:fld>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: per ECMA-376, `<a:fld type="slidenum">`'s `<a:t>` is only a cached fallback; a conformant renderer substitutes the current slide's 1-based index. The candidate prints the fallback text verbatim instead. Same bug already reported on this deck's slides 2 and 3.
