## Reference vs candidate

This is the deck's title slide: a Cisco-branded logo over a sky photo background, a large title ("Cisco Cloud Access Security with Elastica"), a subtitle line, and two metadata placeholders (speaker name and date). The reference shows the title wrapped across two large lines that overflow above its layout box, a Cisco logo top-left, and dashed prompt boxes reading "Speaker Name" and "Date" for the two empty placeholders. The candidate shows the same title collapsed onto one noticeably smaller line, no logo, and no prompt text/borders for the empty placeholders — everything else (background photo, "TDM Presentation", "Protect your business with safe cloud app usage") matches.

## Findings

### 1. Title text shrunk and forced onto a single line

- Where: Title 3 (id 4), region `[0.047, 0.513, 0.959, 0.639]` (the layout's ctrTitle box).
- Reference: renders at the full 52pt specified size, wraps to two lines ("Cisco Cloud Access" / "Security with Elastica"), and overflows upward past the top of its (deliberately short, 644730 EMU tall) layout box since no autofit is defined.
- Candidate: renders as a single line at roughly 60% of the reference's measured glyph height (cap height ~30px vs ~50px in the reference render), fitting entirely inside the box.
- XML:
  ```
  <!-- slideLayout37.xml, ctrTitle -->
  <a:bodyPr anchor="b"/>
  <a:lstStyle><a:lvl1pPr ...><a:defRPr sz="5200" .../></a:lvl1pPr></a:lstStyle>
  ```
  Neither the slide's `<a:bodyPr/>` (empty), the layout's `<a:bodyPr anchor="b"/>`, nor the master's title placeholder `<a:bodyPr vert="horz" wrap="square" ... anchor="ctr" .../>` contains `<a:normAutofit>`, `<a:spAutoFit>`, or any scale/shrink hint.
- Why the candidate is wrong: with no autofit element anywhere in the inheritance chain, OOXML's default is "do not autofit" — text should be drawn at the specified 52pt and allowed to overflow its shape, exactly as the reference does. The candidate appears to be shrinking the title to force it to fit the box, which is unsupported by the XML and is the single largest contributor to this slide's diff (it dominates hot cells r2c2, r3c1, r3c2, r3c3).

### 2. Cisco logo missing

- Where: layout picture "Picture 8" / `logo_black.ai` (id 19), region `[0.047, 0.062, 0.150, 0.176]`, top-left corner.
- Reference: draws the Cisco wordmark logo.
- Candidate: nothing is drawn in that region; the sky background shows through unobstructed.
- XML: `slideLayout37.xml.rels` — `<Relationship Id="rId3" .../image" Target="../media/image3.emf"/>`; `bo-log.json` records `"01": {..., "skipped_images": 1}`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: the logo's source media is an EMF (`image3.emf`), a vector metafile format. The BetterOffice log's `skipped_images: 1` for this slide confirms the image was dropped rather than decoded, consistent with EMF not being a supported picture format yet.
