## Reference vs candidate

Slide 15 shows the title "Create and enforce policies across all users and accounts" over a light-grey content panel: four orange numbered pill boxes on the left listing policy-enforcement capabilities, and a MacBook mockup on the right showing a cropped browser screenshot of the "Protect" product UI. An off-canvas red production note ("Please replace with new breadcrumb boxes from slide 23") bleeds onto the left edge in both renders. LibreOffice draws all four orange pills solid with legible white text, the title cleanly above the content panel, a coherent laptop screenshot, and the correct slide number "15" in the footer. BetterOffice drops the orange fill from all four pills (leaving near-invisible white-on-grey text), lets the title's second line sag into the grey panel, garbles the laptop screenshot with bleed-through content and compression artifacts, and prints the unresolved slide-number field code.

## Findings

### 1. Title's second line sits lower than the reference, overlapping the content panel

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), region ≈ [0.03, 0.06, 0.62, 0.29].
- Reference: both title lines sit fully above the grey content panel with visible clearance.
- Candidate: line 1 lands at the same position as the reference, but line 2 ("and accounts") is pushed noticeably further down, overlapping the top of the grey panel.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:titleStyle><a:lvl1pPr ...>
    <a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc>
    ...
  ```
  (from `slideMaster1.xml`; the slide's own title shape has empty `<p:spPr/>`/`<a:bodyPr/>`, so it inherits this fully.)
- Why the candidate is wrong: the master's title style specifies 80% line spacing. With line 1 positioned identically in both renders, the growing gap before line 2 in the candidate is consistent with rendering at full (100%) line spacing instead of the inherited 80%.

### 2. Orange fill missing from all four numbered step boxes

- Where: Rounded Rectangle 71/74/77/80 (ids 43, 46, 49, 52), region ≈ [0.21, 0.284, 0.55, 0.839].
- Reference: each step is a solid orange (`FFBC30`) rounded-rectangle pill with white numbers and white body text.
- Candidate: all four pills render with no fill at all — the underlying grey panel shows through, and the white number/text runs (styled for contrast against orange) become nearly illegible.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:grpSpPr>...<a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="FFBC30"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
  <p:sp><!-- Rounded Rectangle 71 --><p:spPr>...<a:grpFill/>...</p:spPr>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:grpFill/>` tells the shape to inherit its fill from the enclosing group's `p:grpSpPr/a:solidFill`, which is explicitly `FFBC30` here (ECMA-376 §20.1.8.35). The candidate treats the shape as unfilled instead of resolving the inherited group fill.

### 3. Slide-number field prints its placeholder code instead of "15"

- Where: footer slide-number placeholder, region ≈ [0.955, 0.922, 0.979, 0.952].
- Reference: prints "15", the slide's correct 1-based index.
- Candidate: prints the literal field glyph "‹#›".
- XML: `slideMaster1.xml`: `<a:fld id="{4ABDCABE-3F10-B64C-92F1-862014417034}" type="slidenum">...<a:t>‹#›</a:t></a:fld>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: `type="slidenum"` fields must be evaluated to the current slide's number; `<a:t>` is only a cached fallback (ECMA-376). Printing it verbatim is a field-evaluation gap, not an XML content difference (same pattern seen on slides 2 and 3 of this deck).

### 4. Laptop-screen browser screenshot renders with crop/scale artifacts

- Where: Picture 10 (id 60) and Picture 9 (id 10), nested in Group 10 inside the laptop mockup, region ≈ [0.585, 0.354, 0.917, 0.707].
- Reference: shows a clean composite — a thin browser toolbar/URL-bar strip on top, and the "Protect" product page (header, tabs, policy table) below it, matching each picture's `srcRect` crop.
- Candidate: a band of unrelated content (small chart bars, dotted rows resembling the "Detect" anomaly-activity screenshot) is visible just under the toolbar, and the "Protect" table below renders compressed/overlapping, as if more vertical extent of each source image is being drawn than the crop specifies.
- XML:
  ```
  <p:pic><!-- Picture 10 --><a:srcRect l="189" t="183" r="181" b="90152"/>
  <p:pic><!-- Picture 9 --><a:srcRect t="2918" b="45652"/>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `b="90152"` on Picture 10 keeps only the top ~9.8% of `image40.png` (the toolbar strip); `t="2918" b="45652"` on Picture 9 keeps the middle ~51% of `image59.jpg`. The group's own transform maps 1:1 (`off`/`ext` equal `chOff`/`chExt`, no extra scaling), so the distortion traces back to these two pictures' crop percentages not being fully honored.

### 5. Off-slide production-note callout wraps differently

- Where: Rectangle 58 (id 59), region ≈ [0.0, 0.362, 0.123, 0.762] (box extends further left, off-slide).
- Reference: text breaks as "e replace / n new / mb boxes / slide 23" (visible fragments, box starts off-canvas).
- Candidate: text breaks as "eplace with / crumb boxes / slide 23" — fewer, longer visible lines.
- XML: `<a:bodyPr rtlCol="0" anchor="ctr"/>` with no explicit wrap override; run text `Please replace with new breadcrumb boxes from slide 23` in a 2087880 EMU-wide (≈0.228 slide-width) box.
- Why the candidate is wrong: with no explicit size/wrap override, both engines should measure the same font at the same size to decide line breaks; the differing break points point to a font-metrics or default-size mismatch for this run.
