## Reference vs candidate

The slide illustrates "shadow IT" with a cloud made of two overlapping white puff shapes holding app-logo pictures (Dropbox, Box, OneDrive, Google Drive) and gray "?" badges, a faint gradient triangle labeled "Shadow Data" fading out toward four document-type icons and labels (PCI/PHI/HR/Intellectual property), a Wi-Fi glyph, and a person silhouette in a light-blue circle bottom-left. The reference (LibreOffice) draws the cloud as an actual cloud outline, the triangle as a light, mostly-transparent gray-blue gradient wash, a thin Wi-Fi radio-wave icon, and a filled gray person silhouette. The candidate (BetterOffice) instead draws every one of these as flat rectangles: the cloud puffs become plain white boxes, the gradient triangle becomes a large opaque navy rectangle that blots out "Shadow Data" and all four document icons, the Wi-Fi glyph becomes a blocky camera-like icon, and the person silhouette becomes a solid gray rectangle. The app-logo pictures, title text, "?" badges (roundRect preset shapes), and footer render correctly.

## Findings

### 1. custGeom shapes drawn as their bounding-box rectangle

- Where: every `custGeom` shape on the slide — the two cloud-puff freeforms (id 60's sibling "Freeform 57" id 58 and "Freeform 3" id 4), the big background freeform "Freeform 59" (id 60), the four Wi-Fi arc freeforms (ids 24-27, region ~`[0.116,0.529,0.161,0.586]`), the person-silhouette freeform "Freeform 71" (id 72, region `[0.07,0.668,0.149,0.831]`), and the small glyph freeforms nested inside the four document-icon groups (Group 22/27/15/14, ids 39-50 and 55-57).
- Reference: each renders its actual outline — a fluffy double-cloud silhouette, thin curved Wi-Fi arcs, a person's head-and-shoulders silhouette, and small document/pen/chart glyphs.
- Candidate: each renders as a plain filled (or white) rectangle spanning the shape's `a:off`/`a:ext` bounding box — e.g. the cloud is two overlapping white boxes with square corners, the person silhouette is a solid gray rectangle, the Wi-Fi arcs stack into a blocky camera-shaped blob, and the four document-icon glyphs disappear entirely (their bounding boxes sit on top of the now-opaque navy triangle from finding 2, rendering the same near-black color and becoming invisible against it).
- XML:
  ```
  <p:sp><p:nvSpPr><p:cNvPr id="58" name="Freeform 57"/>...
  <a:xfrm><a:off x="2886272" y="1204529"/><a:ext cx="2646310" cy="1453013"/></a:xfrm>
  <a:custGeom><a:avLst/><a:gdLst>...23 connection guides tracing a cloud puff...</a:gdLst>
  <a:pathLst>...</a:pathLst></a:custGeom>
  <a:solidFill><a:schemeClr val="bg1"/></a:solidFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `custGeom` requires drawing the `a:pathLst` moveTo/lineTo/cubicBezTo/arcTo commands, not the shape's overall extent. Filling the raw `a:off`/`a:ext` rectangle discards the path entirely, which is confirmed pixel-for-pixel: the navy rectangle's top edge in the candidate render sits exactly at `y=0.292` (Freeform 59's `a:off y="1502457"` / slide height) as a straight horizontal line, where the reference shows the path's diagonal edge starting near the clouds and widening toward the bottom-right corner. This single bug is the dominant cause of this slide's 40% diff and drives all four hottest grid cells (r3c4 98.7%, r3c3 97.1%, r2c4 83.7%, r3c2 65.7%).

### 2. Gradient fill with alpha stops rendered fully opaque

- Where: "Freeform 59" (id 60), region `[0.188, 0.292, 1.022, 0.895]` — the large background shape behind "Shadow Data" and the four document labels.
- Reference: a `a:lin` gradient from `accent1` at 42% alpha down to `accent1` at 0% alpha (fully transparent), producing a light wash that fades to nothing, letting the white slide background and the black label text show through clearly.
- Candidate: solid, fully opaque `accent1` (a dark navy), covering the "Shadow Data" label, the four document labels, and their (already-missing, see finding 1) icons, and reducing all that text to low-contrast dark-gray-on-navy.
- XML:
  ```
  <a:gradFill><a:gsLst>
    <a:gs pos="0"><a:schemeClr val="accent1"><a:lumMod val="75000"/><a:alpha val="42000"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
    <a:gs pos="100000"><a:schemeClr val="accent1"><a:lumMod val="75000"/><a:alpha val="0"/></a:schemeClr></a:gs>
  </a:gsLst><a:lin ang="5400000" scaled="0"/></a:gradFill>
  ```
- Why the candidate is wrong: both gradient stops carry an explicit `a:alpha`, and the second stop is fully transparent (`0`). Rendering the shape at full opacity with no interpolation ignores both the gradient's spatial interpolation and its alpha channel, producing a much darker, non-fading fill than any single stop calls for once alpha is honored.

### 3. Slide-number field shows its literal placeholder text

- Where: the master's slide-number placeholder (inherited on slide 3, no shape override in `slide3.xml`), bottom-right footer, region roughly `[0.95, 0.93, 0.98, 0.95]`.
- Reference: computes and draws the actual slide number, "3".
- Candidate: draws the field's literal fallback text, "‹#›", unresolved.
- XML: `slideMaster1.xml`: `<a:fld id="{4ABDCABE-3F10-B64C-92F1-862014417034}" type="slidenum">...<a:t>‹#›</a:t></a:fld>`.
- Why the candidate is wrong: per ECMA-376, `<a:fld type="slidenum">`'s `<a:t>` is only a cached fallback; a conformant renderer substitutes the current slide's 1-based index. The candidate instead prints the fallback text verbatim.
