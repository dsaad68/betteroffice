## Reference vs candidate

Slide 18 is "Manage and control all apps from a single interface": a two-line title over a light-gray card, with four dark-gray rounded-rectangle list items (numbered 1-4) on the left and a MacBook mockup showing a product dashboard screenshot on the right; a red author annotation box about breadcrumbs sits mostly off the left edge of the slide. LibreOffice renders the four list bars solid dark gray with legible white numbers and text, a tight two-line title that fits cleanly above the card, a clean laptop screen with no stray desktop chrome, and readable white annotation text. BetterOffice renders the list bars with no fill at all (leaving the white text nearly invisible), the digits and the annotation text in a dark default color, the title's second line pushed down and clipped by the card panel underneath it, an OS X dock strip bleeding into the bottom of the laptop screen, and extra dashboard content visible that should have been cropped off.

## Findings

### 1. List-item bars lose their `grpFill`-inherited dark-gray background

- Where: Rounded Rectangle 17/20/27/30 (ids 51, 54, 57, 60), one per numbered item, region [0.197, 0.328, 0.491, 0.795].
- Reference: each bar is filled solid dark gray (`676767`), with white body text and a thin white vertical divider clearly legible on top.
- Candidate: the bars render with no fill (transparent, showing the light-gray card background through), so the white text and white divider — which do carry their own explicit white color and render correctly as color — become nearly invisible against the near-white backdrop.
- XML:
```xml
<!-- Group 9, id 10 -->
<p:grpSpPr><a:xfrm>...</a:xfrm><a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="676767"/></a:solidFill></p:grpSpPr>
<!-- Rounded Rectangle 17, id 51 -->
<p:spPr><a:xfrm>...</a:xfrm><a:prstGeom prst="roundRect"><a:avLst/></a:prstGeom><a:grpFill/><a:ln><a:noFill/></a:ln><a:effectLst/></p:spPr>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:grpFill/>` means "use whatever fill the parent group declares", here the group's own `676767` solid fill. All four groups (ids 10, 7, 5, 4) repeat the identical pattern. BetterOffice does not resolve `grpFill`, leaving the shape unfilled instead of dark gray — the single largest contributor to this slide's diff (hot cells r3c2 86.0%, r2c2 60.5%, both over this list column).

### 2. Text without an explicit run color renders dark instead of the style's white

- Where: Rectangle 19/26/29/35 (ids 53, 56, 59, 62), the digits "1"-"4"; and Rectangle 74 (id 75), the red "Please replace with new breadcrumb boxes from slide 23" annotation. Region [-0.105, 0.349, 0.491, 0.774].
- Reference: the digits render bold white against their (correctly dark) bars; the annotation text renders white against its red box.
- Candidate: the digits render in a dark gray/black; the annotation text renders in a dark teal/gray, both close to a generic default text color rather than white.
- XML:
```xml
<!-- digit "1", id 53 -->
<p:style><a:fillRef idx="1"><a:schemeClr val="accent1"/></a:fillRef><a:fontRef idx="minor"><a:schemeClr val="lt1"/></a:fontRef></p:style>
<p:txBody><a:p><a:pPr algn="ctr"/><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" sz="1100" b="1" dirty="0"/><a:t>1</a:t></a:r></a:p></p:txBody>
<!-- annotation box, id 75 -->
<a:solidFill><a:srgbClr val="C00000"/></a:solidFill>
<p:txBody><a:p><a:pPr algn="ctr"/><a:r><a:rPr lang="en-US" dirty="0"/><a:t>Please replace with new breadcrumb boxes from slide 23</a:t></a:r></a:p></p:txBody>
```
- Why the candidate is wrong: neither run carries its own `<a:solidFill>`, so each must resolve its color through the shape's style-matrix `fontRef` (`schemeClr lt1`, theme white), exactly as the reference does. This is the same root cause on two unrelated shapes — a style-matrix default-color fallback that BetterOffice does not apply.

### 3. Laptop screenshot pictures ignore their `srcRect` crops

- Where: Picture 8 (id 65, the OS X desktop/dock background image) and Picture 10 (id 71, the dashboard browser screenshot), region [0.489, 0.288, 0.981, 0.835].
- Reference: the laptop screen shows only the dashboard browser window against a plain blue desktop background; no dock or extra dashboard rows are visible.
- Candidate: an OS X dock strip (with mail and trash icons) is visible at the bottom-right of the laptop screen, and the dashboard screenshot shows an extra "Violations & Alerts" row at the bottom that the reference crops away. Because the full, uncropped source bitmaps are stretched into the same fixed-size frames, the effective zoom level is also slightly smaller than the reference throughout the screen area.
- XML: `Picture 8: <a:blip r:embed="rId8"/><a:srcRect t="251" b="16720"/><a:stretch/>`; `Picture 10: <a:blip r:embed="rId9"/><a:srcRect b="10267"/><a:stretch/>`
- Why the candidate is wrong: `a:srcRect` values are 1000ths-of-a-percent crop insets that must be applied to the source bitmap before stretching into the shape's frame. BetterOffice stretches the full, uncropped bitmap instead, revealing off-crop content (the dock, extra dashboard rows) that the deck author explicitly cropped out.

### 4. Title's second line overflows and is painted over by the card panel below it

- Where: Title 1 (id 2), region [0.034, 0.066, 0.960, 0.209].
- Reference: "Manage and control all apps from a single" / "interface" renders as two tightly-spaced lines, both fully inside the title placeholder and above the light-gray card panel.
- Candidate: the same two lines render with visibly looser spacing; the second line ("interface") sits lower, low enough that the light-gray card panel (drawn afterward, on top) covers its descenders and lower portions of the glyphs, making it read as clipped/washed out.
- XML: `master.xml` `p:titleStyle/a:lvl1pPr`: `<a:lnSpc><a:spcPct val="80000"/></a:lnSpc><a:defRPr sz="3200">`; the slide's `Title 1` has an empty `<p:spPr/>` and `<a:bodyPr/>` with no paragraph-level override, so this 80%-of-normal line spacing must come from the inherited master title style.
- Why the candidate is wrong: at 32pt with normal (100%) line spacing, two lines exceed the placeholder's 731837 EMU (~57.6pt) height and overflow into the panel shape below; at the specified 80% spacing the two lines fit inside the placeholder, matching the reference. This is also the largest single-cell contributor at the top of the slide (hot cell r1c1, 18.0%).

### 5. Slide-number field shows literal field markup instead of the computed number

- Where: the master's footer slide-number placeholder, bottom-right corner, region [0.94, 0.955, 0.98, 0.98].
- Reference: renders "18".
- Candidate: renders "‹#›" verbatim.
- XML: `master.xml: <a:fld type="slidenum">...<a:t>‹#›</a:t></a:fld>`
- Why the candidate is wrong: `<a:t>‹#›</a:t>` inside an `<a:fld type="slidenum">` is only the cached fallback text; a renderer with field support is expected to substitute the shape's actual position in the deck, as the reference does. Small, low-contrast, low-severity element, but a concrete, reproducible mismatch also seen elsewhere in this deck.

## Not reported

- Minor anti-aliasing/hinting differences on the "Manage and control all apps from a single" first title line and other body text, where glyph shapes, weight, and color all match on close inspection.
- Sub-pixel offsets (1-2px) around the laptop bezel edges and card panel corners, consistent with normal rasterization differences once the `grpFill`/`srcRect` bugs above are set aside.
- The red "Please replace with new breadcrumb boxes...slide 23" box and the rotated "Please use the new bre..." comment off the right edge are author leftovers present (in substance) in both renders, not a rendering bug on their own -- only their text color (finding 2) differs.
- The annotation box's text wraps into three lines in the candidate versus four in the reference (e.g. "with new" ends up on the same line as "replace" instead of its own line); this looks like a font-metric/character-width difference rather than a clear single-property bug, so it is noted here rather than filed separately.
